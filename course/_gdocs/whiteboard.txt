Whiteboard Style Video — Training Walkthrough
What You'll Need Before Starting
* A script
* Whiteboard skill — custom Claude skill (link in training resources below the video)
* ElevenLabs (or similar) for voiceover generation
* Image generation: 
   * ChatGPT / GPT-4 (recommended) OR Google Flow (nano banana)
   * Product Reference Image
* Video generation: 
   * Seedance 2.0 (recommended for best output quality) OR Google Gemini Omni (works, but lower quality — see comparison in video)
* CapCut for video editing
Workflow
* Generate the script
* Generate the voiceover in ElevenLabs
* Generate the initial reference image — this sets how the whiteboard looks (style, aesthetic, layout)
* Generate the start frames — individual scene images based on the reference
* Generate the videos from the start frames
* Assemble and edit in CapCut
  

Post-Edit Tips
* Speed ramp your videos to help with retention — how much depends on the pacing of the spoken line
* If the video feels too slow, go back and add another drawing/scene to fill the gap
* Don't stress about gaps until you reach the end — assess the full video first, then fill where needed
