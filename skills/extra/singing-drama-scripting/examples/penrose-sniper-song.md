## What to learn from it

- **The messenger can be an object.** A stolen box does the job of a sister or a church friend.
- **The villain's secret is the product.** The rival never wins on looks — only on the jar. Taking
  the jar takes his power. Same engine as the Cologne Billionaire script.
- **Price contrast inside the story:** "$400 a bottle and I still came second" sets up "$35 is all you need".
- **One absurd, cinematic twist** (the sniper shot) makes it memorable and shareable. It is a
  cartoon — keep it non-violent to people: the target is the jar.
