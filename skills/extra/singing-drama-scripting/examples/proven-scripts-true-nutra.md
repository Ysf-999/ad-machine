## The formula

**A song + a dramatic script + Pixar-style animation.**

- **A song:** every line is sung, start to finish. These run 4 to 14 minutes.

- **A dramatic script:** a first-person story of betrayal, rock bottom, rescue and revenge.

- **Pixar-style animation:** 3D cartoon characters who look like the buyer, with karaoke captions that highlight each word as it's sung.

## The 8 beats every script follows

1. **The shock line.** The first sung line is a betrayal, a humiliation or a tragedy. No product, no claim.

2. **The sacrifice.** Everything the hero gave before it went wrong, so the betrayal lands harder.

3. **Rock bottom.** The mirror, the parked car, the motel. Everything they tried, and the doctor who shrugs.

4. **The messenger.** A sister, brother or friend reveals the real cause: it's not fat, and it was never your fault.

5. **The ritual.** The product as one simple daily habit, sold by taste and feel.

6. **The glow-up.** Week by week, small visible wins.

7. **The revenge payoff.** The person who hurt them sees it and regrets it. A stranger asks: what are you on?

8. **The close.** The singer turns to the viewer, restates the cause and the ritual, then gives the offer.

## How to use these scripts

- Keep the 8 beats in order. Swap the betrayal (husband, wife, stepmom, aging parent) for one your buyer has lived.

- Swap the hidden villain (cortisol, parasites, lymphatic waste) for the real mechanism behind your product.

- Keep the specifics: exact ages, pounds, months and dollar amounts make a made-up story feel true.
