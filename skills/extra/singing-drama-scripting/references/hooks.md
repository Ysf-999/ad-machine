# Hooks

Each script gets **three hooks**: different doors into the same story. Each hook is 1–2 sung lines
and must lead naturally into Verse 1.

## The three doors (from the Penrose Cologne Billionaire script)

| Door | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **The secret** | Authority figure + a secret they hide + "they don't want you to know" | "My boss is an Arab cologne billionaire. But he never wears his own. So I slept with his wife, and she told me a secret the cologne industry don't want you to know." |
| **The confession** | The most scandalous act, said first, then the promise of proof | "I slept with an Arab cologne billionaire's wife, and she told me the secret of how they do it. The cologne industry's been lying to you, and I can prove it." |
| **The crime** | Humble role → transgression → theft, in one breath | "I cleaned an Arab cologne billionaire's pool and then I slept with his wife. She told me his family's secret, and I stole it that same night." |

## Patterns from the other proven hooks

- **Betrayal by the closest person:** "Best man at my wedding, he's in bed with my wife."
- **Humiliation with a number:** "He dumped me for gaining 10 pounds."
- **Tragedy with a place:** "They found my mama on the ground."
- **Taboo glimpse:** "I walked in on my stepmom sending nudes to my uncle on Christmas morning."
- **The small rejection:** "One night, I put on lingerie for my husband." (the rejection lands on line 4)
- **Digital proof of betrayal:** "My wife's Instagram kept showing photos from places I'd never taken her."
- **The timeline punch:** "One month in, and she pregnant." (Penrose Out of Milk — score 100)
- **Proven Penrose winner:** "My girl left me for my best friend." (SecretSong — $114K spend, $42 CPA
  vs a $57 account average; deck slide 4. Full script not on file.)

## Rules

- First person. A relationship word. One concrete detail (a number, a place, a day).
- Under ~15 words per line. Sung, so it must scan — read it aloud with a beat.
- No product, no claim, no question.
- Three hooks = three different doors, not three rewordings of one line.
- The hook's last line must hand straight into Verse 1 without re-explaining.
