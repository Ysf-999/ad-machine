### Do's

- Deliver the "I tried six probiotics" opener with frustration, not whining.
- Read the RCT line slower than the rest — it is the credibility beat.
- Wear the jeans visibly throughout. The wardrobe is the demonstration.
- Keep the mechanism explanation in plain language; no jargon stack.

### Don'ts

- Don't add "doctor recommended" — that claim is not documented for this product.
- Don't promise a specific bloating reduction percent for the viewer; cite only the trial figure.
- Don't soften the failed-alternatives list. Naming brands is the contrast that works.
- Don't hold the bottle up for more than 2s during the hook — it kills the rant feel.

## Strategy Notes

**Awareness Stage:** problem-aware — buyer recognizes bloating but does not know strain specificity is the differentiator. 60s is right because the mechanism reveal needs room to breathe without losing rant energy.
**Market Sophistication:** Stage 4 — buyer has tried multiple probiotics. The script opens with a failed-alternative confession to bypass dismissal, not another "feel better" promise.
**Buyer Belief Shift:** "All probiotics are basically the same" -> "Strain specificity matters more than CFU count, and one strain has trial data for my exact symptom."
**Market Signal:** VOC pulled from r/IBS thread and brand reviews — recurring "I tried Align and got nothing" pattern.
**Reference Control Pattern:** problem-solution-rant for rhythm + opening, podcast-myth-bust for the mechanism authority overlay. Adapted, not copied.
**Schwartz Lever:** sophistication stage 4 — failed-alternative + new mechanism, not a louder claim.
**Halbert Lever:** starving crowd — bloating sufferers actively shopping. AIDA opening with discovery-confession grabber.
**Kennedy Lever:** reason-why anchored on the strain identity, plus risk reversal via the 60-day guarantee.
**Cialdini Lever:** social proof via review screenshots, authority via RCT citation, contrast via competitor brand naming.
**Wundt Inhibition:** removes the "I already tried probiotics" objection by reframing the category — this is a single-strain RCT-backed format, not another generic blend.
**Copy-Chief Pass:** Replaced "works fast" with "67% in 14 days, I felt it on day 9." Moved "consult your doctor" to Launch Notes. Tightened CTA from "check it out" to "Click Shop Now while it's still in stock."
