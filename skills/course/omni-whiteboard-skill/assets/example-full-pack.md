## What this demo is showing

1. **No enumeration.** Clip 02 says "keep everything already on the board unchanged" — it never lists the heading, the nine cards, the check marks. Naming them invites redrawing them.
2. **Board load stays at three.** Heading, grid, checks. The X's modify what's there instead of adding a fourth idea.
3. **Beats are literal.** "Two straight diagonal strokes." No adjectives about confidence, deliberation, or intent.
4. **The locks repeat verbatim** — and they're one line each, so repeating them costs almost nothing.

If a third clip needed a new idea rather than a modification, it would open with the eraser: *"[0-2.0s] Wipes the board clean with an eraser and sets it down out of frame."*
