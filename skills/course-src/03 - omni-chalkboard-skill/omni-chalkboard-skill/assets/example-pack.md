# Worked Example — "Nine Cards" (2 clips)

Format demo. Note how short the action description is — one paragraph, not a beat list.

## THE LOCKS

- **Mode:** congruent · **Aspect:** 9:16
- **Hand:** same right hand, dark sleeve to the elbow, no watch or rings
- **Chalk:** white for everything, red only for problems
- **Narrator:** one calm man, normal American accent, medium-low voice, friendly and unhurried

## Clip 01

**Upload:** nothing.

```text
A hand draws on a dark green chalkboard. The camera is above the board looking straight down and never moves. The board fills the whole picture — no frame, no chalk tray, no desk, no room.

The same right hand every time, dark sleeve pushed up to the elbow, no watch, no rings. Words are written in big rough capital letters, never joined-up. White chalk for everything, red chalk only for problems. The chalk lines are rough and grainy and a little dust puffs off when the hand presses hard. The hand draws fast and does not stop until the clip ends, then leaves the picture.

What happens: The board starts empty. The hand writes "ROOFERS" in big letters across the top and underlines it twice. Below it draws nine boxes with rounded corners, in three rows of three, working left to right. Then it goes back and draws a tick inside every box until all nine are ticked.

Only these words are written: "ROOFERS".

No second hand. No writing that appears on its own. No captions or subtitles or watermarks. No words other than the ones above. No camera movement.

Sound: chalk scraping, a squeak on the underlines, under the voice. No music.
Voice, off camera, nobody visible speaking: one calm man, normal American accent, medium-low voice, friendly and unhurried. He says exactly: "Nine jobs, nine happy customers. This is what a stranger sees when they look you up. A wall of little cards, nothing else."

Filmed on an iPhone 11 on a stand above the board.
```

## What this shows

1. **The action is one paragraph**, not a list. Four sentences covers the whole clip.
2. **Numbers stay exact.** Nine boxes, three rows, two X's, seven ticked.
3. **Nothing is listed back.** Clip 02 says everything already drawn stays where it is — it doesn't name the word, the underlines, the boxes or the ticks.
4. **Colour changes are a clause**, not a step: *"The hand swaps to red chalk and draws…"*
5. **The locks stay long.** Those suppress drift. Only the action description is short.
