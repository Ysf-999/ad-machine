# Marketing Brain Cheatsheet

Condensed operating ammo from the 6 master references. The model already knows the textbook definitions of Schwartz, Halbert, Kennedy, Cialdini, and Wundt. This file is the project-specific operating layer on top of that knowledge.

**Default loading:** Load this cheatsheet for all standard work. Load full references only when explicitly told to deepen a specific framework, when building a brand-new campaign architecture from scratch, or when the cheatsheet line says "load full reference."

## How To Use This File

- Read top-to-bottom on first task in a session, then reference by section.
- When a skill asks for "Schwartz lever," "Halbert lever," etc., pull from these blocks.
- When a decision is unclear, use the **Quick Master Rules** table at the bottom.
- Never invent VOC, proof, credentials, prices, or outcomes regardless of which framework you're applying.

## 1. Avatar Research (Decision-First)

**Core:** Research outputs an advertising decision, not a customer profile.

**Operating levers:**
- **Pain ladder** (4 levels): Surface pain -> practical cost -> emotional/social cost -> identity/status fear. Cold ads start at levels 3-4.
- **Trigger event hunt:** the specific moment that turned a passive sufferer into an active searcher (deadline, photo, diagnosis, bill, failed attempt).
- **Behavior beats opinion:** what they bought/refunded/quit > what they said in surveys.
- **Evidence buckets:** attitudinal (feel/fear), behavioral (did/tried), classification (who/situation). Segment proof by bucket.
- **Frequency x Intensity x Buyer Intent scoring:** rare nuclear phrase can beat common bland complaint.
- **Proof availability check:** can the product credibly support the promise? If not, drop the angle before extracting it.

**Project-specific rules:**
- Quote -> Evidence -> Implication. Not "people want lower bills" but "[exact phrase from review] -> implication: lead cold ads with daily irritation before mechanism."
- Never segment unless segmentation changes the ad.
- "Research to decide, not to know." Every insight must answer: what do we test next?

**When to load full reference:** building avatar from scratch, mining competitor reviews for new positioning, or running first-time category research.

## 2. Schwartz / Breakthrough Advertising

**Core:** Copy doesn't create desire. It redirects mass desire already in motion onto your product.

**Awareness Stage -> Headline Type:**
- **Unaware:** identification headline naming the emotion or attitude. No product mention.
- **Problem-aware:** name the problem + the kind of solution.
- **Solution-aware:** name the desire + that the product can accomplish it.
- **Product-aware:** product name + new superiority (proof, mechanism, repositioning).
- **Most-aware:** product + price/deal/offer only. Pure retargeting copy.

**Sophistication Stage -> What's Fresh:**
- **Stage 1:** direct claim. You're first to name the solution.
- **Stage 2:** escalate the claim. Bigger promises still land.
- **Stage 3:** lead with mechanism. Claims exhausted, HOW is fresher than WHAT.
- **Stage 4:** elaborate the mechanism. Make yours more specific or dramatic than copycats.
- **Stage 5:** identification. Claims AND mechanisms exhausted; shift to WHO buys (tribe, lifestyle, identity).

**7 Body-Copy Mechanisms:** intensification, identification, gradualization (yes-train), redefinition (reframe objections), mechanization (name/feature the mechanism), concentration (attack competitor weakness while showing your fix), camouflage (editorial format, not ad format).

**3 Mind Dimensions** all must operate: desires (sensory), identifications (roles/status), beliefs (existing opinions). Working with beliefs as bridge wins; violating beliefs kills the ad.

**Project-specific rules:**
- One fully-believed promise > 10 partially-believed promises. Fix belief structure before adding more claims.
- Your most powerful claim is not always your best headline. Lead with a fully-believable smaller promise that pulls into the body.
- Prevention headlines almost never work, except for loved ones (e.g., decay-prevention toothpaste pitched to parents).
- Guarantee = final intensification, not a safety net.

**When to load full reference:** writing headlines from scratch, building VSL belief architecture, or constructing email sequence aligned to awareness stages.

## 3. Halbert / Boron Letters

**Core:** Starving crowd beats better product. Find the market first, then the product.

**List/Audience Hierarchy (best -> worst):**
1. Own customer list
2. Repeat buyers + high unit + recent (RFM all hot)
3. Repeat buyers + high unit
4. Repeat category buyers
5. High-income + similar product history
6. High-income + mail-order proven
7. Mail-order buyers (general)
8. Phone book / cold demographic only

**Three RFM metrics in order of importance:** Recency > Frequency > Unit of Sale.

**A-Pile / B-Pile (digital translation):**
- A-Pile = personal-from-name, no pitch in subject line, looks like one-to-one. Opens.
- B-Pile = brand from-name, teaser subject, obvious commercial framing. Tossed.
- 40% of email/DM power lives in the envelope/subject layer, not the body.

**AIDA must flow through every asset:**
- Attention: hook, grabber, counterintuitive claim.
- Interest: specific facts, statistics, story.
- Desire: vivid benefits, lived-in moments.
- Action: close hard. Allocate ~25% of copy to the close. Most writers allocate ~10%.

**Closing checklist (use as audit):** what they receive, restated benefit, step-by-step instructions, what to do, who to make payment to, deadline/scarcity, what they gain by acting now, what they lose by waiting, strong P.S.

**Customization matrix (specificity ladder):**
- Single: occupation OR pain OR location.
- Double: occupation + pain, or income + location, or occupation + life event.
- Triple: rarely usable but devastating when matched (occupation + location + life event).

**Project-specific rules:**
- Specificity = believability. "77.6%" beats "most." Exact dates/places/times make invisible reality.
- Eye relief: short paragraphs, wide margins, editorial typeface. Reads as content, not ad. Up to 5x more readership.
- "You don't have to get it right, you have to get it moving." Idea arrives during writing.
- HALT rule: never decide when Hungry/Angry/Lonely/Tired. State communicates.

**When to load full reference:** writing cold DM/email sequences, building offers around new lists, or auditing why a sequence isn't converting.

## 4. Kennedy DR Tactics

**Core:** You are in the marketing business, not the product business. Operate from Marketing HQ.

**Marketing Triangle (all three vertices required):** Message x Market x Media. Never one message to all segments. Never untargeted media.

**6 Differentiation Triangles:**
1. Apart-Above-Alone (positioning).
2. Problem-Agitate-Solve (copy structure).
3. Star-Story-Solution (narrative).
4. Awareness-Decision-Action (funnel; most brands skip Decision).
5. Good-Better-Best (Yes/Yes choice, not Yes/No).
6. Skepticism Counter (authority + proof preponderance + risk reversal).

**Lead Gen Ad Structure (5):** identity callout, dog-whistle language, unabashed specificity, only necessary info, one response path.

**Romancing the Stone (10 elements for "boring" products):** expert/celebrity endorsement, vital interest hook, difficulty invested, proprietary feature/trademarked process, why-it-works mechanism, leadership claim, what-it-isn't, story-driven testimonials, featured guarantee, long copy until close.

**11-Part CTA Audit:** instructions, "yes" sentences, summary, A/B/C options, deadline + reason, immediate-action bonus, upsell, payment instructions, guarantee restatement, response means, P.S.

**Project-specific rules:**
- Never tell the prospect to "go online to learn more." That hands them to Google/Amazon and the sale ends.
- Bundle > cafeteria. Bundling raises AOV, defies comparison, enables differentiation.
- Info-First system: publish authority before selling. The prospect comes to you instead of the commodity portal.
- The terrible-envelope trick works only if (a) the list is exact or (b) backend economics support acquiring at cost. Most DTC misses this.

**When to load full reference:** building a funnel from scratch (use Awareness-Decision-Action), launching a lead-gen ad, or repositioning a commoditized product (use Apart-Above-Alone).

## 5. Cialdini / Influence

**Core:** Humans run on automatic behavioral tapes triggered by specific cues. The job is to activate the tape, not convince rationally.

**6 Weapons (every offer should stack):**
1. Reciprocity - give before asking; uninvited gift hits hardest.
2. Commitment / Consistency - micro-commit -> identity update -> macro-compliance.
3. Social Proof - peers > celebrities. Most powerful at the decision point, not the headline.
4. Liking - similarity > celebrity. Shared enemy > shared product.
5. Authority - symbols (lab coat, title, association) work as well as substance.
6. Scarcity - newly scarce > always scarce. Visible competition amplifies.

**Hidden 7th: Contrast Principle.** Anchor high first. Present expensive option before the actual price. First frame sets reference.

**Social Proof Operating Conditions:**
- Most powerful under uncertainty (near CTA, not headline).
- Similarity-matched (peer testimonial > celebrity testimonial).
- Hierarchy: peer testimonials with results/numbers > volume claims > expert endorsements > star ratings > media logos > celebrity names.

**Rejection-Then-Retreat:** ask big -> refused -> retreat to actual ask. Triggers reciprocal concession. Closes ~85% vs. 50%.

**Project-specific rules:**
- Appearance of trigger = power of trigger. Lab coat, "Dr.", professional mic on a UGC creator changes conversion the same as the credential itself.
- "Was abundant, now scarce" beats "always scarce." Reduce-from-X-to-Y framing wins.
- Consistency self-reinforces. Strip the original incentive and compliance can rise (Iowa energy study).
- Reciprocity works even when both sides know it's manipulation.

**When to load full reference:** designing email sequences (open with reciprocity), building VSL pricing flow (contrast principle), auditing whether all 6 weapons appear in offer architecture.

## 6. Wundt / DR Psychology

**Core:** Buying is a physiological event. Excitation must overcome inhibition at threshold.

**Excitation - Inhibition equation:**
- Conversion = Excitatory amplitude minus Inhibitory amplitude.
- Copy job: raise excitation (desire, urgency, specificity, proof) AND crush inhibition (objections, price resistance, skepticism).
- Most stalled funnels fail because inhibition is unmet, not because excitation is low. Diagnose before adding more excitement.

**Stimulus Summation:**
- Single touch rarely converts. Cold buyers need 3-7 impressions before threshold.
- Email/retargeting sequences are summation engines.
- Gaps over 14 days reset the residue.

**3 Stages of Stimulation Response:**
1. Inexcitability (latent period) - first touch processes silently.
2. Increasing excitability - retargeting window. Peak conversion probability.
3. Decreasing excitability - creative fatigue. Refresh creative, not audience.

**Latent period rule:** stronger stimuli = shorter latency. Pain hooks convert faster than aspirational hooks.

**3 Feeling Dimensions (run simultaneously):** pleasantness/unpleasantness, excitement/depression, tension/relaxation. Conversion sweet spot = unpleasantness (pain) + excitement (possibility) + tension (urgency).

**Apperception (attention triggers):** intensity, novelty, contrast, emotional valence, personal relevance, continuity (interrupted patterns demand resolution).

**Reflex / Voluntary spectrum:**
- Reflex (impulse): remove friction, streamline to cart.
- Automatic (habitual): subscription, repeat trigger, familiarity.
- Voluntary (deliberated): full VSL, proof cascade, authority stacking, objection destruction.

**Manifold representation (3 levels - hit all to scale):**
1. Visceral (raw desire): pain, hunger, survival. Trigger with sensory language and before/after.
2. Emotional (identity, status, belonging): trigger with social proof, transformation, tribe language.
3. Rational (logic, justification): trigger with data, mechanism, study, value math.

**Inhibition handling:** state objection -> acknowledge briefly -> introduce stronger counter-excitation -> prove counter-excitation dominates.

**Project-specific rules:**
- Feelings precede rational processing. Rational copy is the justification layer after the emotional decision.
- The buyer is already wanting. The job is to tip stored potential energy into action, not invent desire.
- Every lost prospect is recoverable through a different path (vicarious function). Build multiple entry points.
- The first stimulus almost never converts. Single-touch attribution is neurologically wrong.

**When to load full reference:** building retargeting ladders (summation + latent period), constructing VSL architecture (3-level manifold), or diagnosing a stalled funnel.

## Quick Master Rules

| Decision | Lever | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Headline type | Schwartz Awareness | Stage decides type. Unaware = identification, never product. |
| Sequence shape | Halbert AIDA + Schwartz | A-I-D-A; awareness sets the entry point. |
| Pain depth | Avatar pain ladder | Cold ads at levels 3-4 (emotional/identity). |
| Offer architecture | Kennedy + Cialdini | Triangle (M/M/M) + 6 weapons stacked. |
| Believability | Schwartz gradualization | Yes-train of small acceptances before the claim. |
| Close intensity | Halbert 25% rule | 25% of asset is the close. |
| Frequency cadence | Wundt summation | 3-7 cold touches; gaps over 14 days reset. |
| Conversion stuck | Wundt excitation/inhibition | Diagnose: more desire OR less doubt? Usually doubt. |
| Social proof location | Cialdini + Kennedy | At the decision point, not the headline. |
| Price frame | Kennedy + Cialdini | Anchor high, minimize ("less than coffee"), bundle to defy comparison. |
| Sophistication match | Schwartz Stage 3-5 | If category is exhausted, lead with mechanism (3-4) or identification (5). |
| Mechanism missing | Schwartz mechanization | Name it. If competitors copied, elaborate it more dramatically. |

## Lever Naming Convention (for Strategy Notes)

When an output skill asks for framework levers, use these short names:

- **Schwartz Lever:** awareness stage / sophistication stage / mass desire / mechanism / belief chain / gradualization / identification / camouflage.
- **Halbert Lever:** starving crowd / list match / A-pile framing / AIDA / grabber / specificity / 25% close.
- **Kennedy Lever:** marketing triangle / differentiation triangle / lead gen / romancing / 11-part CTA / bundle.
- **Cialdini Lever:** reciprocity / commitment / social proof / liking / authority / scarcity / contrast / rejection-then-retreat.
- **Wundt Lever:** excitation / inhibition removal / summation / latent period / 3-feeling dimension / apperception / manifold level.

Strategy Notes should use **specific** lever choices (e.g., "Cialdini Lever: similarity-matched social proof at CTA") not generic labels ("Cialdini Lever: social proof").
