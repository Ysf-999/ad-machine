# Offer Diagnosis

## Purpose

Diagnose whether the offer is strong enough to scale paid traffic. Treat ads as amplification, not rescue. If the product promise, proof, mechanism, or risk reversal is weak, say so directly.

## Read When Needed

- `../../references/breakthrough-advertising.md` for desire, awareness, sophistication, mechanism.
- `../../references/kennedy-dr-tactics.md` for CTA, bundling, differentiation, guarantee, proof, pricing.
- `../../references/boron-letters-halbert-dtc-framework.md` for starving-crowd validation, market-first offer thinking, list/audience quality, and order logic.
- `../../references/influence-dtc-dr-breakdown.md` for contrast, social proof, authority, scarcity, commitment, and reciprocity levers.

## Diagnosis Checklist

Score each 1-5 and explain the bottleneck:
- mass desire: urgency, staying power, scope
- functional product: what the buyer really buys
- awareness fit: most aware, product aware, solution aware, problem aware, unaware
- sophistication stage: direct claim, stronger claim, mechanism, mechanism elaboration, identification
- unique mechanism: why this works differently
- proof stack: testimonials, demos, numbers, authority, logic
- inhibition: risk, skepticism, embarrassment, price, effort, identity conflict
- offer economics: price, bundle, AOV, margin, repeat potential
- CTA clarity: one action, immediate reason, guarantee, included value

## Output Format

Return:
1. Scale thesis: why this can or cannot scale.
2. Main desire to channel.
3. Functional product statement.
4. Biggest conversion blocker.
5. Mechanism and proof gaps.
6. Offer upgrades ranked by expected impact.
7. Claims to avoid unless the user supplied proof.

If the user passes `--extreme`, add:
8. Extreme DR opportunities: sharper contrast, enemy framing, urgency, risk reversal, price anchoring, and status-threat hooks.
9. Launch-owner notes: the boldest offer claims or implications the user should personally approve before launch.

## Operator Rule

Do not call a weak offer "good." A senior DR marketer protects spend by finding the constraint before creative testing burns budget.
