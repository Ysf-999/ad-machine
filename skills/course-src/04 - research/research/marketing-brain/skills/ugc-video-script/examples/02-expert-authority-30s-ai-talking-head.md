### Do's

- Render the avatar with a measured, calm cadence — not infomercial energy.
- Hold the conversion diagram on screen long enough to read it (≥3s).
- Show the actual product label, not a stylized render, during the mechanism beat.
- Keep the lab coat clean — no branded patches, no fake credentials lower-third.

### Don'ts

- Don't add "MD" or any clinical credential to the avatar persona; the persona is a "dermatologist look," not an attribution.
- Don't replace "fine line reduction" with stronger language; the trial figure does not support a wrinkle-cure claim.
- Don't speed up the proof beat. The 84 vs 31 contrast is the credibility moment.
- Don't add a peeling-related disclaimer mid-script; the objection beat already handles it.

## Strategy Notes

**Awareness Stage:** solution-aware — buyer already uses retinol and believes in the category. Length (30s) fits because the differentiation is one ingredient form, not a full education arc.
**Market Sophistication:** Stage 5 — the market has heard every "stronger retinol" claim. We bypass with a form-of-active argument that reframes the entire category as miscalibrated.
**Buyer Belief Shift:** "I just need a stronger retinol" -> "I need retinaldehyde — one conversion away, ten times faster in trials."
**Market Signal:** Recurring review pattern across competitor brands: "used for 6 months, no real change" — pulled from VOC sweep of 4 leading retinol creams.
**Reference Control Pattern:** authority-expert for the credibility frame, comparison-contrast for the proof-beat overlay. Adapted, not copied.
**Schwartz Lever:** sophistication stage 5 — reframe the category, do not pile on a louder claim.
**Halbert Lever:** A-pile credibility — expert frame plus specific trial figures earn the read in the first 5 seconds.
**Kennedy Lever:** differentiation triangle — same outcome, different mechanism (form of active), backed by side-by-side trial figures.
**Cialdini Lever:** authority via the dermatologist persona, contrast via the 84 vs 31 overlay.
**Wundt Inhibition:** removes the "I already tried retinol" objection by reframing what the buyer was actually using.
**Copy-Chief Pass:** Replaced "more effective" with "ten times faster than retinyl palmitate in clinical trials." Moved any irritation disclaimer out of the hook and into Launch Notes. Tightened CTA from "learn more" to "Click Shop Now."
