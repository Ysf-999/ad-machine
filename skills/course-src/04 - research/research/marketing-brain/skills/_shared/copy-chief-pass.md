# Shared DR Copy-Chief Pass

Run this pass before validation on every creative output (FB ads, LFS, UGC scripts, AI talking-head scripts, native ads). The pass is a final sharpening step. Performance lives in this layer.

## Universal Pass

- If the hook would not stop a buyer in-feed, rewrite it.
- If the pain could apply to any product in any category, make it product/category-specific.
- If the mechanism sounds like a feature list, rebuild as a reason-why with a number, ingredient, or named source.
- If the proof is generic ("trusted by thousands"), add a specific number, source, or testimonial fragment.
- If the CTA sounds optional ("check it out"), make the next action concrete.
- If "may," "might," "could," "designed to," or hedging fills the copy, tighten it.
- If constraints softened the whole asset, move risk language into `Launch Notes` and keep the selling copy sharp.
- Constraints prevent fake claims. They do not prevent aggressive contrast, market hatred, urgency, social comparison, enemy framing, or cost-of-inaction.

## Format-Specific Passes

**FB Ad Copy:**
- First 125 characters must stand alone before "See more."
- Visible hook marked with `||...||`.
- Each variation uses a different hook archetype.
- Each variation deploys at least 2 proof modes and handles at least 2 objections.

**Long-Form Static:**
- Hierarchy is visible (heading, body, callout, proof, CTA).
- Belief shift is one belief, not five.
- Mechanism is named, not implied.
- Image direction is concrete, not "lifestyle shot."

**UGC Video Script:**
- Hook works muted (visual — action, expression, product, demo, or B-roll — carries the hook, not captions).
- `creator_brief` mode = talking points, never word-for-word.
- `full_script` and `ai_talking_head` modes = word-for-word in natural human speech (contractions, filler, real rhythm). Not TTS-safe choppy sentences.
- Mechanism is visible on screen (animation, label, screen recording, demo).
- Spoken word total within target window for the length, never above hard cap.
- Every line traceable to control library, VOC, or product packet via `[source: ...]` tag; originals flagged in Launch Notes.
- One CTA, one button, one action.

**Native Image Prompt:**
- iPhone realism, no fake overlays.
- Background fully focused.
- No invented credentials in image text.

## Default Intensity By Funnel Stage

- TOF: 7/10 aggression. High curiosity, concrete pain moment, clear villain (industry / competitor / failed alternative).
- MOF: 8/10 aggression. Failed-alternative contrast and mechanism differentiation are the centerpiece.
- BOF: 8/10 aggression. Offer, proof, urgency, risk reversal stacked.

## Pass Output

After running the pass, the Strategy Notes section's `Copy-Chief Pass` field must name what was sharpened. Examples:

- "Replaced 'works fast' with 'Day-1 absorption + Day-3 visible reduction.'"
- "Moved 'consult your doctor' from hook to Launch Notes."
- "Renamed enemy from 'old skincare' to 'anti-aging products with weak peptide concentration.'"
- "Tightened CTA from 'Learn more about it' to 'Click Shop Now while orange button is visible.'"
