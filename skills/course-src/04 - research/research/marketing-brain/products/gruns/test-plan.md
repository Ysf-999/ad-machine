# Test Plan

Research date: 2026-05-03

## Objective

Find the strongest TOF belief bridge for Gruns beyond sale-price UGC while preserving the sale/offer as a closer.

## Primary Hypothesis

Broad cold traffic will respond better to Gruns when the first frame names the buyer's failed routine or skepticism, rather than the sale price alone.

Belief shift to test:

Current belief: "I need more discipline or another supplement."
New belief: "The routine format is the reason I might actually stick with this one."

## Test Batch 1: Powder Quitter

Variable tested: Failed alternative contrast.

Creative concepts:

1. Powder Graveyard POV
   - First frame: half-used tub/shaker on counter.
   - Hook: "If your greens powder is still sitting in the cabinet..."
   - Mechanism: no mix, daily pack, easier repeat.

2. Old Routine vs New Routine Static
   - Left: powder, pills, blender.
   - Right: Gruns daily pack.
   - Hook: "The best greens routine is the one you do."

3. Creator Confession
   - Hook: "I kept buying greens powders and pretending this time would be different."
   - Mechanism: lower-friction habit.

Success signal:

- Better thumbstop and CTR than sale-only UGC with comparable landing CVR.

## Test Batch 2: Skeptic's Gummy Audit

Variable tested: Proof-first skepticism handling.

Creative concepts:

1. "Just Candy?" Checklist
   - Hook: "Before you call these candy, check the label."
   - Proof: ingredient/testing/science/retail/guarantee screenshots if approved.

2. Dialogue Skeptic
   - Speaker 1: "A superfood gummy? Be serious."
   - Speaker 2: Walks through why format and proof matter.

3. Screen Recording
   - Creator opens official science/product pages.
   - Shows what they checked before buying.

Success signal:

- Higher click quality and lower landing bounce than broad benefit creative.

## Test Batch 3: Minimum Viable Health

Variable tested: Identification and self-blame relief.

Creative concepts:

1. "Busy Adult Diet" Native Static
   - Hook: "For the days your diet is more busy adult than wellness influencer."
   - Mechanism: daily backup pack.

2. "Actually Take It" UGC
   - Hook: "I did not need another health routine. I needed one with less friction."
   - Proof: daily pack, taste, reviews.

3. "No Wellness Homework" Notes Ad
   - Hook: "How I cover more basics without adding another morning task."

Success signal:

- Increased cold CTR without depressing purchase intent.

## Test Batch 4: GLP-1 Nutrition Support

Variable tested: High-intent life-stage context.

Creative concepts:

1. Eating Less, Not Needing Less Support
   - Hook: "When your appetite changes, your nutrition routine has to get easier, not harder."

2. Small Meals, Simple Pack
   - Hook: "The GLP-1 routine nobody thinks about until meals get smaller."

3. Fiber/Nutrition Checklist
   - Educational static with approved claims only.

Success signal:

- Strong CTR/CVR in separate campaign or ad set without compliance flags.

Launch note:

- Requires legal/compliance review before spend.

## Test Batch 5: Sale Price With Belief Bridge

Variable tested: Whether current winner improves when price is moved after mechanism.

Creative concepts:

1. Hook routine pain first, mention first-order sale after first proof beat.
2. Hook gummy skepticism first, mention sale as risk reversal.
3. Hook powder quitter first, mention sale near CTA.

Success signal:

- Maintains ROAS while broadening cold reach and reducing dependence on first-frame discount.

## Measurement Plan

Track by creative:

- Thumbstop / 3-second hold.
- Hook rate and hold rate for video.
- CTR.
- CPC.
- Landing page view rate.
- Add-to-cart / checkout initiation.
- Purchase CVR.
- CPA.
- ROAS.
- AOV and subscription take rate if available.
- Comment sentiment by objection category.

Interpretation:

- High CTR, low CVR: hook is interesting but proof/offer/landing page did not carry belief.
- Low CTR, high CVR: angle may be BOF or too narrow but qualified.
- Strong comments, weak clicks: identity/controversy may be too entertaining.
- Strong sale creative, weak non-sale: cold belief bridge or landing bridge is missing.
- Strong skeptic creative: build more proof-led statics and retargeting.
- Strong powder creative: scale into old-way/new-way UGC, LFS, and advertorial.

## Landing Page Needs

Minimum before launch:

- Active offer price and renewal terms.
- Guarantee and subscription clarity.
- Ingredient/testing proof module.
- "Why gummies / why eight" module.
- Powder alternative module.
- Reviews from powder quitters or routine adopters.
- Retail proof module if approved.

## Open Questions Before Creative Production

- Which current UGC ads are winning and at what spend?
- Does price appear in first 3 seconds or near CTA?
- What landing page is winning?
- What is first-order price, renewal price, and AOV?
- Can affiliates use clinical, retail, customer-count, and guarantee claims?
- Can we use Target/Ulta names/logos in paid ads?
- Is GLP-1 support a separate SKU/funnel or a messaging lane for core Gruns?
