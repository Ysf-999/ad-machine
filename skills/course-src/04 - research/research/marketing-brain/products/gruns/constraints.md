# Constraints

Research date: 2026-05-03

## Claims We Can Make With Source Verification

Use only if the active page or approved claim sheet supports the wording:

- Daily superfood gummies.
- Supports daily nutrition.
- Helps fill nutrition gaps.
- Supports digestion / gut health using approved structure-function language.
- Supports immune health using approved structure-function language.
- Supports energy metabolism using approved structure-function language.
- Supports cognitive performance using approved structure-function language.
- Supports hair/skin/nails using approved structure-function language.
- Low-sugar or sugar-free where the specific SKU supports it.
- Third-party tested if current source supports it.
- 60-day money-back guarantee if active funnel terms match.
- Retail availability at Target/Ulta if current and permitted.
- Review/customer-count claims if current source screenshot supports them.

## Claims We Cannot Make

- Treats, cures, prevents, or diagnoses any disease.
- Cures IBS, constipation, bloating, fatigue, immune issues, anxiety, ADHD, hair loss, skin disease, or digestive disorders.
- Replaces fruits and vegetables.
- Replaces medical advice, GLP-1 treatment guidance, or prescribed supplements.
- Guaranteed energy, weight loss, digestion, skin, hair, immunity, or focus results.
- "Doctor recommended" without specific proof and approval.
- "FDA approved" for the dietary supplement.
- "No side effects."
- "Cancel any time instantly" unless the active subscription flow proves the exact statement.
- Competitor superiority claims without support.

## Proof Required

- Ingredient / nutrition panel for serving size, sugar, and SKU-specific claims.
- Active landing page screenshot for first-order price and subscription terms.
- Active guarantee/refund policy screenshot.
- Science page screenshot for clinical/survey/testing claims.
- Retail screenshots for Target/Ulta price, rating, availability, or purchase-count claims.
- Approved legal claims matrix before launch.
- Customer testimonial source/permission if using review content in ads.

## Sensitive Topics

- GLP-1 medications and weight loss.
- Digestive discomfort and constipation.
- Disease-adjacent gut health claims.
- Children's health claims.
- Body image, shame, and diet culture.
- Subscription billing and cancellation.
- Sugar/sugar alcohol tolerance.

## Compliance Issues

- Dietary supplement advertising must avoid disease claims.
- Structure-function claims need proper substantiation and, where required, supplement disclaimers.
- Health-related advertising should follow FTC substantiation standards.
- Meta may restrict health, body, personal attribute, or weight-adjacent targeting/copy.
- GLP-1 creative must not imply medical treatment, medication outcomes, or guaranteed weight loss.

## Brand Tone

Recommended:

- Smart, direct, skeptical-friendly, no wellness shame.
- "Real life" over aspirational perfection.
- Useful, native, and proof-aware.
- Playful enough for gummies, serious enough for supplementation.

Avoid:

- Medical white-coat cosplay without credentials.
- Shamey diet language.
- Overly polished influencer wellness.
- Miracle-benefit claims.
- Calling buyers unhealthy, lazy, deficient, or failing.

## Mode Flags

Default mode: aggressive but proof-aware.

No `--extreme`, `--unrestricted`, `--research-only`, or `--creative-only` flag was supplied by the user.

## Forbidden Words / Phrases Unless Explicitly Approved

- "Cure"
- "Treat"
- "Heal"
- "Fix your gut"
- "Reset your microbiome"
- "Detox"
- "Lose weight"
- "Clinically proven to [specific result]"
- "Doctor recommended"
- "FDA approved"
- "No side effects"
- "Works for everyone"
- "Replaces vegetables"
- "Guaranteed results"

## Required Disclaimers / Launch Notes

- Use any required dietary supplement disclaimer if making structure-function claims.
- Do not place disclaimers as the voice of the ad. Keep selling copy sharp, then handle disclaimers in launch notes, landing page, or required disclosure areas.
- For GLP-1 angles, include legal/compliance review before launch and avoid medical advice.

## Countries / Regions

Primary research assumption: USA.

Verify product availability, claims, and ad rules for any non-USA market.
