## Source Quality Notes

High credibility:

- Official product pages for what Gruns itself claims.
- FDA/FTC/NIH for claim boundaries.
- Retail listings for publicly visible price/review/retail context.

Medium credibility:

- Trustpilot and independent review sites for themes and friction.
- Reddit for raw objection language and buyer psychology.

Weak but useful:

- Affiliate reviews, influencer posts, and anonymous anecdotes. Use for language and hypothesis generation only.
