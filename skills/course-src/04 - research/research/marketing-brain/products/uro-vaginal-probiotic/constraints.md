# Constraints

Claims we can make:
- "Supports healthy vaginal pH"
- "Supports healthy vaginal odor"
- "Supports healthy vaginal flora"
- "Formulated with 4 Lactobacillus strains + XOS prebiotics"
- "#1 Best-Selling Vaginal Probiotic in the USA"
- "Over 10 Million Bottles Sold"
- "30-day money-back guarantee"
- Structure/function claims: "supports," "promotes," "helps maintain"

Claims we cannot make:
- "Cures," "treats," or "prevents" BV, yeast infections, or any diagnosed condition
- "Clinically proven to cure vaginal odor"
- Any disease claim (FDA supplement rules)
- Specific medical outcomes without substantiation

Proof required:
- Structure/function claims must be backed by general probiotic/Lactobacillus research
- "#1 best-seller" claim requires category/retail data backing
- Testimonials must be genuine and not imply disease treatment

Sensitive topics:
- Vaginal health — explicit language may be flagged on Meta/TikTok
- Sexual references — "my guy noticed the difference" is borderline for ad platforms
- Body image / shame language — avoid copy that shames women for odor

Compliance issues:
- Meta Ads: vaginal/intimate health products face policy scrutiny — avoid explicit anatomical language in ad copy; use "intimate health," "feminine wellness," "down there"
- TikTok: similar restrictions — "vaginal" may be flagged in some ad formats
- FTC: testimonials must reflect typical results; add "results may vary" where needed
- FDA: must include "These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."

Brand tone: Confident, empowering, destigmatizing. Direct but not crude. Science-backed but accessible. Warm and relatable — like a knowledgeable friend, not a clinical brochure.

Mode flags: Women's health / intimate wellness — treat topic with sensitivity and pride, not embarrassment

Forbidden words: cure, treat, prevent, diagnose, infection (in a disease context), disease, medication

Required disclaimers:
- "*These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."
- "Results may vary."
- "Consult your healthcare provider if you have specific health concerns."

Countries/regions: USA (primary market)
