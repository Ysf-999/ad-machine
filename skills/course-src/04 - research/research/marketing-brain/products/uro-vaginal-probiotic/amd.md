## Platform Compliance Notes

- **Meta Ads:** Avoid "vaginal" in headline copy — use "intimate," "feminine," "down there," "pH balance." Odor/freshness angles are generally fine. Partner/sex angles need careful framing.
- **TikTok Paid:** Similar restrictions — "vaginal" may trigger review. Organic is more lenient.
- **Required on all ads:** *"*These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."*
- **Never claim:** cure, treat, prevent BV/yeast/infections as diagnosed conditions.
