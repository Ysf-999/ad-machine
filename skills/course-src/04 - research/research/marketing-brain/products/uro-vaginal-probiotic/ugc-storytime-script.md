### Do's

- Do lean into the vulnerability of the story in the first 20 seconds.
- Do keep the tone conversational, like you're confessing to a close friend.
- Do hold the bottle clearly so the branding and label are visible during the proof section.
- Do speak naturally, including the pauses and sighs as indicated by the story flow.

### Don'ts

- Don't sound overly dramatic and fake-cry; keep it real and relatable.
- Don't use medical terms like "BV" to avoid platform flags.
- Don't make definitive medical claims; stick to "pH was off" and "bacterial imbalance."
- Don't introduce the product until the mechanism has been explained.
