## agentchad seeds (A-rules; owner can strike any)
Seeded from the free agentchad.io skills (Reality Drama Engine, Trendjack Animator), not from an owner review. Source copies: `knowledge/agentchad/`. The owner can strike any of them; an owner R-rule always wins over an A-rule.
- **A201** Plates first: one approved reference still per character, per product (from the real photos in `brand/product-photos/`) and per place that comes back. Every scene still is made FROM its plates (attached as references, "same woman as image 1; change only: clothes, place, light, pose"). A person drawn again from text is a new person. A new or changed plate needs the owner's yes again.
- **A202** Dialogue = one speaker per clip (shot-reverse-shot). Each turn has the speaker's own still and frozen voice sentence; the listener "listens silently". A clip with both people in frame is a reaction or two-shot where only one person, or nobody, speaks.
- **A203** One voice per character: never mix a person's on-camera voice with a separate TTS narrator voice for the same person.
- **A204** Same clothes per scene. The payoff lands in the same place as the sting, in front of the same witness.
- **A205** Start-frame engines take at most two shots per clip (one hard cut). Never reuse the same clip at another offset to reach a shot count: trim each clip to its slot and use still-only inserts (a slow push on one still) for silent beats under about 1 s.
