## Concrete rules and numbers
- Don't front-load everything ("all our studies, every benefit, why it works"). He says that loses the audience.
- Pace the reveals in this order of breadcrumbs:
  1. Unique mechanism first. His example: "what these monks discovered wasn't just another sleeping aid."
  2. Simple science behind it. His example: "this herb actually repairs your brain's sleep-wake cycle." Keep it simple and add interesting details.
  3. Studies, stacked proofs, results, testimonials.
  4. Build anticipation, then escalate with a cliffhanger such as "but what happened next shocked even our research team." Make it "even crazier."
- Every section should leave the viewer asking "but what happens next?" The goal is building belief in the solution, not just retention, and the vehicle is strong storytelling.
- UGC is short and punchy and gets its point across fast. A VSL changes beliefs.
- As a beginner editor, the brief, script and assets are supplied and you piece them together.
