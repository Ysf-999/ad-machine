## What is taught
The instructor finishes explaining how he chose b-roll for a UGC-style ceramic travel mug ad. The b-roll should let the viewer picture themselves using the product, so he shows other people with the cup. He stresses quick pacing and varying how the creator appears on screen. For this deliberately organic ad he skips transitions, animations and sound effects, then starts on TikTok-style captions in scannable chunks.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- B-roll choice: show other people (e.g. other girls) using the cup so the viewer can visualize themselves using it. The mug is positioned as a stylish accessory that fits outfits.
- Pacing: "something new happens in the ad like every two to three seconds."
- Vary the creator treatment across the ad:
  - creator green-screened over b-roll
  - creator full screen
  - creator green-screened over images
  - creator green-screened over video
  - no green screen, then green screen again, then none ("mashing it up")
- Transitions, animations and SFX are normally added after b-roll. For this ad they were skipped because it must look like the creator edited it herself, as "super organic type of ugc edit". The result is no transitions, no sound effects, simple.
- Captions: TikTok-native look, as people use when posting TikTok videos.
- Caption chunking: split the text into short "scannable chunks".
