## What is taught
The editor builds the hook text layer for an organic-style ad. The script has two parts ("This is choking your dog…" and "And this gets your dog to stop pulling."), so the hook is given about 5 seconds, with each text card held roughly 3 seconds. He places the text in the top half, duplicates and swaps text cards, and checks card durations. He then cuts the voiceover foundation and starts the third layer, b-roll.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- The hook needs at least roughly 3 seconds on screen. Because the script has two parts, he keeps about 5 seconds for the hook and says not to rush it.
- Text card scale is usually about 50. He says this depends on the sequence/canvas size. The frames show the Scale field at 50.0 and Position at 540, 960 (the centre of a 1080×1920 vertical frame).
- Hook text goes on the top half of the frame, so it is easily readable.
- To make the next text card, hold Alt and drag the clip along the timeline to duplicate it, then replace the text or graphic.
- A short two-word card does not need 3 seconds. Shortening it is fine. His example, "FORWARD MOMENTUM", is 1 second 25 frames, and he says "for two words I think that'll pass".
- To check a card's length, select the clip and use the mark-clip button (Set In/Out to clip). The in/out duration then appears in the program monitor timecode, e.g. 00:00:01:25.
- After the cuts are made, the "foundation" is set. The next layer is b-roll, sourced online: clips of a dog pulling on a leash.
