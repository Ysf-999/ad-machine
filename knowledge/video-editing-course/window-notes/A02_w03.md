## What is taught
This window finishes the overview of organic ad styles. Lifestyle ads sell a vibe and an "expensive" feel rather than product features. The shared principle is that the audience should never feel they are watching an ad. Editing for these styles is deliberately simple, so the instructor shifts to the homework: make an organic ad from a brief. He also argues that practice matters more than technical editing tutorials.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Lifestyle ads: don't sell the product, sell the lifestyle. Format the ad around that, make the brand feel expensive, and build a whole vibe around it.
- Key principle for all organic ad styles: viewers should feel they are watching content they already love and engage with on social media, not an ad.
- Testimonials: run them as is.
  - Optionally cut silences and mispronounced words.
  - Optionally add basic captions so the ad is easier to follow. He says even that isn't important.
- Other organic styles (non-testimonial): find an organic Instagram, TikTok or YouTube Shorts video that is "crushing it" in the niche and copy its editing style. That video is the template.
- Homework:
  - A brief sits below the video, with an inspiration video, guidelines and content to use.
  - He may record a Loom walking through the brief.
  - The task is to make an organic ad from the brief and send it in.
  - He personally reviews every submission with actionable feedback. If the ad is good, he gives praise and explains why it works.
- Technical editing (clipping, music) will be covered in later modules, but he calls it "the easy part". Speed comes from reps: the more videos you edit, the better and faster you get.
- No numbers were given for durations, cut frequency, zoom, captions, dB or fonts.
