## What is taught
The instructor shows how to replicate a winning ad (another client's, Honeypacks) by cloning the original creator's voice in ElevenLabs. He downloads the original ad video from the Facebook Ad Library link and uploads it to ElevenLabs Instant Voice Clone. The cloned voice can then voice new AI VO scripts, with hooks and a hold + CTA, written in a brief. The brief itself is a creator/editor brief containing the ad concept and scripts.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Goal: replicate the success of an ad with a very strong hook and hold rate. Clone the original creator's voice and use it for new scripts and hooks.
- Brief structure shown (a Milanote board, "Honeypacks - Slipping HP in Drinks"):
  - Ideation Ad Concept card, which has the theme and a link to the original ad. It says to play with different hook angles, e.g. "I regret slipping this into my man's drink… can barely walk now". It also gives a hypothesis for why the ad works: it talks to women, and men think they are getting "insider" information.
  - Hook 1, Hook 2, Hook 3 cards, each with an "AI VO:" script.
  - A Hold + CTA card (2 cards): an AI VO script plus COPY ("Shop honeypacks.com"). The note says the copy should "only appear with the last line of VO".
  - Arrows from each hook point to the single Hold + CTA block, so any hook can be followed by the same hold and CTA.
- Brief shown to editors is stripped down to the script part for simplicity, though real briefs carry much more information.
- ElevenLabs cloning steps:
  1. Download the source video (Facebook ad).
  2. Go to ElevenLabs → Voices → My Voices → Add a new voice → Instant Voice Clone.
  3. Name the voice (e.g. "Honeypacks female test").
  4. Upload a video or audio file (mp3, wav and so on). Each file must be under 10 MB (the UI says "up to 10MB each"). The sample shown was 9.4 MB.
  5. Optionally tick "Remove background noise from audio samples". Use it if the audio is poor quality. Skip it if the voiceover has no background noise, as here.
  6. Add a description, which is optional but "probably helps" (e.g. "Young African American Female Voice").
  7. Tick the consent checkbox (rights/consent confirmation) and click Add Voice.
  8. Then go to Studio and "start from scratch".
- UI facts on screen: Samples limit is 25. Labels limit is 5. The UI says sample quality matters more than quantity, noisy samples may give bad results, and more than 5 minutes of audio total brings little improvement. Slot usage was 29/30.
