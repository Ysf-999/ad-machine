## What is taught
The instructor generates AI voiceover for "Hook 2" of a Carepod chronic-conditions ad in ElevenLabs Studio. He first creates a chapter per hook and pastes the script. He then iterates on the read: removes needless commas and pauses, capitalises words for emphasis, and regenerates until the delivery sounds human. When the take still sounds flat, he raises Style Exaggeration to 25%.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Make one ElevenLabs Studio chapter per hook (Hook 1, Hook 2, ...). Paste the script, select all the text ("tag everything"), and press Generate.
- Listen carefully to every take. Reject a take that sounds robotic, with no emphasis on words and no positive energy: "she just went through the sentence without any emphasizing."
- Fix the script text, not just the settings:
  - Remove commas that create pauses you don't want. Example: "diagnosis, taught me" becomes "diagnosis taught me", because there's "no need for a short pause."
  - Highlight words to stress by capitalising them: "taught me ONE thing" and "you CAN'T afford to compromise."
  - Punctuation changes between takes: "thing..." becomes "thing -" (an ellipsis becomes a dash) before the later regeneration.
- Regenerate repeatedly. Takes go from "not that good", to "already a lot better", to "she can exaggerate some stuff a bit more."
- Still not expressive enough: open the voice settings and set Style Exaggeration to 25%. Other sliders visible are Stability and Similarity, and Speaker boost is on.
- Changing voice settings affects every paragraph using that voice. The panel warned "This change will affect 4 paragraphs (in 2 chapters) that will need to be regenerated."
- Instructor's technique: he reads the lines aloud himself to hear where the emphasis should fall, then encodes that in the text.
