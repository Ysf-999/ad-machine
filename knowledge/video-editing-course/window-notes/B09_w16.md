## What is taught
Sound design for a VSL-style ad in Premiere Pro. Organise the SFX library into category folders so a specific sound (e.g. risers) is easy to find. Risers build tension in hooks and just before an important point, and a Studio Reverb on the riser track makes the tail carry into the next section instead of stopping abruptly. The instructor then plays the finished mix to show the riser leading into the word "GONE" and a tiny riser used as a transition.

- Put the Studio Reverb effect on the audio track that carries the risers. The instructor says he uses it on that track "and same thing 41 below, which is track 4" (audio is unclear here).
- Studio Reverb settings:
  - Preset: "Great Hall".
  - Change only the Wet level on output. It "would start somewhere around 40"; increase it to taste.
  - The screen shows Wet ≈ 72.3% and Dry ≈ 60% (hard to read). The other visible values are Room Size 70, Decay 6000 ms, Early Reflections 20%, High Frequency Cut 4000 Hz, Damping 100%, Diffusion 70%.

  - A riser builds tension right before the word "GONE" ("Records that had stood for years. Gone.").
