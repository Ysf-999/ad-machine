## Concrete rules and numbers

- After cropping, fit each clip, then make sure the bottom clip is inside the safe zone and visible at all times. Do this by moving its Position and centring it.
- The bottom clip's Position Y was nudged up a little because it sat close to the safe-zone edge. Aim for a balanced layout.
- Keep everything inside the safe zone when building the split screen.
- B-roll match: when the speaker says "I no longer get an arm workout", cut to a first-person clip of her being pulled by the dog.
- If a b-roll clip is too short for the spoken line, use Generative Extend. Drag the clip edge out to the length you need.
- Generative Extend settings: Premiere conforms the clip to 30 fps. Clicking Continue uploads the clip to Adobe servers, which generate extra frames.
- Generative Extend costs credits. Use it only when necessary, or when close to the monthly credit renewal date.
- Check the clip's start and end points before extending. If a bit of the next frame bleeds in at the end, Premiere extends from that last frame and the result will be bad. Trim cleanly first.
- Sequence is 30 fps (project items list 30.00 fps).
