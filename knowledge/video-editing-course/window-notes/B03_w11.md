## What is taught
The instructor is fine-tuning an AI voiceover in Premiere Pro, working on the audio track only. He makes precise cuts between words and trims pauses so the delivery sounds natural. Where a breath or inhale sound is audible, or a gap is too long, he re-cuts and listens again. He judges each change by replaying from the start of the hook.

- Sometimes the cut point you want falls between two frames and Premiere won't allow it. Fix: turn on **Show Audio Time Units** (timeline panel menu). This allows sub-frame, more precise audio cuts.
