## What is taught
How to fix ElevenLabs mispronouncing or skipping part of a domain name in a CTA voiceover. The instructor asks ChatGPT (temporary chat) how to force the spelling, tests its phonetic-spelling suggestion in ElevenLabs, and saves the result as the CTA. He then moves to Premiere Pro to start the edit and set up project folders and bins.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Problem: ElevenLabs keeps dropping or mangling the "co" in "dogfriendlyco.com". The model needs to say "dog friendly co dot com".
- Fix workflow:
  - Open a ChatGPT temporary chat.
  - Ask how to make ElevenLabs spell the domain fully. Put the domain in quotes or brackets and name the part it misses ("co").
  - A second prompt that worked better pasted the whole voiceover line: "when trying to voice over the following line, elevenlabs is not spelling 'co' part, how can i fix that?"
- ChatGPT's suggestions (visible on screen):
  - Add spaces or hyphens: `dog friendly c o dot com`.
  - Phonetic spelling: `dog friendly coe dot com` or `dog friendly koh dot com`.
  - Uppercase or quotes: `dog friendly CO dot com`.
  - SSML: `<say-as interpret-as="characters">c o</say-as>`.
  - Quick test: "dog friendly C O dot com".
- The instructor picked phonetic "koh" (k-o-h), written as "dog friendly koh dot com". The text field read "Shop the sale before it ends at dog friendly koh dot com". The generated audio pronounced it correctly.
- Write the domain as words ("dot com"), not as a URL, for the TTS script.
- The result is saved as the CTA clip and the voiceover is complete.
- ElevenLabs settings on screen:
  - Voice: Brittney – Social Media Voice (Fun, Youthful & Informative)
  - Model: Eleven Multilingual v2
  - Speed: slider about 60% toward Faster
  - Stability: about 25–30% (a warning reads "Under 30% may lead to instability")
  - Similarity: about 50%
  - Style Exaggeration: about 50%
  - Speaker boost: on
- The CTA line is 56 characters and about 2–3 s long.
- Editing starts in Premiere Pro after the voiceover is finished: "edit and polish". He creates a few folders/bins, starting with one for sequences.
