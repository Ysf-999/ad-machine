## Concrete rules and numbers
- To lengthen a b-roll clip to fit narration, use Speed/Duration and set the **duration**, not the speed percentage.
- Workflow:
  - Press X to mark the clip start to end (In/Out).
  - Move the out point to where the clip should end. The program monitor then shows the target in/out duration.
  - Right click the clip, choose Speed/Duration, and type that duration.
- Worked example: the clip was 1 s 14 frames and needed to be 1 s 18 frames, so about 4 frames more. Typing 00:00:01:18 gave a speed of **91.67%**.
- Rule of thumb: the stretch is not noticeable "until the number is not like 18 or 70" (the transcript is garbled here). The intent is that a mild slowdown, here about 8%, is not visible to the viewer. Keep it near 90%+ and avoid large slowdowns.
- Keep the same clip running through the line rather than cutting to another clip.
- Check the script against the generated VO. The line "The difference was immediate for my dog and over 400,000 other frustrated owners" came out wrong ("for my dog and over frustrated owners"), so regenerate it.
- Dialog settings shown: Reverse speed off, Ripple edit (shift trailing clips) off, Time Interpolation set to Frame sampling.
