## What is taught
The instructor generates AI voiceover for a hook-by-hook ad script in ElevenLabs Studio. Each hook is its own chapter, using one cloned/selected voice ("Carepod Mama"). He tunes emphasis by raising style exaggeration and by typing key words in ALL CAPS ("ONE", "CAN'T"). He regenerates only the selected sentence until it sounds right, converts and downloads each chapter, then checks that the pieces sound right when joined. The script lives in a Milanote board with hooks and a "HOLD" body section.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Emphasis fix: raise **style exaggeration** a bit and capitalise the words to stress. Hook 2 became "My recent COPD diagnosis taught me ONE thing – when it comes to the air you breathe, you CAN'T afford to compromise." After this the voice stressed "one" and "can't", which he called "exactly what I was looking for".
- Regenerate only the selected sentence ("Regenerate selection") rather than the whole chapter.
- Workflow per hook: generate, listen, fix emphasis, regenerate, then Convert chapter, wait, and download.
- After downloading, play the sentences back to back to hear how the pieces sound joined. Hook 2 was line 1 (about 7 s) followed by "I tried humidifier after humidifier, hoping for relief." (about 3 s).
- Hook 3 uses the same pattern: "Living with chronic bronchitis taught me ONE thing… the air in your home can either heal you or harm you. For years, I tried humidifier after humidifier, desperately trying to find relief." Again "one" is in caps. Playback showed about 6 s for the chapter.
- Voice settings: the "Override settings" toggle was off, so the project's default voice settings applied. His emphasis tweak was done in the settings, not by overriding per chapter.
- After the hooks are done, generate the full voiceover for the HOLD (body) section. The window ends as he starts it.
- ElevenLabs credits drop with each generation (196,194 → 196,139 → 195,951 remaining), so every regeneration costs credits.
- A new chapter defaults to a different voice ("Honey Packs Female"). He had to switch it to "Carepod Mama" for Hook 3, so set the voice for each new chapter.
