## What is taught
The instructor cleans an AI voiceover (hook clip plus hold clip) in Premiere Pro. He levels peaks with audio keyframes, auto-deletes all pauses via the transcript panel, then checks the script line by line so it sounds natural. The goal is a human-sounding read, with pauses added back deliberately later.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Clip volume is at +13 dB (Properties panel shows 13.0 dB). The instructor says to "keep it around 13."
- To flatten loud peaks ("high bars" in the waveform), add volume keyframes on the clip's rubber-band line. Hold Ctrl so the cursor shows a "+" and click to place marks. Drag the keyframes down to lower those peaks. Adjust to follow the waveform.
- Do this on the loud words (e.g. "one change stops pulling forever"). It is optional elsewhere, "based on the bars / volume."
- Step 2 is to delete all pauses, even though pauses get re-added artificially later. This is his preferred workflow.
  - Open the Text panel, then Transcript, then the filter icon, then choose Pauses.
  - Click Delete, then "Delete all."
  - Premiere ripple-cuts out every pause, leaving visible cuts in the audio clip.
- Step 3 is to go line by line and word by word until the script sounds natural. Criteria:
  1. Pause
  2. Intonation
  3. Natural speaking sounds, meaning inhales and exhales. Make sure they sit right and don't "mess with the vibe."
- After listening, he finds the pause between the hook part and the start of the "hold" part is too quick, so it needs adjusting.
