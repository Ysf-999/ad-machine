## What is taught
The instructor finishes polishing an AI voiceover script in ElevenLabs Studio before generating it. He cleans up paragraph breaks and punctuation, and shows how to insert deliberate pauses. He then explains that the script should sound spoken and imperfect rather than polished, so he adds filler connectors ("And", "So") to stop the read sounding like a list.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- A "(short pause in VO)" note left in the script by the copywriter should be removed, or replaced with a real pause using ElevenLabs "insert break".
- Insert break lets you choose a pause length. He first says "five seconds or three", then corrects himself: 3 s is the maximum, so the options are 1, 2 or 3 s. The default shows as "1s". A pause can go anywhere in the script, including mid-sentence (the frame shows "no hidden mold, [1s] exactly what I've been searching for!").
- Merge fragments that belong to one thought into a single paragraph. Example: "Breathing clean, hydrated air isn't a luxury, it's essential." The dot became a comma.
- Use punctuation to steer delivery:
  - Replace "..." with a dash: "Clearly I'm not the only one who thinks so - thousands of people have upgraded to the World's Cleanest Humidifier."
  - Add an exclamation mark to the CTA ending: "Get yours today and start breathing easier!"
- Regenerate the whole script after editing. Regenerating consumes character credits; the counter went from about 195,019 to about 194,573 remaining.
- Always listen through the full generation. Around the end, the read sounded like a flat list with no emotion and unnatural flow ("No endless filter replacements. No hidden mold. Exactly what I've been searching for. Breathing clean, hydrated air. Breathing clean, hydrated air isn't a luxury. It's essential. Clearly, I'm not the only one who thinks so.").
- Fix flat reads by adding natural spoken words at the start or end of sentences:
  - "And clearly I'm not the only one who thinks so"
  - "So get yours today and start breathing easier!"
- The script doesn't have to be perfect. Small mistakes or spelling issues are fine, even better, because they feel more authentic. The goal is "organic, authentic, real", not perfect.
- Regenerate only the changed section ("Generate selection" / "Regenerate selection") rather than everything. He says he won't regenerate again at the end.
