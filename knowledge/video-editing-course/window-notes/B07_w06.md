## What is taught
The instructor shows how he picks and places trending music for an organic-style ad. He finds a trending TikTok sound, downloads it, and sets its level to about -20 dB under the voiceover. He starts the song's beat drop at the start of the hold, and he leaves the audio unprocessed so the ad feels organic. He stresses that this placement is situational.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Pick music that is trending on TikTok at the time. This track was chosen because it was trending.
- In TikTok Creative Center, open the song's "See analytics" page to check its interest over time, example videos, age range and related interests.
- The instructor noted that interest in this audio was mostly on ads. The age-range chart was empty or down for a couple of weeks.
- To get the file, Google the song name and include the artist name (here "Aesthetic" by Tolan Kim), then download it. Sped-up, slowed-down or reverb versions can also be tried.
- Import the audio into the project and look at the waveform to find where the beat drop is.
- Aim to start the hold at the start of the beat drop.
- Lower the music to roughly -18 to -22 dB. He uses -20 dB.
- Workflow:
  - Cut at the beat drop and delete the part before it.
  - Move the music back so the beat drop lands at the start of the hold.
  - Extend the track through the rest of the hook.
  - Add a cut between the hook audio and the hold audio so the two sections are easy to tell apart.
  - Colour coding marks which section is the hook and which is the hold.
- For an organic feel, do no processing on the music. Leave it as is (no effects or edits).
- Placement is situational. The beat drop could start right in the hook, or later in the hold. Experiment to find your own style, and share what works in the community.
- The example hook line is: "Three reasons I regret getting this from my dog. First off my dog suddenly stopped yanking on the lead. Which means I no longer get that arm workout during our walk."
