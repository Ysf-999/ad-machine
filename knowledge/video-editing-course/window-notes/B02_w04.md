## Concrete rules and numbers
- Cuts should be "as clean and as fast as possible".
- Remove any gap where the speaker is not talking. The waveform on the timeline shows these gaps. One gap he cut was about 0.1 s of silence.
- Remove breaths and pauses between phrases. Example line: "you know that feeling when you take the last sip of your coffee and it's ice cold, that hasn't happened to me in six months".
- Leave "just a bit" of a pause at some points so the speech still sounds natural. Don't strip it to zero everywhere.
- Foundation checklist for the A-roll or voiceover:
  - no empty spaces or blank spots
  - no bad takes of the script
  - no dead air that gives people a chance to scroll away
- Reason given: attention is very low on social media, so don't give viewers space to leave.
- The three hooks (hook 1, 2, 3) are placed back to back and cut at the start of the timeline. Hook 3 starts with "me in six months I was spending money".
