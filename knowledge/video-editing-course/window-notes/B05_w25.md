## What is taught
This is the wrap-up of the first breakdown, a ~40 s organic UGC dog-leash ad. The instructor's main lesson is that two things made it perform: a simple, easy-to-understand script, and a carefully chosen music track. He says editors often skip music selection to save time, and he recommends using an LLM to find the track. At the end he teases the next breakdown, a 2:19 "Reverse Aging VSL".

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Reasons the ad performed well, per the instructor:
  1. Script simplicity. It is easy to understand and "visible in almost every aspect", so the visuals match what is said.
  2. Music, which he says gets neglected.
- Put real effort into music selection and don't take the easy route to save time.
- Music search method:
  - Give the script to GPT and ask it to analyze the script's emotional arc.
  - Then go through music libraries one by one, e.g. Artlist, Epidemic Sound, even YouTube.
- The music track in this ad is "Bunker Buster – Colorful and Animated.mp3". Its volume is set to −8.0 dB (Properties panel). It is 40:10 long (timeline shows 00:00:40:10), starting at 0 and running the whole ad.
