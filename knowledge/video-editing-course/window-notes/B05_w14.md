  - Rec. 709
  - VBR 1 pass, target 19 Mbps
  - AAC 320 kbps, 48 kHz, stereo
  - Estimated file size 11 MB for the 4:21 clip
  - Hardware encoding (Intel)
- Text removal:
  - Use Vmake's Watermark & Text Remover. The instructor says the "remove watermark" mode works fine for text too.
  - Up to 3 files can be dropped in at once.
  - Click "Download 5s Preview Video" (free). A 5 s preview is enough because the clips are under 5 s, so no paid full download is needed.
- Put the cleaned clips back in Premiere on a track above the texted clips (V2 over V1).
- Total of 3 clips needed text removal. Before exporting, the editor double-checked the timeline for any other clips with text.
