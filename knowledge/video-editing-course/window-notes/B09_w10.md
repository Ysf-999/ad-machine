## What is taught
The instructor finishes animating clip-level motion in Premiere. One example is a left-to-right position animation on the "coach" images, done clip by clip. He then exports the "hold" (the finished edit with subtitles and graphics) as a transparent-alpha ProRes .mov. He refines it in After Effects, which gives more control. He prefers this over Premiere's "Replace with After Effects Composition" dynamic link.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Clips that share the same animation run at the same speed. Matching scale speed across them is not needed.
- Coach images: position animated left to right, set up clip by clip.
- After finishing the Premiere animation, export the hold only. The purpose is to refine and animate things in After Effects.
- Dynamic link ("Replace with After Effects Composition") is avoided for three reasons:
  - It stresses RAM, even on a strong rig.
  - He has hit "countless bugs and rendering issues".
  - Preview and render are slow.
- Export procedure:
  - Set the In point at the start of the hold and the Out point at the end.
  - Preset: **Apple ProRes 4444 with alpha**. He calls this "very important".
  - Format: **QuickTime** (.mov), not MXF. MXF has more features, but QuickTime is "good enough".
  - If the preset isn't listed, open More presets, search "4444", and pick "Apple ProRes 4444 with alpha" in the QuickTime row.
  - Star the preset to favorite it.
- Then import the .mov into the After Effects project and build a dedicated hold composition.
