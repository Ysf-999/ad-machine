## What is taught
The instructor explains how to make a UGC-style ad look "camouflaged", as if a creator made it organically rather than looking like an obviously edited ad. His method is to green-screen the creator: cut her out in CapCut's free Auto Cutout, export her on a solid green canvas, then key the green out in Final Cut Pro. This lets him put new backgrounds or elements behind her. He says this is one effective way to do it, not a rule, and that some ads can look obviously edited.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- UGC ads "should look like they're coming from creators". He says this is not a rule; some ads will obviously be ads.
- Goal for this ad: make it "almost camouflaged" so it seems organically made by the creator.
- CapCut cutout workflow, in order:
  1. Import the video into CapCut.
  2. Open the Video panel's Cutout tab and tick "Auto cutout". Wait for the analysis.
  3. Check the result for edge issues. Here he saw none.
  4. In Basic, tick Canvas and set Canvas to Color, with green as the colour.
  5. Export.
- Final Cut step: import the exported clip, apply the "Green Screen Keyer" effect, and the green is removed.
- Newer Final Cut versions (and other editors) may have a built-in cutout or AI subject-analyzer tool. He prefers the "old way" because CapCut is free and works well for him.
- Source clip length is 1:04:12 (about 1 min 4 s at 30 fps).
- Green hex shown in the picker: 0FFF00.
