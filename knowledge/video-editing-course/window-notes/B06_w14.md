## What is taught
The instructor builds a hook for a postpartum UGC ad in Premiere Pro. He picks the opening spoken lines as the hook, sets the hook text card as a PNG overlay, and moves it inside the safe zones. He gives the text a slight tilt so it looks phone-made. He then duplicates the sequence as "Breakdown V2" so he can make hook variations by swapping only the hook text.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- The hook is the opening spoken section, about 4–5 seconds. He cuts it at "Can we stop with the getting my body back narrative for a second, because that's not what this is about."
- Label hook clips with a colour label so they stand out from the hold. He uses blue for hooks. Right-click the clip, choose Label, then pick the colour. Label colours are assigned to shortcuts such as Shift+F1 and Shift+F2, which he says speeds up the workflow a lot.
- The hook text sat outside the safe zones, so it has to be moved inside them.
- Hook text is a PNG overlay exported from Figma. Replacing the PNG file on disk (overwriting "HOOK 1.png") and then reverting it updates the change in the sequence, because the file is linked.
- Slightly tilt the text so it looks phone-made, like an Instagram story with no big edits. He gives rotation values: "0.6", "anything less than 1.5", "even 1.2".
- Final hook text settings seen on screen:
  - Position about 550.4 / 736.0
  - Scale about 67.8
  - Rotation about 1.2°
  - Earlier states: scale 92.9 at position 540/960, then 71.4 at 566/714, then rotation 2.4° at 550.4/708.3.
- Making variations:
  - Duplicate the sequence in the Sequences folder.
  - Rename it with your usual naming convention, here "BREAKDOWN V2".
  - Only the hook text needs to change, since the hold is identical.
