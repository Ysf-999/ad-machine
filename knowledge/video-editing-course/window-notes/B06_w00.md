## Concrete rules and numbers
- Study the brief thoroughly before editing. It tells you the concept and what the ad needs, which helps with creative decisions during the edit.
- If anything is confusing or you have doubts, ask the strategist or the person who wrote the brief. He says this saves time and energy and calls it one of his best pieces of advice.
- Briefs differ by brand and agency. This agency uses Milanote.
- Audience context for the product: most ad spend goes to women, mainly early 30s to early 50s. Keep the audience in mind while editing.
- Product context: supplement collagen (Zend Wellness, "MetaGlow Collagen"). Claimed benefits: naturally boosts GLP-1 (a hormone linked to craving control), decreases stress, reduces wrinkles, promotes joint health.
