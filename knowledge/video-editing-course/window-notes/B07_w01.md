## What is taught
The instructor shows how to pick an AI voiceover type from the creative brief (organic female, or a male podcast-style voice). He then walks through cloning an "organic" UGC-style female voice in ElevenLabs. The source is a TikTok video from a relevant niche. He downloads it, uploads it as an Instant Voice Clone, and saves the voice for use in Studio.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Read the brief first. Knowing what needs to be done tells you which AI voice to use (organic female, or male podcast style). It saves time and AI credits.
- The brief for this ad calls for an organic female voice.
- Source for the clone, in order of preference:
  1. The client's own UGC library. Look for a voice that matches.
  2. If nothing fits, search social media. He uses TikTok and searches keywords related to the ad (here "dog harness").
- The clone source here was the third result, a video from Dog Friendly Co.
- Only the audio matters, so it doesn't matter how the video is downloaded. He right-clicks the video and chooses "Download video".
- The file must be under 10 MB (the ElevenLabs upload limit). His file was about 8 MB; the screen shows "Download.mp4", 00:34 long.
- ElevenLabs steps:
  1. Voices > Create or Clone a Voice.
  2. Choose **Instant Voice Clone**.
  3. Drag and drop the file.
  4. Tick "Remove background noise from audio recordings". Keep it on when there is background music, as in this clip.
  5. Click Next. It analyses the audio.
  6. Use Preview voice to test it. The preview line was "A dream without a plan is just a wish." He judged it "not bad at all".
  7. Enter a name and click Save voice.
  8. Labels are optional (he skips them). A checkbox confirming rights and consent to clone the voice must be ticked.
  9. Go to Studio.
- ElevenLabs updates its layout constantly (he says he has seen about 8 variations), but the process stays the same.
- The account shows 29/30 voice slots used.
