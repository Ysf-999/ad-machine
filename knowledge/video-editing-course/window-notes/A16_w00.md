## What is taught
Content upscaling turns low-quality footage or images (e.g. 360p) into usable material, up to about 4K. It works on images and video. The instructor uses it for low-res internet footage and weak product images. He demonstrates Topaz Gigapixel AI on a blurry Pinterest image, using a before/after split slider.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Use cases: (1) footage from the internet that is low quality; (2) bad product images that need enhancing or upscaling.
- Claimed range: 360p up to "even like 4k".
- Tools in use: Topaz Gigapixel AI for images, Topaz Video AI for video. He mentions Topaz Photo AI exists but doesn't own it.
- Gigapixel settings visible in the demo (the instructor doesn't read these out):
  - Resize Mode: Scale, 2x (other presets 0.5x, 4x, 6x, custom)
  - AI Model: Standard (alternatives: Lines, Art & CG, Low Resolution, Very Compressed)
  - Suppress Noise: 27
  - Remove Blur: 71
  - Face Recovery: on, strength 49
- Demo result: the input was 696x914 px and the 2x output 1392x1828 px.
- The tool shows a before/after comparison. Hovering or dragging left to right reveals how much noise was removed and how much crisper the image is.
- After upscaling you can zoom into the image, which is the point for punch-ins on stills.
- He says other AI tools do the same job. He only shows what he and his team use, and will update the video if something better comes out.
