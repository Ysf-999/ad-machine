## What is taught
The instructor shows how to turn a hook script into an AI voiceover in ElevenLabs Studio. He creates a chapter per hook, pastes the script, and picks a saved voice (a cloned creator voice, "Carepod Mama"). Before generating, he tunes the voice settings: stability, similarity and style exaggeration.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Workflow per hook: rename the ElevenLabs Studio chapter to the hook name ("Hook 1"). Copy the "AI VO" text from the script board (Milanote-style board, "Carepod – Chronic Conditions Angle") and paste it into the chapter. Choose the voice.
- Voice cloning options:
  - Instant Voice Clone: record yourself for about a minute (the UI says it needs only 10 seconds of audio), upload it, and you can generate anything from text in that voice. He says the second example covers this.
  - Voice found in the Voice Library: hit "add" to put it in your library. Saved voices then appear in the voice dropdown.
- His usual settings, set before generating:
  - Stability: about 35
  - Similarity: about 40
  - Style exaggeration: about 15
- Every voice is different and needs its own tweaking. Some voices work better left more stable, and the same goes for similarity and style exaggeration.
- Changing the voice settings makes the affected paragraphs need regenerating (the UI warns "This change will affect 3 paragraphs… need to be regenerated").
