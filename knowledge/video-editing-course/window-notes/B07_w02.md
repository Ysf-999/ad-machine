## What is taught
The instructor starts the AI voiceover workflow in ElevenLabs Studio. The ad script is pasted from the brief and laid out so the structure is visible: three hooks, then the hold (body), then the call to action. He explains the project settings (chapters enabled, model choice) and how to pick a voice before generating anything.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Open Studio from the left panel in ElevenLabs. Start a new project with "New blank project" and choose "audio project".
- Enable chapters in project settings: three dots at the top right, then project settings, then Enable chapters.
- Model: Eleven Multilingual v2. He avoids v3 because it is still in alpha and not stable ("stability over new features").
- Create a new chapter for a fresh start. He named chapter 1 "OG" and the new one "NEW".
- Script layout, with the text copied from the brief:
  - Paste hook 1 at the top, leave a blank line, then hook 2, blank line, then hook 3.
  - Leave a blank space, then paste the entire hold section.
  - Leave one more blank space, then add the call to action.
  - The blank-line separation makes it clear which part is hook 1/2/3, hold and CTA, which helps when editing later.
- Voice selection: click the voice, then "All saved voices". The list is grouped into Recently used, Professional (ElevenLabs' own voices) and Cloned (your own clones).
- Calibrate the voice before generating anything, using a button he points to at the end of this window.
