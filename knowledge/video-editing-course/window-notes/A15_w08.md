## What is taught
The instructor finishes the music/SFX part of his tech stack (Artlist, searched by the effect you need, e.g. "whoosh"). He then covers where to get ad inspiration: save good hooks and ads from TikTok and Instagram, share them in a team "ad-inspo" Discord channel with a note on what you liked, and save them to boards in Atria. This window is a workflow and tooling overview. It contains no editing parameters.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- SFX and music source: Artlist. Search the sound you need ("if you need a whoosh, you search for whoosh"). Every SFX result in the frame is about 00:02 long.
- Inspiration habit: whenever you see a good video, hook or video ad on TikTok or Instagram, save it (copy the link or use Instagram saved folders). Go back to the folder when you have a creative block, then replicate it or take inspiration from it.
- Team practice: a dedicated #ad-inspo channel where anyone who finds a good ad (Instagram, Atria, anywhere) posts it and explains what they like about it. It is used daily to bounce ideas around. The community will get an equivalent channel, "Ecom Talent Inspo Channel".
- Atria: scroll the Discovery feed for inspiration and save ads to named boards, e.g. "good hooks" or "good edits". Reference these boards when editing ads.
- No numbers on cut rate, zoom, captions, dB or fonts are given in this window.
