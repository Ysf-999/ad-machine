## What is taught
Keep every caption and important item inside a "safe zone" overlay so nothing is cropped or covered in any Facebook/Instagram placement (Feed crops the 9:16 frame; Reels covers the bottom with UI). Check the video with a semi-transparent green safe-zone overlay while editing. Then export one 9:16 file instead of per-placement versions. The instructor shows bad examples with text cut off and starts a good example, which carries into the next window.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Manual overlay method, if your editor has no custom overlay feature: place the safe-zone graphic on the top track of the timeline and set its opacity to 40%.
- Feed placement crops the top and bottom of the 9:16 frame. Text outside the safe zone is cut off.
- Bad example 1: the hook caption "Ladies, men are OBSESSED with this smart wallet!" sits at the top, partly outside the safe zone. In Feed it gets cut, so viewers see only "with this smart wallet" and the message stops making sense.
- Bad example 1, later captions: "airtag attachment / SO HE NEVER LOSES IT AGAIN" is half-visible, and "holds up 12+ cards & cash" loses half of "cash" in Feed.
- On Reels, the bottom area holds the profile/advertiser name, description, and a button (CTA). Any text there is hidden. The instructor calls one brand example "insane" for this.
- Rule: all captions and important items go inside the safe zone.
- Export once, 9:16, 1080×1920. This gives one version optimized for all placements.
- Side margins: text can go slightly outside the safe zone on the left and right. He leaves some space from the screen edge there (the explanation is cut off at 09:00).
