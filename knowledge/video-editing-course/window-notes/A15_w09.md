## Concrete rules and numbers
- Atria (tryatria.com) lets you filter by niche/industry. His example is filtering to "beauty and personal care" to see lots of ads in that niche for inspiration.
- Meta Ad Library: search for any brand you like to see all the ads they run. His example is AG1 by Athletic Greens.
- Atria sometimes doesn't have all of a brand's ads. For a brand you like, search the Meta Ad Library as well to see everything it recently ran.
- Meta Ad Library filters named: active status/active types, when the ads were active, and media type (images vs videos).
- The Meta media-type filter options on screen are: All media types, Images (little to no text), Memes (images with text), Images and memes, Videos (ads that contain video), and No image or video.
- Advice: start editing ads and "put in the reps"; there is no secret tool or resource that makes winning ads.
