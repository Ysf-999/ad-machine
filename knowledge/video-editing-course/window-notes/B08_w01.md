## What is taught
How to build the text-only ("organic") ad layer: pick music for the emotion you want, drop it on the timeline, and cut each text card on the beat so card-to-card changes feel seamless. The instructor stresses reading time and consistent text position. He builds the text cards in Figma, exports them as PNGs, and places them in Premiere, starting the first "hold" at the beat drop.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Choose music by what you want the audience to feel. This is why the music is chosen in the text-only version.
- Cut text cards on the beat. It makes the change from one text to the next feel natural and seamless, so viewers aren't pulled off the video.
- Leave enough time to read each card. Older viewers need more time. What takes about 2 seconds to read may take them about 3, 4 or even 5 seconds.
- Keep text in roughly the same screen area from card to card. If it starts in the top half, keep it there. Moving it around from clip to clip confuses the audience.
- Use a safe-zone overlay PNG on a top video track (the timeline has a "SAFEZONE" PNG across the whole length).
- Text cards are exported as PNGs, one per card. The naming scheme is H1, H2, H3… for "holds". Variants within a hold are H1-1, H1-2, H3-1, H3-2, H3-3, and so on. The project bin also shows CTA-1.png, HOOK 1-1.png and HOOK 1-2.png.
- The first hold should start at the beat drop. He finds the drop by scrubbing and lowering the volume, then makes a cut there.
- Figma text style seen on the card "FORWARD MOMENTUM" (the other cards appear to share it, but this is not confirmed):
  - Font: Proxima Nova, Bold, size 92.
  - Fill: white (FFFFFF).
  - Stroke: black (000000), Outside, weight 4.
  - Line height: Auto. Letter spacing: 0%.
  - Export: 1x, PNG.
- Two-tone highlight cards are also used: white text on a red box (E85048) with corner radius 22, and white text on a blue box.
- The Figma file has 2–3 line stacked cards. Examples: "Traditional harnesses and collars give dogs exactly what they want / FORWARD MOMENTUM", and "Dog pulls 👇 Gets redirected back toward YOU".
