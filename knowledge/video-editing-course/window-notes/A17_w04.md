## What is taught
A walkthrough of generating a hook voiceover in ElevenLabs Studio. Paste the script as separate lines/paragraphs, generate, listen, and then reformat the text and regenerate until it sounds authentic. The instructor stresses that you cannot paste a whole script, hit generate once, and get a perfect result. Once the hook sounds good, convert the chapter and download it.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Voice settings depend on the voice, so generate a few times and listen before settling ("play around with it").
- Workflow: create a Project, with a Chapter per hook (here "Hook 1"). Put each script line as its own paragraph. Highlight all lines and hit Generate.
- Separate paragraphs produce an awkward pause between lines. Fix this by merging lines into one paragraph. The two lines were merged as "…one thing...when it comes to the air your child breathes, you shouldn't compromise."
- The most important step for authentic-sounding VO is experimenting with text formatting. Generate, listen several times, and adjust formatting.
- Regenerating only re-renders changed paragraphs. Unchanged paragraphs stay exactly as before, so a good take is not lost.
- Play the full chapter to check it as a whole. If one paragraph sounds "a bit off", select it and use "Regenerate selection". The instructor regenerated the "Desperate to ease his symptoms…" paragraph once.
- When satisfied: Convert → Convert chapter (Hook 1) → wait for it to piece the audio together → Download.
- Sample script: "My son's severe asthma diagnosis taught me one thing. When it comes to the air your child breathes, you shouldn't compromise. Desperate to ease his symptoms, I followed our pediatrician's advice and bought a humidifier, hoping it would make a difference."
