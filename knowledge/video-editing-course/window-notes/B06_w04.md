## Concrete rules and numbers
- Keep a safe-zone PNG overlay on its own top video track and lock it. Leave it on and locked until the edit is completely done, so it can't be clicked, moved or changed by accident.
- Build a custom effects bin of frequently used effects by dragging them in. This avoids digging through folders.
- UGC ads should take "just a couple of minutes" to edit.
- Before editing, know who the ad is for. Keep the avatar and their desire in mind.
- Edit in layers, one task at a time, to avoid jumping between tasks and losing focus. The order shown on the board is:
  1. A-roll/VO
  2. Music
  3. B-roll
  4. Animations/Transitions
  5. Captions/Text
  6. A further step below Captions/Text that is cut off on screen (unclear).
- Start with the A-roll (the VO / talking creator). Make sure there are no silences.
