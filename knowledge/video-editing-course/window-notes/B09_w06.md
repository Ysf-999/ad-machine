## What is taught
This window is a continuing breakdown of a VSL-style ad (runners/athletes story, cordyceps product) in Premiere Pro. For each narration line, the instructor picks b-roll or stills that literally match or emotionally reinforce the words. Quick cuts keep static material such as news screenshots engaging, and a mosaic/pixelate filter on the cordyceps clips builds suspense around the "secret".

## Concrete rules and numbers
- High-contrast colour pairs for emphasis or highlighting: black/white, blue/yellow, red/white. Any colours that fit the ad or client brand palette also work.
- "The international running community was in shock": use a still of the athlete carrying her national flag, celebrating victory.
- "Accusations flew": find news articles about the doping allegations from that time, screenshot them, and make quick cuts between the screenshots.
  - Keep the important keywords in the centre of the frame during the quick cuts.
  - Reason given: staring at one news article is boring, and the fast pace keeps it engaging.
  - A highlighter effect can draw attention to a specific keyword (explained later in the course).
- "Steroids, doping, drugs": use literal stock footage of syringes and pills.
- "But every single test came back clean": use a clip of a Chinese runner finishing a race with no one else in sight, to emphasise the huge winning margin.
- Introducing the coach: use images of the coach and his team.
- "This person reveals something that changed endurance sports forever": show the coach with his team, then a clip of a person running on a treadmill. The treadmill shot looks mysterious, which gives the "coach is going to reveal something nobody knows" feeling.
- "His athletes weren't using synthetic drugs": show the athlete celebrating, plus generic syringe stock footage.
- "Something far more ancient and far more powerful": use a few cordyceps clips with a mosaic filter on top. It pixelates the image and makes the viewer curious about the secret.
- Mosaic how-to:
  1. Choose a clip and duplicate it.
  2. In the Effects tab, search "mosaic" (the instructor keeps it in a Favorites folder).
  3. Apply it, then in Effect Controls change the horizontal and vertical blocks.
  - No block counts are given.
