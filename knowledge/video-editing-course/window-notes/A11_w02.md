## What is taught
Hook text should be on screen from frame one so viewers can scan it and decide whether to keep watching. Long-winded creator intros (UGC) should be condensed into a short, punchy hook that gives context. The instructor runs three exercises that turn a creator's rambling opener into a one-line hook.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Hook text must be present from frame one. If it isn't, viewers get distracted and move on.
- Make it clean and readable, and slightly bigger than the regular captions in the rest of the video.
- Place it where it won't compete with the visual. For the UGC exercises it goes at the top of the screen.
- Viewers should know what the video is about within the first 2 seconds.
- Condense by keeping the contrast or the impressive number, cutting the skepticism and backstory setup, and focusing on the result and who it's for. In his words: "no fluff, just context".
- Worked examples:
  - Creator: "I used to spend hours at the gym doing cardio, but I never saw results until I discovered this 15-minute workout routine." Hook: "I replaced two hours of cardio with 15 minutes of THIS". It keeps the long-vs-short contrast and is snappier. "THIS" is in all caps at the end to create curiosity.
  - Creator: "I was skeptical at first because I've tried every sleep supplement… this one actually helped me sleep through the night for the first time in years." Hook: "This supplement finally helped me sleep 8 hours as an insomniac". It drops the skepticism and keeps the result plus the audience.
  - Creator: "As a professional gamer who spends 12 hours a day in front of screens, I was getting terrible headaches until I found these glasses." Hook: "How I game 12 hours a day without headaches as an eSports pro". It keeps the impressive hours figure and upgrades "gamer" to "eSports pro" for authority.
- Pattern to apply: the hook is one line that combines a concrete number or result, the target person or identity, and an authority-boosting label. A curiosity word such as "THIS" in caps can replace the reveal.
