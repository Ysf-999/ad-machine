## What is taught
The instructor tightens the A-roll of a testimonial ad in Final Cut Pro by cutting dead spaces (silences and breaths between phrases) so the speech flows smoothly. He repeats this for the whole video. He then introduces his concept for the ad: a "green screen" style, where the creator talks in front of supporting visuals, as in TikTok reaction videos. The goal is for the ad to feel authentic, "like she edited it herself", rather than heavily produced.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Before editing, check the audio. Then cut out all dead spaces in the A-roll or voiceover, whichever you are working with.
- Remove silent gaps and breaths between phrases. Example: the gap after "cold undrinkable last half" before "but then one of the women in my class" gets cut, and the sentence then flows more smoothly.
- If you only have a voiceover script and no A-roll, fill the whole video with b-roll. It must flow smoothly with no dead spots.
- For a testimonial, still support what the speaker says visually. The chosen method is a green-screen concept: the talking head sits in front of supporting visuals that show what she describes (her coffee habits).
- The editing goal for this ad is authenticity. Use few sound effects and little flashy editing, so it looks like the speaker made the ad.
- Sequence length after the first tightening pass: about 50:10 at 15:45 in the recording, about 49:20 at 16:00, and 44:28 at 16:15. The tightening pass shortens the timeline by roughly 5.5 seconds, and the unit is unclear (the on-screen readout is hard to read).
