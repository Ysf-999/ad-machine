## Concrete rules and numbers
- Animated captions and titles: download a template or plugin, drag and drop it, then change the text or number. He says this is "super easy" and makes editing faster.
- Logo reveals: swap in your image and the template animates the logo.
- Envato stock video is usually very high quality, "usually 4k". It now has a vertical filter, so you can filter by horizontal or vertical.
- Envato music can be filtered by theme and genre. Envato also offers sound effects, graphic templates, graphics, 3D, presentation templates, photos, fonts and add-ons (Photoshop and similar).
- His single recommendation if you invest in one resource: Envato Elements. It helped him a lot in his early stages.
- Pexels is the free version for photos and videos, and it also has a vertical filter. It has no plugins or templates the way Envato does.
