## What is taught
The instructor introduces a live re-edit of an ad he already made for a client. He will explain the brand, product and ad idea, then rebuild the edit from scratch. Before that he presents his "fastest editing workflow": build the edit in layers, each added on top of the previous one, in a fixed order. This window covers the first three layers (A-roll/VO, music, b-roll) in detail.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Layer order, from the on-screen note "The fastest editing workflow":
  1. A-Roll / VO
  2. Music
  3. B-roll
  4. Animations / Transitions
  5. Captions / Text
  6. Sound Effects
- Layer 1 (A-roll/VO) is the base layer and foundation.
  - Put the voiceover (or creator talking-head) at the bottom.
  - Cut out silences and polish it until it flows smoothly.
  - Everything else is built on top of this.
- Layer 2 (music): he likes to choose the music at the start, even before editing, so he can build on top of it.
  - If the track has drops, position the video around them and play with them.
- Layer 3 (b-roll): once the A-roll is done, go through the script start to finish and add b-roll.
  - Start with the hook.
  - His example is a script about "the best coffee cup", though the sentence is cut off at 03:00.
- Past volume, as he describes it: about 5–6 concepts a day, each with 3 variations. Dividing the edit into layers is what he learned from this.
- He says the workflow comes from his own experience and may not apply identically to everyone.
- He suggests turning his habits into a personal checklist.
