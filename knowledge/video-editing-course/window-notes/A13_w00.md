## What is taught
This is a short lesson on asset management. Store all client footage locally on an SSD instead of working out of Google Drive or Dropbox, and keep it in one fixed folder structure per client. Working this way makes you faster and keeps you familiar with each brand's content, which the instructor says is where many ad ideas come from.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Download all client footage and keep it on a local SSD. If the computer lacks space, buy an SSD ("don't be a brokey").
- Benefit 1: speed. You never have to dig through the client's Google Drive or Dropbox when you start an ad.
- Benefit 2: familiarity. Knowing what footage exists tells you what you can do, and "some of the best ideas for the ads come from knowing the content."
- Folder structure:
  - One master folder, "Clients", which is the SSD.
  - One folder per client (Client A, Client B, Client C...).
  - Four subfolders inside each client folder:
    1. **UGC Content**: content from creators, testimonials, reviews, "basically people".
    2. **Product Shots**: B-roll showing the product in use or in action.
    3. **High-Production Videos**: separate folder only if the brand has any.
    4. **Ad Concepts**: where all exported ads for that client go.
- Everything for one client and brand lives in one place. Add new files whenever the client sends content or you find it, for example on their Instagram.
- The instructor gives no numeric values (durations, dB, zoom, etc.) in this window.
