## What is taught
The instructor sets up burned-in captions for a pet-brand organic ad in Premiere Pro. He generates auto-captions with a small minimum duration and double lines. He then styles them from the brand guidelines: HeadingNow Regular at about 45 pt. He places them in the lower part of a custom safe-zone grid, which keeps them clear of the platform UI "no place" area.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Caption generation (Create captions dialog): format Subtitle, Lines = Double (he prefers double to single). He says the minimum duration should be "seconds", but the number is unclear in the audio. The dialog shows a min-duration value of 12 and a gap between captions of 0 frames.
- Captions are styled from the brand guidelines, which were provided before work started.
- Font: HeadingNow, which the brand normally uses in two weights.
  - Regular ("64 Norm Regular") is used for captions.
  - Medium ("65 Norm Medium") is for bigger text such as titles and headings.
- Font size: he tries 40, judges it "too small", and settles on 45. He says he normally goes between 40 and 45.
- Caption text is centered and white, with no visible stroke or box in the frames.
- Placement: centered horizontally in the safe-zone rectangle, at the bottom side of the safe zone.
  - Text may overlap the small bottom rectangle (the no-go area) only slightly.
  - The Properties panel shows a vertical offset of 334 after he moves the text. The default zone is bottom-center.
- Caption text on screen reads "This one / change stops pulling forever." in two lines. The track is called "Subtitle" (C1).
