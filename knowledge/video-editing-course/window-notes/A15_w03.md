## What is taught
How to source reference and b-roll footage for ads from social platforms. When a script line is hard to show visually, search TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube for the topic and download clips with free third-party downloader sites. This window covers TikTok (ssstik.io, UrleBird), Instagram (iGram) and YouTube (y2mate). It is a tech-stack walkthrough, not editing technique.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- When unsure how to visually show something in the script, search TikTok for what the script talks about and download footage from the results.
- UrleBird:
  - Searches TikTok by hashtag (example: "coffee") and by user.
  - Results show posting date, views, likes and comments counts.
  - It cannot show the comment text.
  - Each clip has a "download without watermark" option.
- Instagram: search a hashtag or keyword (examples: coffee, humidifier), copy the link, paste it into iGram (igram.world). The site auto-detects video, photo, story or reel.
- iGram is described as "the most efficient tool" the instructor has found for Instagram downloads.
- YouTube: paste the link into y2mate, which "most of the times" gives 1080p.
- Download links will be in the video description.
