## What is taught
After the A-roll has been chopped (silences and filler words removed), the last step is making the voiceover as clear and consistent as possible. The instructor does this in Premiere's Audio Track Mixer, where an effect added to a track applies to every clip on it. He loads a Multiband Compressor on the A1 track to fix uneven voice levels.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- A finished A-roll chop has all silences and filler ("pillar") words removed.
- Use the **Audio Track Mixer**, not the Audio Clip Mixer. Enable it from the Window menu.
- An effect added to an audio track applies to all clips on that track, so there is no clip-by-clip editing.
- Track mapping: Output 1 corresponds to A1 (first column), Output 2 corresponds to A2, and so on.
- Voiceover target level: **-6 dB to -12 dB**, described as the "sweet spot".
- The raw, unprocessed voiceover sits around **-18 dB** and is "all over the place" (inconsistent).
- Fix: Multiband Compressor. Path: effects-slot arrow in the track mixer → Amplitude and Compression → Multiband Compressor. Double-click it to open the Track FX Editor.
- The Multiband Compressor defaults on screen (the instructor had not yet changed them):
  - Crossover: low 120 Hz, mid 2000 Hz, high 10000 Hz.
  - Four bands, each with Thresh 0 dB, Gain 0 dB, Ratio 2:1, Attack 10 ms, Release 100 ms.
  - Limiter: Thresh 0 dB, Margin 0 dB, Attack 4 ms, Release 500 ms.
  - Options: Spectrum on Input, Brickwall Limiter, Link Band Controls (all unchecked).
