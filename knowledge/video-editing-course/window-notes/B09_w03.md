  - After choosing the preset, lower the threshold and adjust it by ear to suit the voice.
  - For a woman or higher-pitched speaker, try the "High Voice DeEsser" preset. Other presets exist.
  - Screen shows Multiband mode, threshold about -30 dB, center frequency about 4700 Hz, bandwidth about 3000 Hz.
- Music brief for this ad: "heavy and hard hitting".
- Pick one track with multiple moods and map each section of the script to a different passage:
  - Hook: suspenseful buildup. Pair it with sound effects to emphasize the buildup more.
  - Main body, first part: heavy, repetitive music that is catchy, with a "serious dispute" mood.
  - Next part: serious suspense, matching the script.
  - Product introduction: the music turns positive, like overcoming an obstacle ("makes me feel like a champion").
  - Call to action: a toned-down version of the music, taken from the end of the track.
- Remix tool: changes the music's length by remixing it, so you don't get the same vibe throughout. The waveform looks odd after remixing, and the instructor says that is expected.
