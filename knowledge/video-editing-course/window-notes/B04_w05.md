## Concrete rules and numbers
- Music is placed after the voiceover and cuts are done. Isolate the music track, build a short intro section, then land the main body of the music on the "hold".
- Sync rule: the music's beat/drop should land at the start of the hold (the "Your dog's body…" line). He trims the start of the music clip and drops a marker at the hold point to match it.
- Problem: with raw music under the VO, the speaker can't be heard.
- Fix: put a Simple Parametric EQ on the music track only, with these settings:
  - Center: 1500 Hz
  - Q: 4
  - Boost: −15 dB
- The settings are saved as a preset named "music eq" (right-click the effect → Save Preset, Type: Scale). It can then be dragged onto any music clip.
- Reasoning: voice sits in the mids, around the middle of the spectrum. A −15 dB narrow-ish cut at 1.5 kHz carves space for the voice while the highs and lows of the music stay.
- Music source search used: "indie rock upbeat", filter Instrumental, on Artlist.
