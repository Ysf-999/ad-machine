## What is taught
How to source authentic B-roll from TikTok search and download it without a watermark. Search a keyword that matches the script (e.g. "coffee", "humidifier"), pick viral creator clips, copy the link, and download in HD through a downloader site. The instructor also covers the legal and risk side of using creators' footage.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Search TikTok for a keyword tied to the product or script topic. Viral clips come up, e.g. one with 1.8M views.
- Prefer clips with no captions or text on them, so they can be dropped in as B-roll.
- Match B-roll to what the script says. Example: for a humidifier brand, search "humidifier". If the script talks about bad or plastic humidifiers, use the plastic-humidifier clips.
- Download workflow:
  1. Open the video on TikTok.
  2. Click "copy link".
  3. Paste the link into the downloader site.
  4. Click "Download without watermark / HD".
  5. The result is an MP4 in the highest quality, with no watermark.
- Risk: they have had little trouble so far using social-media B-roll.
  - The creator could ask for removal or could sue ("probably").
  - In most cases the creator sees themselves in an ad, doesn't know the brand, and asks the brand to take the ad down. The instructor says this is the worst that usually happens.
  - Safer use: pure B-roll where creators' faces are not shown.
