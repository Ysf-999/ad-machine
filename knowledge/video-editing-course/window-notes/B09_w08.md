## What is taught
The instructor walks through the b-roll choices in a VSL-style ad for a mushroom/cordyceps pouch product. His rule is that every b-roll clip should visually illustrate the idea in the script line it sits under, so the viewer understands without effort. He covers literal matching, contrast pairs, and a few small effects (a black card, a reversed clip, an inverted clip).

## Concrete rules and numbers
- B-roll should convey the idea of the script line. "If you manage to do that, you are golden."
- Example of an imperfect visual that still works: a turmeric-powder pill stands in for a cordyceps supplement. It conveys "supplement capsule", and viewers don't know the difference.
- Black screen with text for "that's not efficient at all". It emphasizes the competitors' weakness and sets the brand apart. The caption is styled differently there: "THAT'S NOT *efficient* AT ALL..." with a bold sans for most words and a large italic serif for "efficient".
- Reverse a clip for the product intro: the pouches originally fell down, and reversed they fly back up into the frame.
  - Premiere method: right-click the clip, then Speed/Duration, then tick "Reverse Speed".
  - In ffmpeg terms this is `reverse`.
- Literal matching to the script:
  - "mushrooms" gets clips of mushrooms.
  - "directly through your oral tissues" gets the client's clip of the person placing the pouch in the mouth and pushing it to the gum.
  - "straight into your bloodstream" gets a red blood-cell / vein clip.
  - "activating the benefits almost instantly" gets a person running, because it is relevant to the idea.
- Contrast pair:
  - "No digestion, no waste": an abstract clip with many small particles scattering, as if wasted. The second part of it is inverted (the Invert effect).
  - "Maximum absorption": a clip with more uniformity, where the particles are mostly consolidated.
  - The two clips are chosen to contrast with each other.
- "The same compound that helped unknown runners become world champions": cordyceps footage, then athletes in action. This makes the link that cordyceps helped them win.
- "Now optimized for performance": Flow pouches. This connects the cordyceps used back then to the product now.
- The ending is all product clips because of the call to action.
- Hooks use clips of the event. Hook 1 is "How China's track team legally cheated in 1993."
- Cut pace on the timeline is roughly one clip per phrase, about 1–3 s each, with clips changing every few words.
