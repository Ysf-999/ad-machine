## Concrete rules and numbers
- Process for each script is the same as for the first one: edit the text in ElevenLabs Studio, regenerate, and listen until it sounds natural. The regenerated Honeypacks hook "sounds a lot better than it did originally."
- To clone a voice from a video that has music over it, first run the file through vocalremover.org (upload video or audio). It separates the music from the vocal. Save the isolated vocal as mp3 and use that as the cloning sample.
- The tool lets you mute either stem: music only, or vocals only (music slider down, vocal slider up).
- It is "not 100% perfect, but it really does the job," and it is free.
- Winning-ad formula the instructor cites: existing brand content + AI-generated script + AI voiceover + product b-roll on top. It needs no creators, no custom scripts and no custom footage.
- The quality bar is that the voiceover must sound authentic and real. Pasting scripts into ElevenLabs and hoping for the best is the mistake to avoid.
- No timings, dB levels or other numeric edit settings are given in this window.
