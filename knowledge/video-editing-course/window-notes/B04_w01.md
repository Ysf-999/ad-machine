## What is taught
How the editor picks music for an ad. He briefs ChatGPT about the ad and asks for search phrases. He pastes those phrases into stock-music sites (Artlist and Extreme Music). He also shows a workaround for downloading Extreme Music tracks without a subscription, using Internet Download Manager. The workaround is a piracy-adjacent hack, so the agent should not rely on it.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- ChatGPT workflow for music search:
  - Give context first (the brief), then say what is needed, then ask what the model needs from you.
  - Keep chatting until "we're on the same vibe". He says he is not a prompt engineer and does not craft a super prompt.
  - Prompt used (summarised): working on this brief, need music to support the message. Wants something upbeat, cheerful, a little faster, and doesn't mind rock-ish even though the topic is dogs. Asks for search words to use on Artlist/Extreme Music. He attached a screenshot of the brief.
- ChatGPT returned "Main search phrases". He picked the first: **"upbeat rock pop positive advertising"**. Other phrases returned:
  - energetic indie rock commercial upbeat
  - happy confident pop rock upbeat rhythm
  - playful upbeat guitar groove ad
  - fun optimistic pop rock commercial
  - driving light rock energetic feel-good
  - uplifting rhythmic pop beat commercial
  - modern upbeat indie pop electric guitars
- It also returned extra sets: a "dog-friendly / wholesome" set and a "smart solution / confident energy" set. It offered to narrow by BPM range or mood intensity to match the edit's pacing.
- Two music sources: Artlist (most common and popular among video editors) and Extreme Music (paid, "kind of expensive").
- Download hack:
  - Install Internet Download Manager (IDM). He says it isn't free and hints at finding a free version.
  - While a track plays in the browser, IDM shows a "Download video from this page" tab.
  - Check that the detected length matches the track. Example: Pure Black is 2:22 on the site and 2 min 22 sec in IDM.
  - Click it, browse to the project's "music" folder, and type the track name yourself. IDM does not supply the name; the default is "Search - Extreme Music.ts".
  - Start Download. He says the file then opens like the original track.
- Extreme Music search shown: "Minimal cinematic tension" with "Ask me anything" (AI search) on, giving 63 results.
