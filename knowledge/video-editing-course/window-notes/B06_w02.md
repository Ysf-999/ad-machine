## What is taught
This window finishes the instructor's project folder template for ad batches: Audio, Image, Video and Other subfolders under 03 Assets and 04 Other. ## Concrete rules and numbers
- Audio folder: music and SFX (sound effects) go here.
- Image folder: a safe-zone template PNG is kept in the template folder so it is copied into every batch automatically. Any images downloaded for the ad also go here.
- Video folder: all downloaded video goes here. This covers b-roll found online, stock footage, TikTok videos and YouTube videos.
- "04 Other" folder: used rarely, for templates found online such as motion graphics, video templates and graphs.
- Reasons given for organising:
  - Saves time when hunting for something made earlier.
  - Makes client changes easier.
  - Is a good habit for beginners, and gives an edge over other editors.
  - Lets the project be handed to another editor who can read it easily.
- The Premiere project panel's folder structure mirrors the Explorer folder structure exactly.
- No numeric editing values (durations, zoom, dB, etc.) are given in this window.
