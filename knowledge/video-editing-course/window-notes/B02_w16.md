## What is taught
How to break captions into scannable chunks for UGC/TikTok-style ads. A long sentence in one block fails the safe zones and looks too dense, so it is split into short chunks, each on one or two lines. The instructor then gives the caption style: Proxima Nova Semibold with a black outline.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Do not put a whole sentence in one caption. The example "You know that feeling when you take the last sip of your coffee and it's ice cold" would need three lines to fit the safe zones, and viewers won't read that.
- Split it into three chunks:
  1. "You know that feeling"
  2. "When you take the last sip of your coffee"
  3. "And it's ice cold"
- Break each chunk into two short lines so the eye doesn't travel far. For example, "When you take the / last sip of your coffee".
- "That hasn't happened to me in 6 months" is also split into two lines. The instructor prefers this to one long line, which makes the eye travel across the screen.
- Principle: "people don't read captions, they scan them." Ask whether each caption makes sense to the viewer and is easy to read and scan.
- Font is Proxima Nova Semibold, described as the organic TikTok font used on the platform.
- Size is 28.0 in Final Cut. The instructor says size depends on the editing software, so use this as a template.
- Outline is black, width about 3 (screen shows Width 3.0, Opacity 100%, Blur 0).
- Captions should look exactly like organic, native TikTok captions.
- Centre alignment, placed in the lower-middle of the frame.
