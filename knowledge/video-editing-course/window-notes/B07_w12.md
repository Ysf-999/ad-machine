## What is taught
The end of the organic-ad breakdown covers the last two layers, captions/text and SFX. Captions are auto-generated from the transcript in Premiere, restyled in a brand-matching font, and checked against the script. The hook and CTA cards come from Figma, and everything has to sit inside the safe zone. SFX are minimal: one seatbelt click where the harness clips in. The instructor also says to use jarring or "uneasy" effects only where the context calls for that feeling, then make variations, double-check, and export.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Spotting the technique (the jarring/seamless effect from the preceding part) is "halfway there". Then experiment to make it as seamless as possible.
- Don't use it every time. Use it only where it matches the context, e.g. to deliberately convey panic, uneasiness or other bad emotions.
- Captions workflow in Premiere: open the sequence, go to the Text panel, click "Create captions from transcript".
- Pick a font that matches the brand and the ad. This ad uses Proxima Nova (Semibold per the Properties panel).
- After generating captions, check them against the script and fix errors.
- Hook and CTA text are made in Figma and exported as PNGs, both in one sweep. They are placed as image layers (the hook at the start, the CTA at the end).
- Check that all content is inside the safe zone.
- SFX: no overdoing it. The only SFX is a seatbelt click each time the harness clips in (e.g. on the "Three reasons I regret getting this from my dog" hook). It is described as "works like a charm".
- Finish by making variations, double-checking, then exporting.
- Turn off the safe-zone overlay before export so it isn't in the final.
