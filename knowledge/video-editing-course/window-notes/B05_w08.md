## What is taught
The editor builds the b-roll layer of a "Watch" organic-style ad in Premiere Pro, working from the client's creator-footage bank. For each voiceover line (front-harness redirect, "stopping their pulling without pain", "no training needed") he searches the stock folder for creator clips that match what is being said. He drops them on V1/V2/V3 over the VO, trims them to the phrase, and scales them up when the framing is loose. Where footage is limited he reuses clips or looks for more creators to diversify.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Source b-roll from the client-provided creator bank. Folders seen: Brolls, livamos, Raw Footage Week, socials with taylor, drive-download, stock, music, VO.
- Match the visual to the spoken line:
  - "front attachment redirects that energy back towards you" gets a clip of a leash or harness being clipped or redirected.
  - "stopping their pulling without pain" gets a walking-dog clip.
  - "no training needed / just physics doing the work" gets a dog clip. He wants a different one here and says the one he first used is probably not right.
- Trim each b-roll clip tightly to its phrase. Clips on the timeline are about 3–8 s long (for example a clip spanning ~00:22:20 to ~00:25:00). Cuts are frequent, with a new clip every few seconds.
- Diversify: do not use the same creator for everything. He looks for "more clips from other creators" and notes "there should be one more".
- Scale clips up when the framing is wide. He says "let's scale them up" while placing the clip. The Properties panel shows the default Scale of 100%, Position 540×960, Opacity 100%. The timeline appears to be a 1080×1920 vertical sequence (inferred from the 540/960 position).
- Stack extra b-roll on V2 and V3 above the main V1 layer. Overlaps go on V2/V3 so a cutaway sits on top of the creator talking head.
- Audio is left as a separate VO track (named clips like "hold dogs.mp3" and "so you're stopping their pulling without pain.mp3"), with a music track under it ("Bunker Buster – Colorful and Animated.mp3"). VO is cut into per-phrase clips.
