## What is taught

The principle is that for each part of the script, the visual should illustrate or add context to what the creator is saying. ## Concrete rules and numbers
- Listen to what the creator says, then find footage that gives more context to it.
- Every part of the script should be "enhanced with visuals". Visuals are matched to the spoken line, e.g. "last cold coffee sip" gets a clip visualizing that feeling.
- If there is no product-use footage (e.g. her drinking from the Stoke cup, because she only recorded the script), don't force it. Use the creator herself on screen for authenticity, or other footage.
- Pattern-break rule: "every few seconds something new should come on."
- Example of the rhythm, given in the transcript:
  - Green-screen creator with b-roll in the background.
  - Then a switch to a new frame ("cold that hasn't").
  - That frame stays on for about 2 seconds ("six months i was").
  - Then back to the green-screen creator with an image in the background ("here i was spending money…").
- Total length of the example project is about 45 s (timeline shows 45:02).
- The project is 1080×1920, 30 fps.
