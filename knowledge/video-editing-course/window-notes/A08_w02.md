## What is taught
The instructor walks through the brief for VSL Ad #2, a coffee-cup brand (Stoke / STTOKE), laid out in a Milanote board. The ad structure is: very fast, hectic hooks that build tension, then a "hold" that slows and relaxes, then product talk and a CTA. He also points to where to source footage: stock sites, social platforms, the client's shared folder and the brand's Instagram.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Concept: inspired by the fast-paced editing style of the example ad. Start by attacking the coffee industry and cup alternatives (microplastics, "ugly, bulky, wasteful"), then introduce Stoke Cup as the ultimate coffee cup.
- Hooks: fast-paced, super hectic, with hectic music. "A lot of things going on", building tension.
- Hold: at the hold, chill out the script and shift the pacing from hectic to relaxed and more tranquil. Relaxed is the emotion the product should evoke.
- After the hold, the rest of the ad talks about the product, then a CTA.
- Brief on screen: 3 hooks, 1 hold, 3 total ads.
- Brief on screen: sizes needed list 1080x1080 (1:1), 1080x1350 (4:5), 1080x1920 (9:16) and 1920x1080 (16:9). Which are ticked is unclear.
- Brief on screen, visuals for the hook: quick, shocking visuals such as microplastics in water, landfill overflowing with coffee cups, coffee mugs breaking, and a close-up of someone drinking from a stained, cracked mug.
- Brief on screen: captions on some parts of the script to illustrate figures and make it engaging (the brief says "as seen in example").
- Brief on screen, hold visuals: relaxing, slow-paced vibes, with captions on some parts of the script (e.g. "World's First Shatterproof Ceramic Cup").
- Sticky-note shot ideas:
  - Someone drinking or preparing coffee in a plastic cup.
  - A quick match-cut scene of a bunch of coffee shop videos ("like 4-5, make it fast"), maybe one showing someone grabbing a plastic cup.
  - Falling coins / scattered coins / penny money.
  - A production line making cups.
  - An "oddly satisfying clip" (ferromagnetic fluid).
  - A barista giving coffee to the person, at the end of the video.
- Footage sources for the product part: the client's folder (huge, many subfolders, "sniff through that") and the brand's Instagram, including its reels and the Tagged tab. Clips can be downloaded and used.
- Voiceover: the board has ElevenLabs/AI voice audio files per hook, hold and CTA (e.g. "Jessica AI Voice Jan 16 2025"). Voiceover is therefore pre-supplied in the brief.
- The instructor says these VSL ads will be more time-consuming.
