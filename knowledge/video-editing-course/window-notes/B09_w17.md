## Concrete rules and numbers
- Add a whoosh or transition sound where a cut feels like "something is missing". It "can make things a whole lot better".
- Use SFX to bring the audience's attention to key moments, and pick sounds with a build-up (a riser leading into the cut).
- Build an SFX library so that "once you tackle things in layer", the work is quicker next time. This is the "make your life easier" point.
- Before export, go through the video and look for errors and glitches.
- Check the work both before and after export.
- Reason given: skipping checks sends errors to the client, which looks bad because the errors were avoidable. Checking trains your eye to catch mistakes.
- The instructor gives no dB, duration or timing numbers in this window.
