## What is taught
This is a walkthrough of a UGC ad brief board (Milanote), not an editing demo. The instructor explains the assignment structure: 3 hooks plus one shared hold, built from the creator's talking-head takes and the same creator's b-roll. He deliberately gives no example edit so students can try it themselves. He suggests editing first, then taking the hooks, retention and live UGC breakdown modules, then coming back to compare and improve.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Ad structure: 3 hooks and 1 hold. Each variation uses a different hook on the same hold. The board states "3 hooks, 1 hold, 3 total ads" (small text, partly legible).
- The hold is a story script made of numbered lines, in order: first, second, third, fourth, fifth and so on. Arrows between clips show the order.
- The footage needs polishing: cut out the bad takes and trim to the script.
- The concept is the "salon quality hair" angle for a hair dryer product. Talking points are salon quality at home, and other dryers burn your hair while this one doesn't.
- A proper UGC concept means the same creator both delivers the script (talking to camera) and shoots the b-roll of herself using the product. The talking-head and b-roll footage match.
- No numbers on durations, cut rate, zoom, captions or audio are given in this window.
