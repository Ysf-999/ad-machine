## Concrete rules and numbers
- **Fit music to the video length with Remix.**
  - Select the music clip, open Essential Sound > Edit > tag as Music > Duration > Method "Remix".
  - Drag the end of the audio clip back (or out) to the desired length. Premiere analyses the clip ("Clip Analysis" progress) and remixes it to that target duration.
  - Choose the end point so the music's natural ending (where the waveform drops) lands on the call-to-action section. He first extended it too far ("that's too much"), then pulled it back.
  - Under Customize, the Segments and Variations sliders adjust the remix. Both show 5.0 on screen. Slider ends are labelled Fewer/More (segments) and Melodic/Harmonic (variations). He says to play around to taste.
  - The panel showed a target duration of about 00:00:37:26 and later 00:00:33:09. The remix duration shown was slightly different from the target (e.g. 00:00:40:11, then 00:00:35:17).
- **Ducking** is available in the same panel but is off in the screenshots.
- **B-roll workflow for a big VSL project:**
  - Use the b-roll plan made in the planning stage. He lost his plan but says planning still helped.
  - For this ad he found clips of the events and the athletes through Google search and YouTube links.
- **Google "Short videos" trick:** search the topic (example: "cordyceps mushroom"), then click the "Short videos" tab.
  - It aggregates short-form video from Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest and Shutterstock.
  - He suggests using it as the primary way of searching, because it saves time finding specific clips.
- **Upscale low-quality downloads.** YouTube downloads can be poor quality. If the clip is perfect but low-res, upscale it with Topaz Video AI (paid, "incredibly powerful").
