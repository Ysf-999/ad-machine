## Concrete rules and numbers
- Goal: AI voiceover so natural that listeners don't clock it as AI. It should not sound like the generic "TikTok sound".
- Proof claim: the example ad is "on pace to become a top spending ad in account". It used only AI voiceover and product B-roll, with no creator footage recorded for the voice.
- Reasons to use AI VO:
  - No waiting for creators to record the script.
  - No mispronounced words.
  - It is fast, easy and effective "if you know how to do it right".
- Example ad length is 0:41 (QuickTime scrubber reads 00:41).
- Example script structure (about 41 s, conversational UGC tone):
  - Hook: "If you have a humidifier at home, let me show you something that actually blew my mind."
  - Problem: humidifiers get gross and can't be cleaned properly.
  - Solution: stainless steel.
  - Skeptic beat: "At first, I was like, okay, but why though?"
  - Proof/mechanism: you can sterilize it, and it comes apart with no weird corners.
  - Credibility: invented by a doctor.
  - Social proof: after a month it's still clean.
  - Benefit: no filters to buy.
  - Personal use case: baby girl's nursery.
  - Close: "I honestly wish someone told me about this sooner."
- The script uses short sentences, casual filler ("like", "look,", "literally") and a first-person story voice.
