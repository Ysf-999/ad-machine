## What is taught
The instructor walks through the b-roll choices in a VSL ad, line by line. Each script phrase gets a stock or abstract clip that matches the idea, not necessarily the literal words. He also shows two effects: Mosaic as a pixelation transition effect, and Invert to mark a "bad thing" moment.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Mosaic effect: horizontal blocks 75 and vertical blocks 75. He says "play around with the number" to get the look you want. The pixelation sits on the "Cordyceps." reveal.
- Invert effect: applied to a high-contrast clip of a pill being destroyed. It is used "to emphasize that this is a bad thing."
- B-roll is matched to each script phrase, with new clips changing roughly every phrase or two:
  - "reveal the secret, cordyceps": a few cordyceps clips.
  - "rare fungus found in the high mountains of Tibet": cinematic drone shot of Tibetan mountains (found on TikTok).
  - "here's what they discovered that no one else knew": close-up of cordyceps mushrooms.
  - "increases ATP production": abstract animation of something coming to life, with energy increasing as the clip progresses.
  - "boosts your energy on a cellular level": clip resembling cells in a high-energy state.
  - "VO2 max": abstract clip that resembles a lung.
  - "body's ability to use oxygen during exercise": man running. He picked running over skiing or other exercise because it fits the ad's idea and audience (runners/athletes).
  - "problem with traditional mushroom supplements": supplement pills, then cordyceps mushrooms.
  - "up to 90% of those supplements gets destroyed in the digestive system": pill being digested, then the inverted high-contrast clip.
  - "before reaching a bloodstream": clip that resembles blood flow.
- Stock footage does not have to match the script exactly. Abstract visuals that evoke the idea are fine.
