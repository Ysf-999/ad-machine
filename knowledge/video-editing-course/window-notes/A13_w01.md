## What is taught
This window covers local asset management for client content. Download new content (creator uploads, links shared in Slack/Discord) immediately and store it in a client folder on your own drive, so it isn't forgotten when the next ads are made. Sub-organise inside the client folder by creator and by purpose (e.g. a hooks folder), and keep it clean so the team can quickly see what content exists for an idea.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- No numeric settings (durations, zoom, dB, etc.) are given in this window.
- Download and save new content the moment you come across it. Do not leave it as a link in Slack or Discord. The instructor has forgotten client-sent content that way and later didn't remember it existed.
- Store it locally (SSD, Mac, PC). Seeing it later while browsing folders prompts "this is new, we should use this".
- Add subfolders per content creator inside the UGC content folder. Example: a "Sarah" folder for a creator who records for the brand every month.
- Reason to segment by creator: ads from a specific creator can perform better, so you will want to reuse that creator's content more.
- For visual products (his example is a skincare cream), keep a "hooks" folder of scroll-stopping, interesting visuals to use as hooks.
- Keep the structure clean and organised, because it saves time when making ads.
- A tidy library makes you valuable to the team. When the team brainstorms an idea, you can tell quickly whether you already have content for it (the sentence is cut off at 05:55).
