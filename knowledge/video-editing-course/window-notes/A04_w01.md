## What is taught
This window is course logistics, not editing technique. The instructor explains that the action-item ads give students portfolio pieces and experience to cite when pitching clients. He then walks through the organic briefs in a Milanote "Ad Briefs" board. The first brief is "Organic AD#1 – Mom IG Reel", an organic, mom-influencer-style Instagram reel. The brief structure is 3 hook texts plus 1 shared hold, producing 3 ad variations.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Each ad concept is delivered as 3 variations. The only difference between variations is the hook text.
- The hold (the rest of the video) is identical across all variations.
- Brief for Organic AD#1: 3 hooks, 1 hold, 3 total ads.
- Brief checkboxes: type of ad = Video Ad. Size needed = 1080x1920 (9:16). Other sizes are listed but unchecked: 1080x1080 (1:1), 1080x1350 (4:5), 1920x1080 (16:9).
- Ideation concept for AD#1: "caption/music/no VO style that focuses on how the baby loves to use the pillow." So there is no voiceover, only on-screen captions plus music.
- Hook copy (Peekaboo Pillow):
  - Hook 1: "My baby's favorite ways to use Peekaboo Pillow"
  - Hook 2: "4 ways baby loves her Peekaboo Pillow"
  - Hook 3: "My baby's favorite pillow is a must have for new parents"
- Hold copy, as a bulleted list of the beats shown in the hold: Tummy time, Nursing, Bonding face to face, Hanging with big sis!
- Organic ad #2 is "Comment Reply". It also uses 3 hooks, 1 hold and 3 total ads, and the instructor says it is "pretty much the same thing".
- The instructor calls these the easiest video ads among the action items.
