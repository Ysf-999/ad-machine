## What is taught
The instructor shows how to shape AI voiceover delivery in ElevenLabs Studio by editing the script's punctuation and then regenerating individual lines. Punctuation controls emphasis and flow. Exclamation marks add emphasis, and commas, dashes and caps join list items so they don't sound disconnected. Expect to regenerate the same line several times until it sounds right, because a flat, emotionless read sounds like AI.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Add an exclamation mark at the end of a sentence to emphasize it. He changed "Carepod is 99% sterilizable." to "...sterilizable!"
- Merge related lines into the same paragraph/section so they are generated together. He moved "You can actually boil it to ensure it's completely clean." up into the same block as the sterilizable line.
- Periods between list items make the read disconnected. Original: "No endless filter replacements. No hidden mold. Exactly what I've been searching for." Each period ends the phrase and the delivery drops.
- Fix for listing lines:
  - Use a comma between list items: "No endless filter replacements, no hidden mold".
  - Use a dash before the payoff phrase: "- EXACTLY what I've been searching for!"
  - Capitalize the word to stress (EXACTLY) and end with an exclamation mark.
  - Highlight/select just that line, then hit Regenerate.
- Regenerate only the selected line ("Regenerate selection"). He did about 4–5 takes of this one line:
  - Take 1: still disliked.
  - Take 2: "feels weird".
  - Take 3: "she just emphasizes this too much".
  - Take 4: disliked.
  - Take 5: "a bit better", accepted.
- Accept a "good enough" take rather than chasing perfection.
- Goal: the voice must not sound emotionless, because "if it doesn't have any emotion in it, it just sounds like AI."
- Final text shown for the list line is all lowercase with a comma before "exactly" and an exclamation mark. The caps/dash version was tried and swapped for this one in later takes.
- Credit counter in the UI: about 195,190 remaining, dropping to about 195,019 after the regenerations. This is roughly 85–170 credits per short line regenerate, so regenerating is cheap.
