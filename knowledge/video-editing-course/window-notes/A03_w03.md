## What is taught
The instructor walks through "good" safe-zone examples in Final Cut Pro, with a green overlay marking the safe area on a 9:16 timeline. Text and the product must sit inside the safe zone so Reels cropping doesn't cut them off. He then shows a 4:5 (1080×1350) ad, which has no safe-zone constraints. He previews it in Meta Ads Manager to show that it looks fine in feed but is cropped on Reels and Stories.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Reels crops the sides of an ad slightly, so keep text away from the left and right edges.
- Small overshoot of the safe-zone edge on the left and right is tolerable, but nothing should ever sit outside the safe zones.
- Captions must be inside the safe zone.
- The product must be visible inside the safe zone throughout the ad. His examples were a cleaning action and the humidifier on a stand.
- Text at the bottom must also stay inside the safe zone.
- Meta (Facebook/Instagram) video ads are either 9:16 or 4:5.
- 4:5 = 1080×1350.
- For horizontal footage, don't stretch it to vertical, because too much gets cropped. Export it as 4:5 instead.
- 4:5 videos have no safe zones, so you can place things anywhere.
- 4:5 looks exactly as designed in feed. On Reels and Stories it is cropped to fill the screen, and the leftover space is filled with colour or UI buttons.
- Optimising within safe zones is what makes an ad "fit all placements".
