## What is taught
The instructor builds hook variants H2 and H3 of a UGC-style ad ("Watch me outsmart my dog") in Premiere Pro. For each variant he duplicates the finished H1 sequence, renames it, and swaps only the hook voiceover and hook b-roll. Everything after the hook (the HOLD section and the rest of the ad) stays the same. He follows the script/brief (Milanote board) for the copy and visual notes, for example swapping "himself" to "herself" when the dog is clearly female.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Variant workflow: duplicate the sequence (h1 → h2 → h3), rename it, replace the hook VO clip and hook visual, and leave the rest of the ad untouched.
- Copy the text/caption attributes onto the new hook line ("copy and apply the attributes"), then adjust the text to the new hook.
- Hook copy per the brief:
  - H2: "Watch me outsmart my dog". Visuals: "same idea as V1 but diff clip".
  - H3: "Watch my dog train himself". Visuals: "same idea as V2 – if the dog is wearing pink/has feminine name visible – please swap to 'herself'".
  - HOLD 1: "Your dog's body is built for pulling and that back clip harness is making it worse". It uses AI VO (female, generic).
- Gender check: a dog with a purple/pink harness and a feminine name visible means "herself", so H3 became "Watch my dog train herself".
- Hook visual trimming: the hook clip is trimmed or slipped so its length matches the VO/script. He picks a "really powerful clip" and adjusts its length to match.
- Hook clip length is short, about 1:12–1:19 (frame timecodes) for the VO. The hook VO files are 1:14 (h2) and 1:19–1:20 (h3). The h1 hook VO is 2:05.
- Hook text and b-roll change at about the 1:15 mark, where "Your dog's body is built for pulling" starts.
- The hook visual should be strong. Quote: "it's not really good in terms of visuals maybe we could find even better". He keeps looking for a better clip until he is satisfied.
- Hook VO file naming: h1 hook dogs.mp3, h2 hook dogs.mp3, h3 hook dogs.mp3, plus hold dogs.mp3 and cta.mp3. ElevenLabs downloads are saved into the batch's VO folder as h1.mp3, h2.mp3, h3.mp3, hold 1.mp3, hold 2.mp3.
- AI voiceover settings on screen (ElevenLabs):

  - Model: Eleven Multilingual v2.
  - Sliders (approximate): speed mid, stability ~60%, similarity ~65%, style exaggeration ~10%.
  - Speaker boost on.
  - The H3 line is 26 characters.
- Hook text style (Essential Graphics panel): font HeadingNow, 65 Norm Medium, size 57, white text with a dark/black fill and an outer stroke (stroke value not legible). The caption is a white-on-black box style, centred, and sits at the top-middle of the frame at the start. At 66:45 it sits lower (~y 408 in the preview).
