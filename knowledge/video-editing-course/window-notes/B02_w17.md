## What is taught
The instructor wraps up the live breakdown of a UGC-style ad. Captions should copy the native look of TikTok/Instagram captions so the ad feels organic. He also recaps the order of work: build in layers (A-roll or voiceover first, then music, then b-roll, then enhancements). For this organic UGC ad he deliberately added no effects, animations, or transitions.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Check how captions look on TikTok or Instagram. Write a caption in an Instagram story, screenshot it, and replicate it in the editing software, so it is as similar and as organic as possible.
- Goal: make UGC ads seem as authentic and organic as possible. Captions that look exactly like ones made on the platform are "a huge plus".
- This ad uses standard TikTok-style captions, always in the same place, "nothing crazy".
- Layer order for editing:
  1. A-roll (sometimes there is only a script or voiceover instead).
  2. Music.
  3. B-roll.
  4. Last: transitions, animations, sound effects, and captions.
- For an organic UGC ad, add no crazy effects, animations, or transitions, so it reads as authentic.
- Caption style visible in the Final Cut inspector: Proxima Nova, Semibold, size 28.0, centered, two short lines per card. Outline is on, black, width 3.0, opacity 100%. Line spacing 0, tracking 0%.
