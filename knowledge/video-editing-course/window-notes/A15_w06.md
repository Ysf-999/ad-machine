## Concrete rules and numbers
- Priority order for footage: brand footage first, "as much footage from your brand… as possible".
- Use stock or other footage when not showing the product, e.g. when "talking about some other solutions" or something else, to show that part of the ad creatively.
- Footage workflow summary: scrape social media first, then use stock resources (Pexels, Artlist, Envato) to support the ad.
- Artlist is mainly for music and SFX, but it recently added stock footage, which he rates as high quality. It also has a "Vertical Only" filter, useful for 9:16.
- Music research method:
  1. Search the brand's niche on TikTok, e.g. "jewelry" for a jewelry brand.
  2. Open several videos and listen to which music and sounds are trending in that niche.
  3. TikTok may not allow direct music download, so identify the song (title and artist) and search for it on YouTube.
- Artlist demo: search "coffee" with the Footage tab, filtered to Vertical Only. The example clip showing duration was 00:11.
- Pexels is described as free stock footage. The Artlist templates section has no templates comparable to Envato's, but its stock footage is useful.
