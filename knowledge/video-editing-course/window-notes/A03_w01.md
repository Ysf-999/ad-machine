## What is taught
The same 9:16 ad file is cropped differently across Meta placements: FB feed, Instagram Stories, Reels and Posts. The fix is a "Safe Zone Guidelines" overlay (a green PNG) that the editor lays over the video while editing. All text, captions and product shots must sit inside the green area, which accounts for the Reels/Stories UI buttons. The instructor then starts demoing it in Final Cut Pro with bad examples.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- No numeric margins are given in this window. The rule is qualitative: everything not green is a no-no.
- All text, captions and product shots must be visible inside the safe zone.
- The video itself stays full frame (1080x1920, 30 fps in the Final Cut project). Only the important elements must be inside the zone.
- Avoid the right-side button column (like, comment, share, save) and the bottom area (caption/CTA/"Learn more" UI). Placing things there is a huge issue.
- Slight spill past the left or right edges of the safe zone is tolerated ("not a huge issue").
- The same file gets cut on top and bottom in Stories and cropped in Posts. It shows full screen in Reels and in the FB feed's own frame.
- Workflow: in Final Cut, use View → Custom Overlay to load the safe-zone image. It can be toggled off at any time. He says other editors can probably do the same.
