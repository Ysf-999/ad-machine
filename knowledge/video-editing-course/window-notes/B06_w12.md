## What is taught
The instructor finishes positioning the burned-in caption text inside the safe zone. He moves it with the X/Y position values and shrinks the font because it looks too big. He then cleans up the caption text in Premiere's Captions/Text panel. He checks wording against the brief's script and by ear, and splits long caption cards so they are short enough to read.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Position values: the first is X (horizontal), the second is Y (vertical). The on-screen Y value goes from about -335 to about -364 as the caption is nudged down.
- Reduce the caption font size if it looks too big. On screen the Font Size value is 40 and the style is Proxima Nova Semibold.
- Keep a little breathing space between the caption and the edge of the safe zone, so nothing gets cropped.
- Review the captions card by card in the Captions panel. Repeat the check "until you make sure that everything is right."
- Verify uncertain words or spellings against the script in the brief.
- UGC creators don't follow the script word for word and add fillers like "well," "that" and "this." Double-check by ear and cut the fillers.
- If a caption card carries too much text ("most people might not read it"), split it with the cut tool into shorter cards.
- Worked example: "Can we stop with the getting my body back narrative for a second?" was split into two cards, "Can we stop with the" and "the getting my body back narrative for a second?" The cut was made after the first "Can we stop with the," the rest stayed as the second card, and the repeated words were removed.
