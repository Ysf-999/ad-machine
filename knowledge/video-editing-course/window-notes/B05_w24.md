- Purpose of the effect: it pulls the viewer's attention to the right side of the frame.
- Workflow for hook variations: copy the finished clip, select the clips in the other hook sequences, and use Paste Attributes. This applies the effect to hooks h2 and h3 as well.
- Final QA step: watch the whole ad again and look for flaws to fix or improve before publishing.
- Hook variations are separate sequences (h1, h2, h3) of the same ad. The full ad runs about 40 s (h1 is 00:00:40:11, h3 is 00:00:39:29).
- Script of the finished ad:

  - Solution: move the clip to the front, so pulling force works against the dog.
