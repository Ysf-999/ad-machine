## What is taught
The instructor breaks down an organic ad made for Dog Friendly Co (a premium dog brand), focused on the dog harness with a no-pull front D-ring add-on. He plays the finished ad, then opens the brief and the inspiration ad, a "I made a huge mistake buying the Teabox chai sampler" video. The brief's idea is reframing positives as negatives. ## Concrete rules and numbers
- Target audience: most ad spend goes to women aged about 25 to their 60s. Keep this in mind when reading the brief.
- Ad concept: "Negative framing" reframes positives as regrets, e.g. "Three reasons I regret getting this for my dog."
  - The stopped pulling becomes "no more arm workout."
  - Everyone asking about it becomes "I have to stop every two minutes."
  - The lifetime warranty becomes "zero excuse to buy a new one."
- Structure: 3 hook variations + 1 hold + 1 CTA. The brief says "2 total ads" and "3 hooks, 1 hold" (the text is small, so the exact count is partly unclear).
- Hook 1: "3 reasons I regret getting this for my dog." Visuals: putting the harness on the dog, easy on, 1–2 seconds.
- Hook 2: "New dog owners – don't make the same mistake as me! Let me show you why I regret buying this harness…" Visuals: same idea as V1 but a different clip.
- Hook 3: "I made a huge mistake buying one of these for my dog, let me show you why." Visuals: aesthetic close-ups of the product on the dog.
- Hold content: the 3 reasons, then "these are the kind of problems I can live with", the front D-ring/control line, the shock-absorption leash line, and "if your dog's a puller, this is your sign to upgrade their gear".
- CTA: "Join over 400,000 happy dog parents today."
- Caption style in the finished ad: 2 lines, centred, roughly mid-lower frame, short phrases of about 4–6 words each (e.g. "My dog suddenly stopped / yanking on the lead,"). White text, small, no visible box.
- Caption style in the inspo ad: white rounded-box background with black text, 2–4 words per card ("buying the Teabox", "made with real spices", "if you still wanna").
- The instructor says to take your time and understand the brief whenever AI voiceover is required. The sentence is cut off at 03:00.
