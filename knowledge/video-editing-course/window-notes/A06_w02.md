## What is taught
This window finishes the brief for UGC Ad #3, a mock assessment for a shilajit brand ("Nurojit" in the brief). ## Concrete rules and numbers
- Dr. Squatch-style editing: new visual about every 2 seconds, "very engaging, very eye catching", tactical visualization of what the script says.
- Study Dr. Squatch ads, for example on Atria. Clients like editors who can do this style, and Dr. Squatch is popular in e-commerce.
- The brief's variations: 3 hooks + 1 hold = 3 total ads. Each of the 3 voiceover hooks is combined with the one hold.
- Each hook and the hold come with a ready voiceover mp3 (Hook1, Hook2, Hook3, Hold1). The voiceover is the base track.
- The workflow is to lay the voiceover down, then add lots of b-roll on top, animate things, and visualize the script.
- Footage comes from many Google Drive folders (about 20 links). Using all of it is not required: pick the best clips from a great variety, use as much as possible to keep the ad engaging, and come up with creative hooks from the content.
- Concept per the brief: an AI VO video, a mini VSL about the brand being the only shilajit brand that posts third-party lab results. The lab results from their website must be shown, for example as a screen recording.
- Editing guidelines on the board: "go as crazy as possible with the editing (a bunch of b-roll, make it quick and punchy)" to replicate Dr. Squatch's style.
- This is the third of three UGC briefs, and the editor sends each one back for feedback.
