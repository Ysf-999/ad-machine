## Concrete rules and numbers
- Gigapixel AI is desktop software for Mac or PC. You import the image rather than upload it, pick a model, and apply.
- Gigapixel models shown: Standard, Lines, Art & CG, Low Resolution, Very Compressed.
- The auto-model toggle lets the AI choose the model. The instructor says to sometimes pick manually for the best result.
- The Low Resolution model on the test image gave a "decent" result. The only flaw he noticed was the Apple logo; the rest looked clean.
- Face Recovery is available for faces and was on, with strength 49.
- Gigapixel scale options: 0.5x, 2x, 4x, 6x, custom.
- Video input in the example was 360x640 at 30 FPS. The instructor reads it as "360 times 740" and calls it unusable on social media today.
- Video output resolution can be 2x or 4x. He chose 4x, which gives 1440x2560.
- Topaz Video AI options:
  - Stabilization, for shaky footage.
  - Motion deblur.
  - Frame interpolation, described as "just slowing it down". It turns a regular shot into slow motion, and you choose how much to slow it.
  - Enhancement, where you choose the AI model.
- He uses the basic enhancement model with Parameters set to Auto ("these are the settings I use") and then clicks export.
- A 3–4 second clip should export in a couple of seconds.
- He did not use stabilization, motion blur, or slow motion in this example.
