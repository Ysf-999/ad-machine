## What is taught
The instructor walks through the briefs for the second and third organic-ad assignments in a Milanote board. Organic Ad #2 is a "comment reply" ad for gym-bro content. Organic Ad #3 is a "haircare routine" ad where a creator's own footage is narrated only through captions over trendy music. Both should look like native social content rather than ads. The window ends with submission instructions.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Organic Ad #2 (Comment Reply):
  - The hook is a TikTok-style comment reply. Fake comments are generated on tokcomment.com (the brief links it).
  - It should look like content gym bros make and watch. Use trendy gym-bro music.
  - 3 variations. The only difference between them is the comment reply used in the hook.
  - The brief's variation counts are 3 hooks, 1 hold, 3 total ads.
  - Brief text: the footage is limited. The gym bro does only about 3 moves (glute bridge, pike-ups, lateral lunge), so it is a quick video. Start the ad with him walking toward the camera to show off the physique.
  - Sample hook copy is Q&A pairs: "Bro how if do I get shredded as you?" / "Got you 👇"; "Dude, what is the secret to getting cut like that?" / "This."; "Advice for leveling up my core training?" / "Add instability. Get yourself a slide board 🔥".
- Organic Ad #3 (Haircare Routine):
  - It should look like organic social content. A creator recorded herself and "tells the story" only through captions, with trendy music, about her experience with the product.
  - 3 hook variations, 1 hold, 3 total ads. Size needed is 1080x1920 (9:16), the only size ticked in the brief.
  - Hook visuals: show the final result in the hook (she shows off her blowout at the start and the end of the original video).
  - The hold is a sequence of captions, Caption 1 to Caption 9, which is the copy for the rest of the ad.
  - Brief notes: there is enough footage for the length of the "script", so match shots to captions. For music use "viral girly sound/music", and the brief gives YouTube inspiration links.
- Music recommendations are optional ("you can or don't have to use").
- Delivery: do one ad or all three. Sending all three lets the instructor review them together. Upload to Google Drive and send the link, or send directly on Discord.
- Instructor: "shouldn't take you a whole lot of time", "no need to overcomplicate".
