## What is taught
The instructor is in After Effects, polishing a motion-tracked red arrow that points at a dog's collar. He adds a drop shadow to give it depth, then repositions the whole tracked path with the position keyframes. He rotates the arrow and fades it in with opacity. Finally he starts a CC Spotlight effect on the footage, which has to follow the tracker.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Drop shadow on the arrow layer, for a polished, slightly 3D look:
  - Opacity 75%.
  - Distance 20. He tries 15 and says "let's keep with 20".
  - Softness (the "substance" in the transcript) 30, then 50. The final screen shows Softness 50.
  - Direction stays at the default 135°.
  - The shadow colour looks black.
- The pasted tracking data makes the arrow stick to the dog's collar, which "looks really awful". To offset it:
  - Select the arrow layer and press P to show Position.
  - Click the word "Position" to select every keyframe at once.
  - Drag the arrow in the viewer. The whole path shifts together, so the offset applies to every frame.
- Target placement: the arrow points in from above the collar, then moves slightly below it while rotating, with the tip kept on the collar.
- Rotation is keyframed. The frame at 50:00 shows a rotation of about 33° near 0:00:18, with keyframes at the start and mid-clip.
- Fade-in: animate Opacity, which makes it "look a bit smoother". Opacity runs 0% to 100% over roughly the first 0:00:08 to 0:00:09 (two keyframes close together at the start).
- Scale of the arrow layer is 13% (the arrow.png source is 2000×1200).
- Spotlight, to be applied to the video layer:
  - CC Spotlight with From 972,192, To 540,960, Height 50, Cone Angle 10, Edge Softness 25%, Intensity 75, Color white, Render "Light Shadow".
  - It darkens the whole frame except an oval of light.
  - To make it follow the dog, parent its From/To to the tracker's feature or attach point. The instructor trails off before finishing ("I don't know what even").
