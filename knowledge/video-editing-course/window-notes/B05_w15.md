## What is taught
The instructor shows how to make a "red arrow effect" in After Effects: a red arrow PNG whose pointer tip stays pinned to a colour spot (the harness clip on the dog) in the footage. The steps are to create a comp with the clip and the arrow, track a high-contrast point with the Tracker panel's Track Motion, then apply that track's X/Y position to the arrow layer. The window starts with the arrow's placement on the back-clip area, which was set in Premiere.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Name the AE project after the effect, e.g. "red arrow effect".
- Source of the arrow: any red arrow PNG found by searching "red arrow png". The screen shows it is 2000x1200 px.
- The arrow's tip (pointer side) must sit on the target colour or spot. Move the layer's anchor point to the tip, and shrink the arrow first.
  - Screen: arrow scale about 51% (51.0, 51.0%).
  - Screen: anchor point is set to about 1000, 600 and position 540, 960 in a 1080x1920 comp.
- Tracking steps:
  1. Disable or hide the arrow layer during tracking.
  2. Select the video layer, then open the Tracker panel and click Track Motion.
  3. Place the track point on a feature that is vivid or noticeable to the algorithm. Here it is a dot or spot on the dog's harness or collar.
  4. Press Analyze Forward (the play button).
  5. Watch the result. Tracking may not be perfect, and the track point can drift on some frames.
  6. When it finishes, click Edit Target and choose the arrow PNG layer. Click OK.
  7. Click Apply, set Apply Dimensions to X and Y, and click OK.
  8. Enable the arrow layer. It now follows the tracked point.
- The tracked clip on screen is about 2:18 s long (the clip in the comp runs about 0:00–2:17 at 30 fps).
