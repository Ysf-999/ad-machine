## What is taught
This is the intro to the safe-zone lesson. The instructor says safe zones are a big driver of ad performance and that many brands skip them. One ad can be served in Feed, Stories and Reels placements, and each crops or overlays the frame differently. He teases a way to optimize for every placement without exporting two versions (9:16 plus a 4:5 duplicate). The method itself is not shown in this window.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Meta (Facebook/Instagram) can place one ad in several placements: Feed, Stories, and Reels (Facebook and Instagram). All of them look different.
- In Feed the ad gets cropped.
- The naive workaround is to export a 9:16 version, duplicate it, and re-optimize it for 4:5 (1080×1350). He calls this unnecessary, because every ad gets exported twice.
- The goal is a single export that is safe in all placements. The technique is promised later and is not given in this window.
- Optimizing for safe zones puts you "a step ahead of everyone else", because many ads in the Ad Library and Atria ignore them.
