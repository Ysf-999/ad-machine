## What is taught
This window is the brief for a live-edit example, not editing technique. The instructor walks through the brand (Stoke/STTOKE coffee cups), its product page, and the ad angle: "your last sip feels like your first." He shows the creator-testimonial script with three hooks. The editing itself starts after 09:00.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Product positioning from the brand site:
  - The cups look aesthetic.
  - They keep coffee hot longer, which addresses the pain point of coffee going cold quickly.
  - They have a ceramic interior.
  - They are billed as the "world's first shatterproof ceramic cup."
- Product-page claims, read on screen and aloud:
  - Keeps hot for 6 hours.
  - Keeps cold for 8 hours.
  - Two-time award-winning design and ergonomics.
  - 100% leakproof screw-on lid.
  - Price shown: $71.92 USD, down from $109.90.
- Ad angle: Stoke cups make the last sip feel like the first sip.
  - The first sip is warm and exciting. By the last sip it's cold and disgusting.
- Format: a creator records a testimonial about how Stoke helped, and the editor turns that raw footage into an ad.
- Three hook scripts (spoken copy):
  1. "You know that feeling when you take the last sip of your coffee and it's ice cold? That hasn't happened to me in 6 months…"
  2. "I used to hate taking the last sip of my morning coffee… until I discovered why it kept getting cold…"
  3. "Want to know how I stopped throwing away half of my morning coffee every day?"
- Hook purpose, per the instructor: make people realize their coffee gets cold by the time they're meant to finish it, and that it doesn't have to.
- Optional b-roll suggestions listed under each hook:
  - Hook 1:
    - Close-up of someone taking the final sip, grimacing in disappointment.
    - Quick sequence of steam disappearing from coffee over time.
    - Person picking up the cup, realizing it's cold, shoulders dropping.
    - Split screen of past (cold coffee) vs present (steaming coffee).
  - Hook 2:
    - Close-up of a disappointed expression after taking a sip.
    - Quick montage of common coffee mugs showing temperature loss.
    - Time-lapse of coffee going cold in a regular mug.
  - Hook 3:
    - Slow-motion shot of coffee being poured down the sink.
    - Quick cuts of half-full coffee cups being thrown away.
    - Person looking frustrated at a coffee cup.
    - Money being spent at a coffee shop counter.
