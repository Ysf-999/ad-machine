## What is taught
The instructor shows how he built the hook of a "last sip of cold coffee" ad. He wanted viewers to feel understood, so he searched TikTok for a funny reaction clip ("When coffee gets cold.."), then used it as a green-screen background behind the talent. He says to use safe zones while editing, and to cover the rest of the video the same way: product b-roll when the speaker mentions the product, and visuals for the story build-up.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Hook idea: visualise a relatable emotion, here the last sip of cold coffee, so the viewer feels understood.
- Sourcing: search TikTok with several keyword variants, such as "cold coffee", "when your coffee gets cold" and "cold coffee feeling". He found the clip quickly and easily this way.
- The hook clip is a ~7 s TikTok reaction clip with on-screen copy "When coffee gets cold..". It was downloaded with a TikTok downloader (SnapTik) and previewed in QuickTime.
- Hook structure: the reaction clip sits in the background and the talent is keyed over it with a green screen. The talent says: "You know that feeling when you take the last sip of your coffee and it's ice cold? That hasn't happened to me in six months."
- Hook caption/text must sit inside the safe zones. He checks this with a custom safe-zone overlay at 50% opacity, and says the text "fits perfectly".
- For the rest of the video, match visuals to what is said:
  - When she talks about the product, show product b-roll. Example: "this cup was literally invented by coffee lovers" is paired with a shot of the cup.
  - For story build-up, use matching story visuals (the sentence is cut off).
- Audio: the downloaded TikTok clip's audio is set to -∞ dB (muted), so only the talent's voice is heard in the hook.
