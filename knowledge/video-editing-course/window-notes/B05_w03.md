## Concrete rules and numbers

- Rephrase the search when results are poor. "dog pull" became "leash pulling", then "lead pulling" ("lead" is the UK term), because different wording surfaces different clips.
- Pick clips where the subject is clearly visible, such as a dog's back or rear while it walks and pulls. He says "more visible back clip" and picks the one that shows the pulling clearly.
- Download workflow: open the TikTok video, copy its link into TikDownloader (tikdownloader.io), and click a download button. The buttons are MP4 [1], MP4 [2], MP4 HD and MP3.
- File naming is sequential: "dog pulling", "dog pulling 2", "dog pulling 3", "dog pulling 4", "lead pulling". Saving under an existing name triggers a "replace?" prompt, which he answers "No" and renames instead.
- Save everything to the project subfolder `…/BATCH#95 – Watch How I Outsmart My Dog/stock`. Sibling folders are mid, refs and final.
