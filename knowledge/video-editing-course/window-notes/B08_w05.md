## What is taught
The editor finishes a Premiere breakdown of an organic-style Dog Friendly Co. ad. He shows a slow keyframed push-in on a clip where the subject walks away. He then walks through how each remaining b-roll clip was matched to its on-screen text line (features, fit, quality, ideal outcome, CTA). He then starts on the hook variants, again matching clip to text so it grabs attention and shows the pain point.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Subject walking away from camera: start at a higher scale and scale down. Start scale is about 80, with a position keyframe added so the subject stays centred.
- At the end of the clip, scale returns to 50 (the default 100%-fit value in this project). Position resets to the centre (540, 960 on a 1080x1920 frame).
- Scale 80 to 50 gives a slow "pull-out" so the subject doesn't just walk away. His verdict: "hardly enough that it's not distracting but it's something better than just having them walk away."
- Experiment when you have time, since you learn more by doing than from tutorials.
- Clip-to-text matching used in the body:
  - "Dog Friendly Co. harness with Front + Back clips": show the back clip, then the front clip.
  - "Perfect fit GUARANTEED": a clean shot of the harness fitting a dog perfectly.
  - "Premium materials. Last YEARS, not months": close-up, aesthetic quality shots of the material.
  - "Stop fighting physics START USING IT": the ideal outcome, a peaceful walk with no pulling and a relaxed owner.
  - CTA ("Join 300,000+ happy dog parents / Shop @dogfriendlyco"): a happy, beautiful environment with owner and dog having a good time. End on a positive note that matches where the buyer hopes to end up.
- Hook 1, "THIS IS CHOKING YOUR DOG...": a clip of a dog pulling its owner (the owner in all white, on mud). The clip's purpose is to grab attention and show the pain point at the same time.
- Hook line 2, "AND THIS GETS YOUR DOG TO STOP PULLING.": b-roll of the owner attaching the front D-ring.
