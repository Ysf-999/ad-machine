## What is taught
This window is the outro of the "organic ads" lesson. The instructor says strategy matters more than editing skill: knowing how to edit is worthless if you don't know what to put in the video. He assigns students to make an ad from the organic ad brief in any editing software, without worrying about technique. Submissions will be judged on audience understanding and on whether the ad feels native to the platform, not on polish.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- No numbers, durations, zoom values, caption specs or audio levels are given in this window.
- Judging criteria for the organic ad assignment:
  - Show you understand who you are talking to, who you are selling to, and who the ad is for.
  - Make the ad feel native to the platform, because "that's what organic ads are".
  - Not judged: polish, transitions, fancy animations or effects.
- Pick any editor (Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, CapCut). The tool doesn't matter. Learn the technical side by making ads.
- Next lesson: UGC ads. He says they are meaningfully different from organic ads.
