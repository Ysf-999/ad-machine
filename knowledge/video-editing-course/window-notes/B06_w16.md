## Concrete rules and numbers
- SFX level: a "Hollow Pop 06.wav" sound effect has Reverb (Room Reverb preset) added under Essential Sound > Creative. The reverb amount reads about 2.5 and the instructor says to decrease it a bit. He first says "to maybe half of it", then "let's decrease it a tad bit". The audio line being tuned is "Your prolactin levels stay elevated".
- Before exporting, set source in / source out:
  - Go to the end of the video and step back two frames.
  - Set the out point there. The exported range then runs from the start to that point only.
  - Without it the export keeps going into black or blank frames. This matters when other clips sit further along the timeline.
- Turn off the safe-zone overlay layer before export ("No one wants their safe zones in their final version"). The safe zones are a PNG on the top video track.
- Export steps:
  - Click Export (top-left of the Premiere workspace tabs).
  - Set the file name, following your own naming convention.
  - Choose the output location.
  - Preset: Match Source – Adaptive High Bitrate. The instructor says it is "good enough".
- Review the whole edit attentively before exporting, even if it is boring. This saves your time and other people's time.
- Review the exported file as well. Rendering can introduce artifacts or errors that only show up after export. He checks it in VLC.
