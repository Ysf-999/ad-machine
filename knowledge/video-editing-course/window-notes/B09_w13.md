## Concrete rules and numbers
- Premiere highlighter: draw a rectangle (shape), fill it bright yellow (a "highlighter colour"), then in Effect Controls set Opacity > Blend Mode to **Multiply**. The text underneath stays readable and looks marked.
- Then cut the shape and move it to the next clip or line so the highlight follows the text. Cut the shape per clip and reposition it.
- The method is the same as in After Effects, so use whichever app you prefer. After Effects can also do it.
- Parallax needs the **subject and background on separate layers**.
- Photoshop subject separation:
  - Use the Object Selection tool with its mode set to **Lasso** (the default is Rectangle). Draw a rough outline around the subject and Adobe's AI selects it.
  - If a part is missed, hold **Shift** and lasso it to add to the selection. Zoom in to check.
  - Click **Select and Mask**, refine if needed, then set Output to **Layer Mask** and click OK. The background is removed.
  - The instructor skips the Select and Mask settings because there are many tutorials on them.
- Then you need a clean background plate behind the subject. He starts by duplicating the layer (the transcript ends mid-step).
- Monitor size: on a smaller monitor you may need to rearrange panels to leave room to animate.
