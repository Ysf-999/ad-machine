## Concrete rules and numbers
- Match b-roll to the voiceover line. "Busy morning schedule" gets a girl walking around town. "Tired of making trips to the microwave or throwing out expensive coffee" gets a cup-in-use lifestyle shot. The "first sip to last sip" line gets a traveling girl using the cup throughout her day.
- Close the ad with many aesthetic lifestyle shots that show the target avatar, "what someone watching this ad would want to be."
- Viewers should visualize themselves with the product. Show people who are nicely dressed, charismatic, and care about lifestyle, using the cup as it "elevates their whole style."
- Show every product feature in the footage (for example, "keeps your coffee warm for a long time"), and then back it with the lifestyle visuals.
- When the product has no obvious problem/solution demo, get creative with b-roll. Most products have a specific problem and are easier to demonstrate being used.
- "50% of selling this cup or even more is just lifestyle", i.e. identification marketing: seeing girls with charisma and aesthetic using the cup makes the viewer think "I have to have this as well."
