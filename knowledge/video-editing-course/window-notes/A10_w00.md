## What is taught
This window is an assignment brief, not an editing lesson. The instructor walks through a Milanote board for "High-Production Ad #1", a 10–15 s, three-step ad for a shilajit brand. The editor gets raw studio footage to color grade, polish and cut together with on-screen text. When the student is done, they can compare their edit to the instructor's own.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Length: 10–15 seconds.
- Structure is Hook → Hold (3 steps plus a product shot) → CTA.
- Variations needed per the board: 3 hooks, 1 hold, 3 total ads.
- Hook copy options (the board shows all three; the instructor says only "3 steps to lasting energy" aloud):
  - Hook 1: "3 Steps To Lasting Energy"
  - Hook 2: "Ready To Feel The Power Of Shilajit?"
  - Hook 3: "Pure Energy, No Crash, Made Easy:"
- Hold copy, in order:
  1. "#1 Prepare a warm drink"
  2. "Pure Shilajit Resin (Show the product here)" – the product shot goes between step 1 and step 2.
  3. "#2 Dip the spoon" – show a bit of shilajit taken with a small spoon.
  4. "#3 Mix together"
  5. "All-day energy boost ready." – the last on-screen text, shown when the drink is prepared.
- CTA: brand logo (Nurojit, per the board) plus the text "Your morning ritual, elevated."
- Sizes needed: the 1080x1920 (9:16) box appears ticked on the board. 1080x1080, 1080x1350 and 1920x1080 are unticked. Type of ad: Video Ad.
- Editor tasks, as the instructor lists them: add text on screen; color grade the raw videos; "polish" them so they look nice.
- Footage: one main one-take video plus supporting b-roll to mix in and "play around with" throughout the video.
