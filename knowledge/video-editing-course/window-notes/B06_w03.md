## What is taught
Premiere Pro project and sequence setup for a 9:16 UGC ad edit, aimed at beginners. The instructor covers sequence settings, a layout he sticks with, keyboard shortcuts, the track count, and placing a safe-zone overlay image on the top video track at low opacity and locked. Nothing is edited yet.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Sequence frame rate: 30 fps (timebase "30.00 frames/second").
- Frame size: 1080 horizontal × 1920 vertical (9:16). The instructor says this was covered earlier in the brief.
- Pick one workspace layout and stick with it. He prefers a vertical-oriented layout because most of his ads are 9:16.
- Learn keyboard shortcuts as early as possible. He has a PDF of basics, and says it is hard at first and improves with time.
- Timeline has 10 video tracks and 6 audio tracks. He says 10 is enough for what he does, and that a lot of tracks are needed when he gets into VSS.
- Safe zone:
  - Drag the safe-zone image from the Assets/Image folder into the timeline.
  - Keep it on the topmost video layer so all edit content sits below it.
  - Set its opacity to about 20.
  - Lock the track.
- If a panel such as Effect Controls or Properties is missing, enable it from the Window menu (a tick mark shows it is on).
