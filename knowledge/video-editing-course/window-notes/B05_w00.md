## What is taught
Clip selection starts from the brief, which says what footage to search for. The editor works from hook to CTA, so the hook comes first. In this ad the hook visual is "similar to OG ad": a creator clip showing the bungee leash and then the front D-ring mechanism. The editor finds the creator's raw footage in the brand's stock-footage folder, marks in/out points, drops the video only onto the timeline, and uses Premiere's Fill button to fit 4K footage to the 9:16 frame.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Always go back to the brief for clip selection; it tells you what to search for.
- Work in order from hook to CTA, so find the hook footage first.
- Hook visual brief: "similar to OG ad". Two clips are needed:
  1. The creator showing the bungee leash ("use physics to stop your dog from pulling").
  2. The mechanism: the front clip / front D-ring attachment that makes the dog turn around.
- Keep a per-brand folder for everything related to the brand, including creator-sent footage. Search it by creator name (here "livamos"); there were 4 clips of her.
- Marking a clip in the source monitor:
  - Press I at the start of the part you need.
  - Find the end precisely and press O.
  - Drag the "drag video only" icon to the timeline, so no source audio comes with it.
- The footage is 4K in a 1080x1920 sequence, so it imports oversized. Do not adjust Scale by hand to find the number (the instructor says it should be about 50). Instead go to Properties → Transform → click Fill.
- Screen values before Fill: Scale 50%, Position 540/960, Anchor 1080/1920. After Fill, Scale shows 100%.
