## What is taught
The instructor sets up an AI voiceover demo for two scripts: one for a humidifier client (the Milanote board says "Carepod – Chronic Conditions Angle") and one for a different product, Honeypacks. Voiceovers are made in ElevenLabs, a paid tool whose free tier is weak. This window covers only browsing and filtering the ElevenLabs Voice Library to find realistic, human-sounding voices and adding them to your own library. Actual script generation is deferred ("which I'll show in a bit").

## Concrete rules and numbers
- ElevenLabs is paid. A free version exists, but the instructor says it is "not that good".
- Voice-finding workflow: Voices → Library tab → filter, then preview a voice, then click **Add** to put it in "My voices". Pick the voice later when making the script.
- Library filters named by the instructor:
  - Language (e.g. English)
  - Accent (e.g. American)
  - Gender: male, female, neutral
  - Age: young, middle-aged, older
- Use-case category tabs: Narrative & Story, Conversational, Characters & Animation, Social Media, Entertainment & TV, Advertisement, Informative & Educational. The instructor names social media, entertainment/TV and advertisement as relevant.
- Goal: voices that sound like real humans, authentic, "don't sound like AI at all".
- Each voice has a preview button. Previews on screen run roughly 7–9 s.
- Account limit: "My voices" shows 30/30 custom voice slots used. Adding a voice triggers a "maximum amount of custom voices (30/30)" warning. You must delete voices or upgrade to add more.
