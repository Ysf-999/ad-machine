- List of benefits: add a "tick" or "ding" style SFX on each item.
- List of negatives or bad things: add a buzzer SFX.
- Transitions: add a whoosh, swoosh or swipe SFX on each one.
- Basic principle: any time something appears visually on screen, support it with an SFX.
- Purpose: increase retention, make people pay more attention, support claims and visuals.
- Caveat: his team doesn't use heavy SFX, and more heavily edited ads "just don't work for us". He still says to try it and implement it.
