## What is taught
The instructor finishes tuning an AI voiceover script in ElevenLabs Studio, then plays back the regenerated "Hold" chapter of the Carepod ad. His main point is that AI voiceovers sound robotic because people don't spend time on pronunciation and delivery. He says it takes about five minutes of attention, and that pasting a Claude/ChatGPT script straight into ElevenLabs and hitting generate does not work.

## Concrete rules and numbers
- Add connective filler words such as "and so", "maybe", "because", "basically", "stuff like that" at the start or end of sentences. They make the script flow more naturally.
- After editing the script, regenerate the audio and listen again. He says "I think I need to regenerate again" after his changes.
- Spend about 5 minutes thinking through how each line should be pronounced and how it should sound. He says this removes the "always sounds like AI" problem.
- He calls poor AI VO "usually a skill issue", meaning people don't pay enough attention.
- Don't copy-paste a raw Claude/ChatGPT script into ElevenLabs and expect it to be done.
