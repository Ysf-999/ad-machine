## Concrete rules and numbers
- ElevenLabs Studio regeneration:
  - To fix a bad read, switch playback to "playing selection" and regenerate sentence by sentence instead of the whole script.
  - After generating a paragraph, lock it so it can't be changed by accident.
  - Changing the global settings mid-generation cancels everything generated so far.
  - Use "Override settings" to change settings for only the selected paragraph. Those settings apply instead of the global ones.
  - Volume changes don't require regenerating (tooltip on screen: "Changing that will not require re-generating the paragraphs").
  - When finished, click Export, choose the audio format, then download.
- Premiere setup:
  - Bring in a safe-zone image, extend it to cover the sequence, set its opacity to 20, and lock the track.
  - Edit in layers, same as the previous video: A-roll/VO first, then music, then b-roll.
  - Enlarge the audio track height so the waveform is visible.
- VO cutting:
  - Cut out the silences and breaths. Keys mentioned: Shift+C, then Q / E / Q (the transcript is garbled, so the exact sequence is unclear). Q and E are presumably ripple-trim previous/next edit.
  - The instructor says breaths aren't a big issue since the VO is AI-generated, but silences still get cut.
- Colour-code VO clips: blue = hook, green = hold (body), teal = call to action.
- The instructor adds his "usual effects" to the audio track so it is clear.
- Music:
  - Music "gives soul to your ad" and makes the audience feel something.
  - Target feel here is a light-hearted, happy vibe, which has been working well recently for dog-friendly creatives.
  - He has three ways of sourcing music. Option one is TikTok or other social platforms. The rest is cut off at 15:00.
