# B05 — 5 video editing breakdown part 1 organic ad 1.3 (Video Editing Breakdowns)

**Summary:** A start-to-finish build of a ~40 s organic/UGC-style vertical ad (Dog Friendly Co, batch #95, "Watch How I Outsmart My Dog") in Premiere Pro, with AI VO, sourced b-roll, an After Effects tracked-arrow callout, captions, and three hook variants. The closing lesson is that the ad performed because of a simple script and a carefully chosen music track.

## Key lessons

1. **Start from the brief and work hook → CTA.** The brief (a Milanote board) says what footage to search for. Colour-coded script lines map to visual notes: pink = "back clip closeup / red arrow to highlight it", green = "dramatic drag clip", orange = "demo this mechanism". Check the script doc for notes on each line before editing it; with no note, you have free direction. [00:00, 03:00]
2. **Keep a per-brand footage bank and search it by creator name.** Creator-sent footage lives in "stock footages" (Brolls, livamos, Raw Footage Week, socials with taylor). Mark In (I) and Out (O) in the Source monitor, then drag "video only" so no source audio comes along. [00:00, 24:00]
3. **Fit 4K or off-size footage with Properties → Transform → Fill, not manual scaling.** The sequence is 1080×1920 (Position 540/960). [00:00]
4. **Run Scene Edit Detection on pre-joined clips.** Right-click → Scene Edit Detection → "Apply a Cut at Each Detected Cut Point". Then slide the cut to a meaningful moment: a VO word ("stop" rather than "pulling") or a music beat. Leave the end of the hook open for b-roll. [03:00]
5. **Source b-roll on TikTok with plain keywords** ("dog pulling", "dog drags owner", "lead pulling"; "lead" is the UK term). Download without a watermark via tikdownloader.io ("MP4 HD") into the project `stock` folder with sequential names (dog pulling, dog pulling 2…). If results are weak, rephrase the search or switch VPN location (he went from Sweden to the US), then refresh. [06:00, 09:00]
6. **Pick clips by their first frame and thumbnail.**
   - Prefer POV or over-the-shoulder shots with natural motion blur, stabilised on the dog, with the owner's action visible.
   - Reject clips where you can't see what's happening.
   - Reject big, bulky or bulldoggish dogs and prong collars, which look unpremium for Dog Friendly Co.
   - Trim to only the part that carries the message. [06:00, 15:00, 18:00]
7. **Match b-roll length to the VO line it covers.** A clip that ends before the line is a flash with no information. Don't stack two short clips over a sentence. If one visual runs about 3+ seconds, split the segment in two and use two different clips for retention. Keep the dog visible throughout. [12:00, 15:00]
8. **Time each clip to start and end on VO phrase boundaries.** Split the VO into per-phrase clips and cut the video at the audio cut points. The Text-panel transcript timestamps help cut VO precisely. Track which stock clips you've already used. [15:00, 21:00]
9. **Illustrate the mechanism line with footage that actually demonstrates it** (leash or harness being clipped to the front). Reject clips that are too short for their line. Diversify creators for "visual diversity". [21:00, 24:00]
10. **Stretch a clip to fit by setting Duration, not Speed %.** Press X to mark in/out, move the out point, then right-click → Speed/Duration and type the duration. Example: 1 s 14 f → 1 s 18 f gave 91.67%. Another clip ended at 88.52%. Keep slowdowns mild (~88–92%) so they're invisible. A 1-frame mismatch is acceptable. [27:00, 33:00]
11. **Proof the generated VO against the script and regenerate bad lines.** "Over 400,000 other frustrated owners" came out wrong. [27:00]
12. **ElevenLabs tuning trades emotion for intelligibility.**
    - Voice: Brittney – Social Media Voice; model: Eleven Multilingual v2.
    - Speed 1.00, Style Exaggeration ~10%, Speaker boost on.
    - Stability moved up to ~60% and similarity down to ~mid so a rushed take became clear.
    - A regeneration costs one credit per character (81 chars ≈ 81 credits), so iterate.
    - Later, the hook was regenerated with Style Exaggeration 28% and stability ~50% for more life.
    - Save as mp3 into the `VO` folder, one file per hook (h1/h2/h3 hook dogs.mp3, hold dogs.mp3, cta.mp3). [30:00, 66:00, 69:00]
13. **Mix: the VO goes on top of the music.**
    - VO clips sit at about +12.0 dB (+11.4 and +13.0 dB also seen).
    - The music has a Simple Parametric EQ notch (Center 1500 Hz, Q 4.0, Boost −15 dB) and volume −6.0 dB when the VO was hard to hear. The final Properties panel shows −8.0 dB.
    - The music runs the whole ad with a fade-out under the CTA. [30:00, 75:00]
14. **Add social proof with a collage of four creators and four breeds for the "400,000 owners" line.**
    - Place four ~3 s clips on V1–V4, select all, Nest, and name it "collage".
    - Each clip: Scale 50% on a 1080×1920 frame.
    - Quadrant positions: TL (270, 480), TR (810, 480), BL (270, 1440), BR (810, 1440).
    - If the source is larger (e.g. a clip at 25% = 50% visual), use the matching scale value. [33:00, 36:00]
15. **Pre-CTA: use one long clip (or two) for the last line.** The CTA is a saved "cta" clip plus cta.mp3, reused as-is. Review the whole assembly for vibe, feeling and a clear message. [39:00]
16. **Remove burned-in text from sourced clips.**
    - Export only the texted clips, with a few extra frames of handle at head and tail.
    - Export settings: H.264, 1080×1920, 30 fps, Rec. 709, VBR 1-pass 19 Mbps, AAC 320 kbps 48 kHz stereo.
    - Save to `mid`, then clean in Vmake (Watermark & Text Remover; up to 3 files; the free "Download 5s Preview Video" is enough for clips under 5 s). Put the cleaned files on V2 above the originals.
    - Three clips needed this. [42:00]
17. **Build the "red arrow + spotlight" callout in After Effects, then pull it back via Dynamic Link.**
    - Layers: arrow.png (2000×1200, scaled ~13–51%, anchor moved to the tip) over the clip.
    - Track Motion on a vivid spot, Analyze Forward, Edit Target → arrow, Apply X and Y.
    - Offset the whole path by selecting all Position keyframes (press P, click "Position") and dragging.
    - Drop Shadow: Opacity 75%, Direction 135°, Distance 20, Softness 50.
    - Keyframe rotation (~30°), opacity 0→100% over ~8–9 frames, and scale with Easy Ease (F9).
    - CC Spotlight: Height 50, Cone Angle 10, Edge Softness 25→72%, Intensity 0→~51, white, Render "Light Shadow". Its From/To are tied to the tracker's Feature Center or Attach Point.
    - Back in Premiere, render the in/out range so it plays smoothly. [45:00, 48:00, 51:00]
18. **Captions: generate from the transcript, then style for UGC readability.**
    - Create captions from transcript: format Subtitle, Lines = Double, max length 35 characters ("optimal"; default is 42), gap 0 frames.
    - Skip the hook text and the CTA line as captions; captions cover only the body.
    - Font HeadingNow 64 Norm Regular, size 45 → 50.
    - White fill, black 5 px outer stroke, Round or Bevel join instead of Miter, no shadow (shadows look cheap and complicated).
    - Position about 0/346 (offset ~334) in the lower half of the safe zone. Don't run captions in one long line; break them into balanced two-line layouts. About 80% of viewers watch without headphones and skim.
    - Select all captions → Upgrade Caption to Graphic. They land on V4. [51:00, 54:00, 57:00, 60:00]
19. **Work inside a custom safe-zone grid** (made by "ecom talent"). Keep text out of the platform UI "no place" areas. Captions may only slightly touch the small bottom rectangle. [54:00]
20. **Hook text card:**
    - Built by hand with the Type tool: HeadingNow 65 Norm Medium (the medium weight is for titles and heads).
    - White rounded background box, black text, no stroke or shadow, roundness ~21 ("by instinct"), centred in the upper-middle.
    - The size went 64 → 57 → 50 ("smaller is easier to read").
    - The CTA caption uses the same style, smaller. [60:00, 63:00, 69:00]
21. **Make hook variants by duplicating the finished sequence** (h1 → h2 → h3). Replace only the hook VO, hook b-roll and hook text; copy and apply attributes for the text style. Per the brief:
    - H2: "Watch me outsmart my dog".
    - H3: "Watch my dog train himself", swapped to "herself" when the dog wears pink or has a feminine name.
    - Hook VO files are about 1:14–2:05 (frame timecodes). The sequence is ~40 s. [63:00, 66:00]
22. **Fix framing problems with a directional punch-in.** If the subject sits at the frame edge, zoom toward that side (Scale 100% → 178% with Position adjusted). He used the "Malice Screen Zoom In Right" preset. Then Paste Attributes onto the h2/h3 hooks and do a final full watch for flaws. [69:00, 72:00]
23. **Cut density:** about one clip every 1–3 s, with V2 inserts every few seconds and V4 captions running most of the ad. [30:00, 63:00]
24. **Why this ad performed:** a simple script whose visuals match what is said, plus a carefully chosen music track.
    - Don't skip music selection to save time.
    - Give the script to GPT, ask for its emotional arc, then audition tracks in Artlist, Epidemic Sound or YouTube.
    - The track used is "Bunker Buster – Colorful and Animated". [75:00]
25. **Reference playback (Guard Blinds "Reverse Aging VSL", 02:19, 9:16).** No teaching here, only example style:
    - Mid-frame white captions, 2–4 words per card, key words large and bold, secondary words smaller.
    - A new visual every 2–4 s.
    - A split-screen comparison and near-black frames for "darkness" beats.
    - A black end card ("30 DAY IN-HOME TRIAL"). [78:00]

## Editing recipe

1. Read the brief; set the sequence to 1080×1920. Generate the VO (ElevenLabs) per section (hooks, hold, CTA) and import it to A1. Put the music on A2. [00:00, 30:00]
2. **Hook:** find the creator raw clip, mark I/O, drag video only, and apply Fill. Run Scene Edit Detection. Slide the cut to "stop" or the beat. Leave the tail of the hook empty for b-roll. [00:00–06:00]
3. **Hold 1:**
   - Split the VO into phrases (use the transcript timestamps).
   - Source TikTok clips for "dramatic drag"; download HD and trim.
   - Place a clip under each phrase and split at the VO boundaries.
   - Split any visual longer than ~3 s into two clips.
   - Use creator-bank clips for "move the clip to the front" (the mechanism demo).
   - Stretch clips to fit using Duration. [06:00–30:00]
4. **Proof and close:** build the 4-up collage (nest, Scale 50%, quadrant positions) for "400,000 owners". Add one long clip for the pre-CTA line, then the saved CTA clip and cta.mp3. [33:00–42:00]
5. **Text removal:** export the texted clips with handles, clean them in Vmake, and put the results on V2 over V1. [42:00]
6. **Callout:** build the red arrow + CC Spotlight in After Effects (track, drop shadow, rotation, opacity, scale), then render the range in Premiere. [45:00–54:00]
7. **Captions:**
   - Generate the captions (35 characters, double line, subtitle format).
   - Style them: HeadingNow Regular 50, white with a black 5 px outer stroke, round join, no shadow, ~y 346 inside the safe zone.
   - Upgrade them to graphics.
   - Skip the hook and CTA lines, and build the hook card by hand.
   - Add a CTA caption. [54:00–66:00]
8. **Variants:** duplicate h1 → h2 → h3. Swap the hook VO, b-roll and hook text per the brief. Apply a directional zoom if the subject sits at the frame edge, then paste attributes across the variants. [63:00–75:00]
9. Do a final review pass, check for typos (a "pulling orever" typo was visible on screen), and publish. [63:00, 72:00]

## How an ffmpeg/Python agent applies this

1. **Brief-driven selection:**
   - JUDGE: map each script line (and its note) to a candidate clip. A vision model should check each candidate against the note.
   - AUTOMATE: parse the brief text into per-line tasks and name files by hook/section.
2. **Footage bank:**
   - AUTOMATE: index the folder by creator or filename with ffprobe (duration, resolution) and cut with `-ss/-to`. Dropping source audio is `-an`.
   - JUDGE: confirm the in/out range is the right action.
3. **Fill to 9:16:**
   - AUTOMATE: `scale=1080:1920:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase,crop=1080:1920`.
4. **Scene detection:**
   - AUTOMATE: ffmpeg `select='gt(scene,0.3)'` or PySceneDetect to find cuts.
   - JUDGE: choose the cut position relative to VO words and beats. Get word timestamps from a transcription (Whisper or ElevenLabs) and beats via librosa.
5. **TikTok sourcing:**
   - SKIP: the browser, VPN and tikdownloader steps (manual and ToS-sensitive). If footage is supplied, ingest it.
   - JUDGE: the clip-quality criteria (POV, motion blur, premium dog, readable action). Use frame-grab thumbnails (first frame) plus a vision model.
6. **Clip choice by first frame:** JUDGE with a vision model on the first frame and the owner/dog visibility. AUTOMATE a check for no burned-in text (OCR) in the chosen range.
7. **Length to VO line:** AUTOMATE.
   - Get phrase start/end from the VO transcript.
   - Choose a clip with duration ≥ the line, or split any line over ~3 s into two clips.
   - Assemble with concat or trim.
   - Flag any clip shorter than its line.
8. **Cutting at phrase boundaries:** AUTOMATE with forced alignment of the VO to the script, then build the cut list from the word timestamps.
9. **Mechanism clips and creator diversity:**
   - AUTOMATE: group by creator and avoid reusing a used-clip ID.
   - JUDGE: whether the footage really demonstrates the mechanism.
10. **Duration-stretch:** AUTOMATE.
    - Compute ratio = clip_len / target_len. Use `setpts=PTS/(ratio)` or `setpts=(target/orig)*PTS`.
    - Enforce a floor of ~0.88 on the speed.
    - Prefer trimming if more than ~12% slowdown is needed.
11. **VO proofing:**
    - AUTOMATE: transcribe the generated mp3 and diff it against the script. Regenerate on mismatch.
    - JUDGE: whether it sounds right.
12. **ElevenLabs:** AUTOMATE through the API with these settings (stability ~0.5–0.6, similarity ~0.65, style 0.1–0.28, speaker boost). Budget about 1 credit per character. JUDGE the take for intelligibility versus emotion.
13. **Mix:** AUTOMATE.
    - VO `volume=+12dB` (or loudnorm; check clipping).
    - Music `volume=-6dB..-8dB` with `equalizer=f=1500:width_type=q:w=4:g=-15`.
    - Add `afade` out at the end. Consider sidechain ducking as an alternative.
14. **Collage:** AUTOMATE.
    - Four inputs, each `scale=540:960`.
    - `xstack` or overlays at (0,0), (540,0), (0,960), (540,960). This is equivalent to the 270/810/480/1440 centre positions.
    - Each clip is ~3 s.
    - JUDGE: that the breeds and creators differ.
15. **Pre-CTA and CTA:**
    - AUTOMATE: concat the saved CTA clip and cta.mp3.
    - JUDGE: review the assembled cut for vibe and clarity (a full-ad model pass or a human).
16. **Text removal:**
    - AUTOMATE: detect text frames with OCR, then export those segments with handles (±few frames).
    - Cleaning: Vmake is a web UI and can't be driven. Substitute `delogo`, a blurred box or crop-zoom to hide the text, or an inpainting model, and verify visually. Or prefer clips without text.
    - JUDGE: that the cleaned result is acceptable.
17. **Red arrow + spotlight:**
    - AUTOMATE (approximately): OpenCV tracking (CSRT or optical flow) on the target spot, which outputs per-frame x,y.
    - Overlay arrow.png with `overlay=x='...':y='...'` from the track data (or `sendcmd`). Apply a drop shadow (a blurred dark copy at an offset of 20), a rotation of ~30°, and opacity/scale fades.
    - Spotlight: a radial vignette mask (`geq` or a premade radial PNG) following the track, at 50% darkening.
    - Dynamic Link and the AE UI: SKIP.
    - JUDGE: that the arrow tip lands on the harness.

    - Generate ASS from the word timestamps.
