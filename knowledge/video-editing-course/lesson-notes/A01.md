# A01 — 1 introduction (Video Ads Mastery)

**One-line summary.** Module intro, not an editing tutorial: the instructor argues that ad style must follow product and audience, and that a raw phone-shot ad can beat an expensive cinematic one. He previews four ad types and the course structure.

## Key lessons

1. **Learn strategy before formats.** Buyer psychology, identifying the perfect customer and what stops the scroll come before ad formats. [00:00–00:45]
2. **Four ad types are covered:** organic-style (feels native to the platform), UGC (looks authentic), high-production (when a premium feel is needed) and VSL (keeps people glued to the screen). [00:45]
3. **Pick the style by product, niche, brand and client.** No single style wins everywhere. [00:45–01:30]
4. **Polish does not equal performance.** In the Wazoo Survival Gear case, a cinematic woods shoot (days of planning, hours of filming, professional cameras) "did nothing". A random person with an iPhone doing ASMR-style content, sharing favourite gear, "printed money". [01:30–02:00]
5. **Judge an ad by resonance, not looks.** "Sometimes the worst looking ad is actually the better ad." [02:00]
6. **Curriculum order:**
   - key characteristics of each ad type and when to use them
   - how to pick the style for product, brand and client
   - how to structure scroll-stopping hooks
   - how to retain viewers
   - the instructor's tech stack and workflow
   - live workshops [02:45, 03:00]
7. **The course includes live from-scratch ad edits and breakdowns of the agency's best-performing ads, with reasons they work.** The first topic is organic-style ads ("absolutely crushing it right now"). [03:00–03:19]

No editing numbers (cut rate, zoom, caption specs, dB levels) are given in this lesson.
