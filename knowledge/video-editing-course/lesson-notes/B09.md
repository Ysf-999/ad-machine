# B09 — 9 video editing breakdown part 5 vsl ad (Video Editing Breakdowns)

**One-line summary.** A full layer-by-layer teardown of a ~1:40 story-based VSL ad for Flow Pouches (cordyceps oral pouch, 1993 Chinese track team story). It covers b-roll planning, AI voiceover, music, line-by-line b-roll matching, animation in Premiere and After Effects, captions, SFX, and final QA.

## Key lessons

1. **Know the brief and the structure before editing.** Product: Flow Pouches, a mushroom (lion's mane, cordyceps) oral pouch for focus, nicotine and caffeine free, an alternative to nicotine pouches. Ad spend goes mostly to men in their late 20s and up. The ad is 3 hooks plus 1 hold (the long VO body). [00:00–04:00]
2. **Use a curiosity-and-story script arc.** [00:00, 06:00]
   - Hook: "How China's track team legally cheated in 1993."
   - Story: 3 world records in 15 days (1500 m, 3000 m, 10,000 m), doping accusations, every test clean.
   - Reveal: the coach credits cordyceps.
   - Mechanism: ATP, VO2 max.
   - Problem: up to 90% of supplements is destroyed in digestion.
   - Product: "That's why we created Flow Pouches", absorbed through oral tissue straight into the bloodstream.
   - Benefit stack: "No digestion. No waste. Maximum absorption."
   - Close and CTA: "Try them today…", then the brand name.
3. **Plan b-roll before opening the editor.** [03:00–06:00]
   - Make a 2-column table (Script line | B-roll), with the script line by line on the left.
   - Be very specific about each b-roll, so you have "a targeted mission" instead of endless scrolling.
   - Highlight or cross off each line once its clip is downloaded.
   - Edit the table as new ideas come up during the edit.
4. **Pick the AI voice for the ad's tone.** [06:00]
   - Real-incident story → an "authority" narrator (ElevenLabs "James T", speed 1.0x). He contrasts it with a casual, relaxed voice that he says is also great for AI.
   - Put each hook in its own block.
   - CAPITALISE words to emphasise (JUST, EVERY, FAR, POWERFUL, TRULY, GONE).
   - Use "…" and " - " for pacing.
   - Listen to the generations before exporting.
5. **Cut the VO tight but keep deliberate pauses.** [06:00–09:00]
   - Remove silences and breaths. Fillers are unlikely with AI VO.
   - Keep a small gap before the word you want to land, e.g. "Records that had stood for years… gone." Without the gap it hits much weaker.
   - Cutting doesn't have to be word by word every time.
6. **Colour-code the VO clips on long scripts.** Blue = hook, green = hold, orange = emphasis words, and a colour change where the product is introduced. [06:00–09:00]
7. **VO audio chain.** [06:00–12:00]
   - Multiband Compressor, preset "Broadcast".
   - DeEsser.
   - Hard Limiter at -6 dB.
   - DeEsser values: preset "Low Voice DeEsser" (for a low male voice), Multiband mode, threshold about -30 dB, centre frequency 4700 Hz, bandwidth 3000 Hz. Lower the threshold and tune by ear.
   - Use the "High Voice DeEsser" preset for a woman or higher-pitched speaker.
8. **Music: one track with multiple moods, each mapped to a script section.** [09:00–15:00]
   - Brief: "heavy and hard hitting". Track used: Artlist "2050 – Game Over" (~3:48).
   - Hook: suspenseful buildup, plus SFX.
   - Body part 1: heavy, repetitive, catchy, "serious dispute" mood.
   - Next part: serious suspense.
   - Product intro: positive, "like a champion".
   - CTA: toned-down passage from the end of the track.
   - Don't keep one vibe throughout.
9. **Fit music length with Premiere's Remix.** [09:00–15:00]
   - Essential Sound > Edit > Music > Duration > Method "Remix".
   - Drag the clip end to the target length so the natural ending (where the waveform drops) lands on the CTA.
   - Customize sliders (Segments, Variations) were both at 5.0 on screen.
   - The waveform looks odd after remixing, which is expected. Add a fade-out at the end.
10. **Find b-roll fast.** [12:00–15:00]
    - Google the topic and click the "Short videos" tab. It aggregates Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest and Shutterstock. Use it as the primary search.
    - Also use YouTube links, documentaries and stock (Artlist-style).
    - If a clip is perfect but low-res, upscale it.
11. **Upscale low-res archival footage.** [15:00–18:00]
    - In Topaz Video AI, use a 4x upscale (source became 1536×1152).
    - Model Proteus for most cases, Rhea for 4x. The UI also shows Iris (faces).
    - Settings: add noise 0, recover detail 20, focus fix off, grain off, 25 fps original, no slow-mo, interpolation off.
    - Export via Cloud (credits, for weak machines) or locally via "Export as…".
    - Archival footage that is slightly blurry still looks acceptable in vertical.
12. **Every b-roll clip should convey the idea of the line it sits under.** The match doesn't have to be literal, and abstract visuals are fine. "If you manage to do that, you are golden." [18:00–27:00] Examples:
    - "September 1993, Beijing": a family photo of the runner from a documentary, for authenticity ("a real person").
    - "Unknown Chinese women runners": footage of the athletes running.
    - "Obliterated 3 world records": a runner with a massive lead.
    - 1500 / 3000 / 10,000 m: one high-quality still of the runners per event.
    - "Records that stood for years": a clip of the runner in the event.
    - "International community in shock": the athlete with her national flag.
    - "Accusations flew": news screenshots.
    - "Steroids, doping, drugs": syringe and pill stock.
    - "Every test came back clean": a runner finishing alone.
    - Coach introduction: images of the coach and team, then a treadmill shot (mysterious).
    - "Weren't using synthetic drugs": the athlete celebrating, plus syringe stock.
    - "Cordyceps secret": cordyceps clips with a mosaic.
    - "Tibet": a drone mountain shot from TikTok.
    - ATP: an abstract animation coming to life.
    - "Cellular energy": a cell-like clip.
    - VO2 max: an abstract lung-like clip.
    - "Use oxygen during exercise": a man running (chosen to fit the runner audience).
    - Supplements: pills, then a pill being digested, then an inverted high-contrast clip.
    - Bloodstream: a blood-flow or red blood-cell clip.
    - Oral absorption: the client clip of the pouch placed at the gum.
    - "Activating benefits almost instantly": a person running.
    - Closing lines: cordyceps, then athletes, then product shots only (CTA).
    - A turmeric-powder pill can stand in for a cordyceps supplement.
13. **Black or high-contrast cards add emphasis.** Black/white, blue/yellow and red/white pairs work, as does any brand-palette pair. [15:00–27:00]
    - A black card with the single word GONE (large bold italic white caps).
    - A black card "THAT'S NOT *efficient* AT ALL…" (bold sans, with a large italic serif for "efficient") to stress the competitors' weakness.
14. **Keep static material alive with quick cuts.** For news screenshots, cut quickly between several, keeping keywords in the centre of the frame. Add a highlighter on key words. [18:00]
15. **Mosaic effect for suspense.** [18:00–24:00]
    - Duplicate the cordyceps clip and apply Mosaic with horizontal blocks 75 and vertical blocks 75 (Sharp Colors unchecked). It builds curiosity about "the secret".
    - Play with the numbers.
16. **Invert for a "bad thing".** Apply Invert to a high-contrast pill-being-destroyed clip. [21:00–24:00]
17. **Reverse a clip for the product intro.** Pouches that fell down fly back up into frame. In Premiere: Speed/Duration > Reverse Speed. [24:00]
18. **Contrast pairs.** "No digestion, no waste" gets scattering particles (second half inverted). "Maximum absorption" gets a more uniform, consolidated clip. [24:00]
19. **Re-review the b-roll for weak points where viewers might click off.** Swap clips or animate there. Fixing weaknesses at the b-roll layer makes later layers easier. Hook matches: hook 1, runner before the event then in action; hook 2, Chinese athletes running; hook 3, a runner overtaking almost everyone. [27:00]
20. **Zoom several consecutive stills together with one adjustment layer.** [27:00–30:00]
    - Adjustment layer on V5 affects V1–V4 only.
    - Add Transform and animate Scale 100 → about 112.
    - Per-clip scaling can't match speed across 3 images.
    - Create via Project > New Item > Adjustment Layer.
    - Other clips get a clip-level animation, e.g. coach images with a left-to-right position move. Matching speed across them is unneeded.
21. **Export the hold for After Effects.** [30:00–33:00]
    - Set In/Out around the hold. Preset Apple ProRes 4444 with alpha, QuickTime (.mov). If not listed, search "4444" in More presets and star it.
    - Reason: dynamic link stresses RAM, has bugs, and previews and renders slowly.
    - Export spec: 1080×1920, 30 fps, Rec. 709, 1:37:03. The file was about 3.0 GB.
22. **Split the flattened hold into per-cut layers in After Effects.** [33:00]
    - Right-click the layer > Scene Edit Detection > "Split Layer at Scene Edit", with Create Pre-comps unchecked.
    - Safe-zone PNG on top at 20% opacity, fit to comp (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+G), locked.
23. **Put b-roll inside a framed rectangle for "more class".** [33:00–39:00]
    - White rectangle 1080×1350 (4:5), constrain proportions off. Centre it to the composition, then scale it down inside the safe zone with a small gap ("breathing space").
    - Rounded rectangle, Roundness 16.
    - Every clip uses the shape as Track Matte (dropdown or pick-whip). Resize and reposition each clip to fit.
    - Do this in After Effects, not Premiere.
    - Add small animations where useful, e.g. position and scale zoom to the coach's face (scale about 200% over a few frames).
24. **Highlighter effect on article screenshots.** [36:00–42:00]
    - A bright-yellow rectangle with blend mode **Multiply** (shape 537×93 in After Effects).
    - Works in both After Effects and Premiere (Effect Controls > Opacity > Blend Mode > Multiply). Cut and reposition the shape per clip or line.
25. **Parallax from a still.** [39:00–45:00]
    - Separate subject and background in Photoshop: Object Selection in Lasso mode, Shift-lasso to add missed areas, Select and Mask, Output = Layer Mask.
    - Reload the selection (Ctrl+click the mask), Select > Modify > Expand 3–5 px, then use Remove to erase the subject from the background plate. Ignore leftover artifacts the subject will cover.
    - Export the subject and the background group as PNGs.
    - In After Effects, scale only, two keyframes each: at 0 s both 100%; at about 10 s subject 115% and background 106%.
    - Closer objects move faster. He admits it doesn't matter much ("just for fun").
26. **Captions.** [42:00–48:00]
    - Premiere: Text panel > Create captions from transcript. Captioning Preferences: Maximum length in characters at the minimum, Lines Single. Result: 1–3 word cards.
    - Style: select all, Zone grid centre cell (screen centre).
    - Font Proxima Nova Bold, size about 80, white fill, stroke off.
    - Shadow 1: black, distance 0, size about 165, blur about 14.2, opacity about 71–74%.
    - Optional Shadow 2: black, distance 0, opacity about 57%, angle 135°, blur about 8.8, size about 70.
    - Proofread for transcription errors, delete duplicate captions, and make sure black screens carry no stray text.
27. **Sound effects are the last layer.** [45:00–51:00]
    - Build a personal SFX library sorted by folder: Impact, Risers, Shutter+Clicks, Whoosh.
    - Use risers in hooks and right before an important point (e.g. before "GONE"). A tiny riser works as a transition.
    - Put Studio Reverb on the riser track: preset "Great Hall", raise only the Wet output (starts "somewhere around 40"; screen showed about 72.3%). This stops the riser ending abruptly.
    - Check by soloing the track and comparing with and without.
28. **Add a whoosh or transition sound where a cut feels like something is missing.** [51:00]
29. **QA before and after export.** Check the whole video for errors and glitches. Attention to detail is a core editor skill, and avoidable errors reaching the client look bad. [51:00–52:39]

## Editing recipe

1. Read the brief and ad structure (3 hooks + 1 hold; the CTA is product clips).
2. Make the 2-column Google Doc, script line vs specific b-roll.
3. Generate the VO in ElevenLabs. Use separate hook blocks, caps for emphasis, and "…" and "-" for pacing.
4. In Premiere, cut silences and breaths and keep a gap before key words (e.g. "gone"). Colour-code the clips: hook, hold, emphasis, product intro.
5. Apply the VO chain: Multiband Compressor (Broadcast), DeEsser (matched to the voice), Hard Limiter -6.
6. Choose a multi-mood track and map its passages to hook / body / suspense / product / CTA. Use Remix to hit the target length so the ending lands on the CTA. Add a fade-out.
7. Source b-roll via Google "Short videos", YouTube and stock. Upscale weak archival clips in Topaz (4x, Proteus).
8. Lay b-roll line by line, cutting roughly every phrase (1–3 s). Add the black GONE card, a mosaic on the cordyceps reveal, Invert on the "bad" pill, a reversed pouch clip, and quick-cut news screenshots.
9. Re-review for weak points and fix them.
10. Animate: adjustment-layer zoom (100→112) over grouped stills, and per-clip position moves.
11. Export the hold as ProRes 4444 with alpha. In After Effects, run Scene Edit Detection and add the safe-zone guide (20%). Build the 4:5 rounded (16) white frame as track matte per clip, then the coach zoom-in, Multiply highlighter, and parallax if wanted. Render back.
12. Create captions from the transcript (single line, minimum characters). Centre them, Proxima Nova Bold ~80, white, double soft shadow. Proofread.
13. Add SFX (risers before key points, whooshes at cuts), with Studio Reverb (Great Hall) on the riser track.
14. QA before export and after export.

## On-screen specifics

- **Final ad:** about 1:40 (the export dialog shows 00:01:37:03; the FINE CUT timeline shows about 1:50–2:00:10 including trailing clips). Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps.
- **Premiere project:** "Flow Pouch – BATCH#34 – RunnersAthletes – Video – IDEA – Mufariz".
  - Sequences: FINE CUT, BREAKDOWN, HOOK 1/2/3, PANCAKE.
  - Folders: 01 Sequences, 02 Footage, 03 Assets (_CLIENT, Audio with _SFX, Image, Video), 04 Other (Adjustment Layer).
- **Layer stack, top to bottom:**
  - Subtitle track C1 (orange caption blocks).
  - Purple full-length "SAFEZONES.png" guide.
  - CTA-1/2/3 PNGs on V3–V6 near the end.
  - Adjustment layers and small fx clips.
  - V1 with many short clips (HOLD.mp4 and stills).
  - A1 VO, A2 music with a fade-out, A3 riser/SFX track with Studio Reverb, A4 sustained low sound.
- **CTA frame:** the stacked lines "Unlock Your" (white pill), "Full Potential Now" (green pill, white text), "flowpouch.com" (white pill), with the caption "Try them today".
- **Caption look:** white bold sans, centred mid-frame (about 47% down), 1–3 words per card with punctuation kept. A soft shadow, no box, no stroke. Cards run about 0.5–1.5 s.
- **Cut pace:** b-roll about every 1–3 s, on phrase boundaries. Cordyceps clips are slightly slowed (e.g. 97.44%, 91.49%). Some clips are speed-changed (e.g. 250.42%, 259.42%).
- **Framing:** the final video uses the 4:5 white rounded frame inside the 9:16 canvas (black above and below). Burned-in Chinese hard subs on archival footage are cropped or sit under the captions.
- **Hook title:** the two-line title "How China's Track Team "LEGALLY Cheated" in 1993" in the same white bold style.
- **Hook copy variants:**
  - "How China's Track Team 'LEGALLY' Cheated in 1993"
  - "How China Shattered 3 World Records EXPLAINED"
  - "The Truth Behind China's 1993 'Doping' Scandal"
- **Fav effects bin:** Auto Reframe, Crop, Drop Shadow, Gaussian Blur, Horizontal Flip, Invert, Lens Distortion, Mosaic, Noise, Transform, Vertical Flip, Warp Stabilizer.
- **Transform effect on the adjustment layer:** anchor/position 540/960, Scale animating 100→~112, uniform scale on, Bilinear sampling.
- **After Effects project:** comps Clip parallax, HOLD, HOOK 1/2/3, Main, Main 2. The "Main 2" comp had about 64 HOLD.mov layers in a stair-step. The safe-zone PNG is 2160×3840.
- **Studio Reverb screen values:** Great Hall, Room Size 70, Decay 6000 ms, Early Reflections 20%, HF Cut 4000 Hz, Damping 100%, Diffusion 70%, Wet about 72.3%.
- **Project assets:** Milanote briefing board ("Flow Pouch – BATCH#34 – Runners/Athletes", with Hook/Hold/CTA cards) and an "Editing Breakdown" board (B-Roll → Animations/Transitions → Captions/Text → SFX).
