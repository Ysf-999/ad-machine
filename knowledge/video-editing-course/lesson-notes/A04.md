# A04 — 4 action item organic ads (Video Ads Mastery)

**Summary:** This lesson introduces the hands-on action items (a Milanote "Ad Briefs" board) and walks through the three organic-ad briefs: Mom IG Reel, Comment Reply and Haircare Routine. Each brief is 3 hooks + 1 hold = 3 ads, and each should look like native social content, not an ad.

## Key lessons

1. **Do every action item and send the results for feedback.** The instructor says doing the work with feedback is "the best way to learn" and "the fastest way". The ads also become a portfolio covering organic, UGC, VSL, high-production and static ads, which can back job applications and client pitches. [00:00-06:00]
2. **Board order and size.** The briefs run Organic → UGC → VSL → High production → Statics. The organic briefs are Ad#1 "Mom IG Reel", Ad#2 "Comment Reply" and Ad#3 "Haircare Routine". [00:00-03:00]
3. **Every concept ships as 3 variations: 3 hooks + 1 shared hold = 3 ads.** Only the hook text (or hook comment reply) differs, and the hold is identical in all three. [03:00-09:00]
4. **Deliver 1080x1920 (9:16).** Only that size is ticked for the video briefs. 1080x1080, 1080x1350 and 1920x1080 are listed but unchecked. The ad type is "Video Ad". [03:00-09:00]
5. **Organic #1, Mom IG Reel (Peekaboo Pillow).** This is a caption + music, no-VO style focused on how the baby loves to use the pillow. [03:00-06:00]
   - Hook 1: "My baby's favorite ways to use Peekaboo Pillow"
   - Hook 2: "4 ways baby loves her Peekaboo Pillow"
   - Hook 3: "My baby's favorite pillow is a must have for new parents"
   - Hold beats: Tummy time, Nursing, Bonding face to face, Hanging with big sis!
6. **Organic #2, Comment Reply (Brrrn Board slide-board workout).** [06:00-09:00]
   - The hook is a TikTok-style comment reply. The brief links tokcomment.com for generating fake comments.
   - It should look like content gym bros make and watch, with trendy gym-bro music.
   - The footage is limited. The man does only about 3 moves (glute bridge, pike-ups, lateral lunge), so the video is quick.
   - Open with him walking toward the camera to show off the physique.
   - Sample hooks are Q&A pairs:
     - "Bro how if do I get shredded as you?" / "Got you 👇"
     - "Dude, what is the secret to getting cut like that?" / "This."
     - "Advice for leveling up my core training?" / "Add instability. Get yourself a slide board 🔥"
7. **Organic #3, Haircare Routine (Laifen).** The creator "tells the story" about her experience with the product only through captions over trendy music. [06:00-09:00]
   - Show the final result in the hook. She shows her blowout at the start and the end of the original footage.
   - The hold is Caption 1 to Caption 9, short sentences with occasional emojis, running from "I used to go to the salon every few weeks for a blowout… but it was so expensive 😅" to "Honestly, if you're still going to the salon, this will change your life. Just give it a try!"
   - Match shots to captions. There is enough footage for the length of the script.
   - Music: "viral girly sound/music". The brief gives YouTube inspiration links, and music recommendations are optional.
   - Hooks:
     - "What if you could skip the salon… and still get this?"
     - "No more $40 blowouts. Salon hair at home? Yes, please."
     - "Get THIS at home, without the salon price tag."
8. **Keep it simple.** These are the easiest video ads among the action items. The instructor says "shouldn't take you a whole lot of time" and "no need to overcomplicate organic ads". [03:00-09:00]
9. **Delivery.** Do one ad or all three; sending all three lets the instructor review them together. Upload to Google Drive (or similar) and send the link, or send directly on Discord. [06:00-12:00]

## Editing recipe

For each organic brief:
1. Read the brief card: client, example ad, sizes, variations, ideation concept.
2. Build one hold from the raw footage (and captions or music), at 1080x1920.
3. Make the hook for variation 1. Hook text goes on the opening (Mom IG Reel, Haircare). For Comment Reply, the hook is the comment bubble.
4. Duplicate the timeline and swap only the hook text or comment for variations 2 and 3.
5. Add trendy music that fits the niche (gym-bro, viral girly, etc.).
6. Export the 3 files, upload them to Drive, and send the link (or send on Discord).

Brief-specific notes:
- **#1:** Write the captions over nursing, tummy time, face-to-face and sister footage. No VO.
- **#2:** Start with the walk-toward-camera clip, then the 3 moves, with the comment reply bubble in the hook.
- **#3:** Hook with the blowout result, then Caption 1-9 matched to the shots.

## How an ffmpeg/Python agent applies this

1. **Do all action items and get feedback.** SKIP: this is course logistics. The agent can still produce the deliverables.
2. **Board order and file counts.** SKIP: Milanote is a GUI board. If briefs are provided as text, parse them.
3. **3 hooks + 1 hold = 3 ads.** AUTOMATE: render the hold once. Burn each hook text as an ASS/drawtext overlay or a hook segment, concat with the hold (`-f concat` or `xfade`), and loop over the 3 hook strings.
4. **1080x1920 output.** AUTOMATE: `scale=1080:1920:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase,crop=1080:1920`, then verify with ffprobe.
5. **Mom IG Reel (captions + music, no VO).** AUTOMATE: write the caption lines as ASS events timed to beats, with an opaque white box (`BorderStyle=3`) and a serif font. True rounded corners are not available in ASS, so render a rounded-rect PNG with PIL and overlay it. Mix music with `amix` or `volume`. JUDGE: check that beats match the footage (tummy time, nursing, etc.) and that the style looks plain and organic.
6. **Comment Reply.** AUTOMATE: render a TikTok-style comment bubble PNG with PIL (white rounded box, avatar circle, bold black text, "Reply to … comment:") and overlay it on the hook with `overlay=enable='between(t,0,N)'`. Generating the comment on tokcomment.com is a web tool, so build the bubble locally instead. JUDGE: check that the clip order starts with the walk toward the camera and that the look fits gym-bro content.
7. **Haircare Routine.** AUTOMATE: lay out Caption 1-9 as timed ASS events (white bold text on a black box, a different color for the product name) and cut the footage to match. JUDGE: use a vision model to confirm shot-to-caption relevance and that the blowout result appears in the hook.
8. **Music choice.** JUDGE: pick or verify "trendy" music (viral girly, gym-bro). The agent cannot judge trendiness without a reference. Mix at a level that sits under the captions.
9. **Keep it simple.** AUTOMATE: use minimal effects, with captions + footage + music only.
10. **Delivery.** SKIP: Drive and Discord submission is manual. The agent only exports the 3 files with clear names such as `ad1_hook1.mp4`.
