# B04 — 4 video editing breakdown part 1 organic ad 1.2 (Video Editing Breakdowns)

**Summary:** How the instructor chooses, downloads and mixes background music for a ~40 s vertical dog-harness organic ad ("Watch How I Outsmart My Dog", Dog Friendly Co, batch #95). He works from the brief and uses ChatGPT for search phrases. He auditions Artlist tracks until one is a 100% fit. He then carves a voice pocket with a Simple Parametric EQ (1500 Hz, Q 4, −15 dB) plus a −6 dB volume cut, and finishes with a fade-out on the natural ending.

## Key lessons

1. **Start music selection from the brief.** Ask what message the ad must deliver. [00:00]
   - His default for dog-friendly brands is calm and friendly, supporting the core message of the script rather than playing a big role.
   - Exception: the reference ad had "really good spend and overall good performance" and used fast-paced rock, so he matches that vibe.
2. **If you lack music knowledge, use ChatGPT as an advisor.** [00:00–03:00]
   - Keep a dedicated "Ecom Editing" ChatGPT project so past music requests build up as history.
   - Its past chats include music selection, tone and emotional arcs, and music for UGC.
3. **Prompt structure for the music search:** give context (screenshot of the brief), say what you need, then ask what the model needs from you. [03:00–06:00]
   - Keep chatting until "we're on the same vibe". No "super prompt" is needed.
   - Prompt used: upbeat, cheerful, a little faster, rock-ish is fine even for dogs, and give search words for Artlist/Extreme Music.
4. **ChatGPT returns phrase lists.** He started with the first, "upbeat rock pop positive advertising". Others returned:
   - energetic indie rock commercial upbeat
   - happy confident pop rock upbeat rhythm
   - playful upbeat guitar groove ad
   - fun optimistic pop rock commercial
   - driving light rock energetic feel-good
   - uplifting rhythmic pop rock beat commercial
   - modern upbeat indie pop electric guitars
   - Extra sets: dog-friendly/wholesome (friendly upbeat acoustic pop whistle clap; happy feel-good indie folk upbeat; playful commercial acoustic upbeat fun) and smart-solution/confident (clever upbeat indie rock tech ad; modern confident pop rock groove upbeat; driving rhythmic optimistic upbeat guitars).
   - ChatGPT also offered to narrow by BPM range or mood intensity.
5. **Two sources:** Artlist (most common among editors) and Extreme Music (paid, "kind of expensive", "used by Fox studios"). [03:00–06:45]
6. **Extreme Music tracks list alternate versions** (Full 2:22, 30 Seconds 0:30, Light, Less Cello, No Percussion). He grabs the 30-second cut via a free-preview download hack. [06:00]
   - A download manager (IDM) was used to capture the playing track.
   - Check that the detected length matches the track, name the file yourself, and save it in the project's `music` folder.
   - The hack is piracy-adjacent, so do not rely on it.
7. **Audition properly.** Don't listen only to the intro. Click into the waveform (e.g. 0:05, 0:20, 0:25, 0:28, 0:19, 0:47, 0:57) to hear where the beat and energy are. Go through "almost all" results. [06:00–15:00]
8. **Tune the query by feel.**
   - Drop "advertising" because it creates an "advertising feel" he wants to avoid. [06:45]
   - Reject tracks that feel wrong (one was "a bit of a disco style"). Even a good track isn't final if a better one may exist.
   - Drop genre words, keeping only mood. "upbeat rock pop positive" became "upbeat positive". The goal is to recreate the mood and experience in the viewer's mind, not a genre label. [06:45–09:00]
   - Drop "positive" because it pulls a "hopeful" mood. [09:00]
   - Get new keywords from ChatGPT rather than scrolling. He tried "upbeat energetic", then noted pop and funk styles fit. Past dog ads suggest "indie" as a fallback. [09:00–12:00]
9. **Final search: "indie rock upbeat" plus the Instrumental filter** (URL `vocalId=2`). [12:00]
   - This merges the reference's rock feel with an upbeat vibe, and the music won't compete with the VO.
   - Criteria: upbeat, playful, cheerful, fits "dog style" (friendship, home, comfort).
   - Decision rule: keep going until the track is a match "100% without doubt without any hesitation".
   - Results ran about 0:49–5:12, mostly 2:20–4:00, so picks must be cut or looped.
   - Real-time cost was about 8 minutes.
10. **Order of work:** VO and cuts first, then music, then video clips. [15:00, 24:00]
    - Isolate the music track and build a short intro section.
    - Land the main body/beat drop of the music at the start of the hold (the "Your dog's body…" line). Trim the music start and drop a marker at the hold. [15:00–16:00]
11. **Raw music buries the voice.** Fix it with a Simple Parametric EQ on the music track only. [15:00–21:00]
    - Center 1500 Hz, Q 4, Boost −15 dB.
    - Save it as a preset ("music eq", Type: Scale) and reuse it on every music clip.
    - Reason: speech sits in the mids, so the cut creates "an artificial space for the voice".
12. **EQ first, volume second.** Lowering volume alone makes the mix flat and the speech still unintelligible. A/B test with and without the EQ. [18:00–21:00]
13. **One EQ is sometimes not enough.** Also lower the clip Level after the EQ: about −5 dB, then −6.0 dB (final). Judge by ear that the voice stays clearly on top. [21:00–24:00]
    - VO clip levels were boosted: hook clip 13.0 dB, "hold dogs" 12.0 dB, "cta.mp3" 12.0 dB.
14. **Check the music against the whole script.** Edit it if its tone or mood changes partway, or slide a better moment into place. [21:00–24:00]
    - Here the track's natural ending fit the CTA VO ("…shop the sale before it ends at dogfriendlyco.com"), so the only edit was a fade-out. [24:00]
    - The sequence ends at about 00:00:40:11.

## Editing recipe

1. Read the brief (Milanote board, 3 hooks, AI VO female/generic script, CTA).
2. Check what the reference ad used (fast rock) and decide the vibe.
3. Screenshot the brief into the ChatGPT "Ecom Editing" project. Give context, need and "what do you need from me", then get search phrases.
4. Search Artlist (and Extreme Music). Start with "upbeat rock pop positive", then drop "advertising" and genre words, then "positive". Try "upbeat energetic", then settle on "indie rock upbeat" plus the Instrumental filter.
5. Audition by clicking into the waveform. Download the 100%-sure track into the project's `music` folder. (Final pick on screen is "Walking Around – Instrumental version", Eldar Kedem, Indie/Rock, 2:58, saved as an .mp3 in Downloads/Music. The timeline shows "Bunker Buster – Colorful and Animated", 2:46, so that is the track actually used.)
6. Cut and tighten the VO first (many short per-line clips on A1) and place the music on A2.
7. Trim the music start so its beat/drop lands at the start of the hold, with a marker there.
8. Add Simple Parametric EQ (1500 Hz / Q 4 / −15 dB) to the music and save it as the preset "music eq".
9. A/B the EQ on a few lines (e.g. "and that back clip harness is making it worse").
10. Lower the music Level to −6 dB, boost the VO clips (about 12–13 dB), and listen through the whole VO plus music.
11. Fix any mood shifts. Add a fade-out at the end if the ending fits the CTA.
12. Move on to finding and placing video clips.

## How an ffmpeg/Python agent applies this

1. **Music from the brief and reference vibe:** JUDGE. A model compares the brief and reference ad (tempo, genre, energy). Check that the music supports the script and matches what performed.
2. **ChatGPT-style search phrases:** AUTOMATE. Call an LLM with the brief text or image and the prompt pattern (context → need → what do you need). Ask for 8–10 phrases plus mood variants.
3. **Artlist/Extreme browsing and the IDM hack:** SKIP the stream-rip hack (piracy-adjacent, GUI-only). Use only licensed or API-accessible libraries, or user-supplied files. Apply the query-pruning logic (drop "advertising", genre words, "positive"; add Instrumental) when a library offers search.
4. **Audition by scrubbing:** AUTOMATE partly. Cut 10–15 s snippets from the middle and later parts of each candidate. Compute BPM and RMS energy with librosa/ffmpeg, and prefer instrumentals about 2–4 min long. JUDGE with a model or human for "100% fit, no hesitation".
5. **Align the drop to the hold:** AUTOMATE.
   - Detect onsets or the biggest energy rise with librosa (`onset_strength`, `beat_track`).
   - Take the VO hold start from silence detection or caption timings, then shift the music with `adelay` or `atrim`/`asetpts` so the drop lands there.
   - Then JUDGE by ear.
6. **Voice pocket EQ:** AUTOMATE with `equalizer=f=1500:width_type=q:width=4:g=-15` on the music stem only. Optionally use `sidechaincompress` for extra ducking.
7. **EQ first, volume second:** AUTOMATE with `volume=-6dB` after the EQ, then render a with/without comparison. Check VO vs music loudness (`ebur128`, or `astats`) so the voice sits clearly above the music (the instructor judged by ear). JUDGE with Whisper: transcribe the mixed audio and confirm the words come through. The instructor also boosted VO clips about +12–13 dB (`volume=12dB`); treat that as a starting point and loudness-normalize.
8. **Music clip length and mood changes:** AUTOMATE. Trim the track to the video length (~40 s) with `atrim`. Find the natural ending with a beat grid or energy envelope and add `afade=t=out`. JUDGE whether tone shifts need a re-cut.
9. **Presets:** AUTOMATE by storing the EQ and level values as a config. SKIP saving a Premiere preset (GUI-only).
